Ramparts are the chest towers on the sides of every bastion. Their chests are where most of your obsidian comes from, and they hold iron for a pickaxe or an axe when the overworld left you short.

## How it works

- Ramparts come in several layouts, named by their chests: triple, single, ruined, lantern (double) and diagonal. A triple has the most loot.
- Housing's right rampart is always a triple.
- In Ranked the ramparts together hold at least 3 iron and 5 obsidian. On Housing the iron can be in any chest; on Bridge it may be in the lodestone chest.
- Trades rarely give 20 obsidian on their own, so plan to loot ramparts while piglins trade.
- Opening a chest angers piglins that can see you. Break line of sight for about 2 seconds first, or loot from a spot they can't see.
- Breaking a chest angers every piglin nearby, which routes use on purpose to start a chase.
- Fast looting (shift plus a hotkey) empties a chest almost instantly.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set the rampart variants to random.
2. Loot each layout and learn where its chests sit, so you can reach them without being seen.
3. Fast-loot every triple you open until it needs no thought.
