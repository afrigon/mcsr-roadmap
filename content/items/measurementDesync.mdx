An eye of ender starts from where the server thinks you are, not from what F3 shows. If those two
disagree when you throw, the eye's line is wrong even though the bot reports full certainty.

## How it works

- Your client only tells the server your position after you have moved at least 0.03 blocks, or
  after up to 1 second. Small movements in that window go unreported.
- Throw inside that window and the eye launches from your old position while F3+C gives the new
  one.
- Any of these resyncs your position before a throw:
  - exit a boat and don't move;
  - jump in place once you are fully stopped (jumping with leftover momentum carries it);
  - stand completely still for more than 1 second;
  - a pearl landing, leaving a bed or respawning.
- Walking into a corner only stops you faster; you still need to jump or wait.

## How to practice

1. Make "stop, jump, throw" or "boat out, throw" a fixed habit before every measured eye.
2. When a 100% result misses, assume desync first and check whether you threw while moving.
