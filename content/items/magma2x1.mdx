The 2×1 is the standard ravine portal. It needs only two magma blocks side by side, which almost
every ravine has, and it is several seconds faster than the small L.

## How it works

- At the bottom of the two magma blocks, place two doors on one side, a third door, and blocks on the
  two remaining sides.
- Offhand the bucket. Start mining the magma, then hold right click while aiming just under the
  obsidian, so you scoop the lava from directly below as soon as the block breaks.
- Place a block where the magma was, then place the lava as obsidian.
- Repeat at the same angle for the next lava. Aim at the edge of the magma block: aiming at its top
  scoops water instead.
- Place the last lava against the block and step slightly forward, so you drop into the swimming
  position.
- Grab the last lava from the corner, then drain the water.

## How to practice

1. Learn it slowly, step by step, with the guide paused. The mine-and-scoop motion is the hard part.
2. Drill only the 2×1 in a portal practice map until you finish it without water in your bucket.
3. Use it on L-shaped ravines too: it works there as well.
