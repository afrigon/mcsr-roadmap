At higher levels the hotbar keeps one layout from spawn to the dragon. The End is where it pays off:
in the zero cycle every slot has to be where your hands expect it.

## How it works

- Plan slots that stay valid for the whole run, so nether and End items replace overworld items in
  place instead of reshuffling.
- Don't rebuild the hotbar for the zero cycle: only add or replace what you need. Items you won't
  use there, like the axe or spare eyes, can give up their slot for the fight.
- Sort the hotbar between pearls in the End, since you are waiting anyway.
- Glowstone or crying obsidian can share the obsidian slot, and the bed goes in the offhand for
  doubles.
- Arrange the hotbar before placing the last eye: filling the portal teleports you at once.

## How to practice

1. Practise zero cycles with your real End hotbar, not a convenient one.
2. Use a hotbar sorting practice map until sorting between pearls is reliable.
