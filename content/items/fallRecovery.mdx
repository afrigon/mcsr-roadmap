Falling off the tower mid-zero is common, especially while learning. If you survive the fall, you can
often get back up and still finish the zero instead of switching to a one cycle.

## How it works

- Survive the fall legally: water bucket or boat. The shift clutch is banned.
- Pearl back onto the tower, or loop round to a side setup if that is quicker from where you landed.
- On a 1/8 setup the fall is short enough to rebuild right where you land.

## How to practice

1. In the practice map, step off your setup on purpose, clutch, and get back into position.
2. Watch the two 1/8 fall recoveries below.
