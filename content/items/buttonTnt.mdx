TNT routes blow up gold walls instead of mining them. How you light the TNT decides whether the blast starts a fight with every piglin it touches.

## How it works

- TNT lit with flint and steel counts as damage from you. Any piglin or zombified piglin it hits is angered as if you punched it: it chases you, ignores gold, and won't trade.
- TNT lit by a button does not count as you, so the blast leaves them calm.
- A button costs one plank. Place it on the TNT (or on a block touching it) and press it.
- Routes that use this: TNT chalice on Bridge, TNT Treasure, and CrispyTNT on Stables, which uses two buttons.

## How to practice

1. Keep a plank or two in every bastion run so you can craft a button on the spot.
2. On the practice map, light the same TNT once with flint and once with a button, and watch how the piglins react.
