Half-down starts from the top stairs like top-down but skips the rampart chests, going straight to
the gold. It is the core that top-down builds on: everything after the stairs is the same, so
learning it first makes top-down a short step.

## How it works

- Come down the top stairs into the three-block-tall, two-block-wide spot and block it off behind
  you.
- Place two blocks, mine two, and take the first gold block.
- Piglins usually fall in. Mine the block below the one they stand on before the block they stand
  on; in the other order they can land and path over to you.
- Two more gold blocks sit behind the wall. Mine toward them, then place blocks so the drops are
  pushed over to you. Placing too fast can elevate them up instead; mine and retry.
- Craft ingots, toss a couple, drop down, block in above you, and dig down three and forward one for
  the trading hole.
- Mine the remaining gold from that spot and throw it in as you build back up. Toss ingots into the
  corner so gravel dropped on the piglins later doesn't hand the gold back to you.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, pick Housing and spawn at the top stairs.
2. Repeat the block-off and the two-block gold push until no drop elevates away.
3. Then add the chests at the top to turn it into top-down.
