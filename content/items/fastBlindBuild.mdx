Building the blind portal quickly, and at the right moment, turns its build and its 4-second
animation into time you would have spent waiting for blazes anyway.

## How it works

- Build the blind at the fortress at the end of a spawner cycle, so building, measuring and coming
  back overlap with the next blaze wave.
- Jump-stacking: jump and place obsidian under yourself to build the portal's sides in one motion
  instead of placing each block from the ground.
- Light the portal right after placing the last block, with flint and steel or a fire charge.
- Blazes knocked into a lit portal near the spawner get sent to the overworld; some runners break
  the portal when they come back for that reason.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, build ten-obsidian portals with jump-stacking until each takes only a few
   seconds.
2. In blaze practice, build the portal right after a wave and time how many blazes are waiting when
   you return.
