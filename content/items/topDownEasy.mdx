Top-down is the beginner housing route, for when you reach the bastion from its upper part. You loot
the ramparts first, then pull every piglin down into the gold room and trade there. It is slower than
manhunt but far more consistent, and a lava floor in the lower part does not change it.

## How it works

- Housing's right rampart is always a triple chest; the left is a triple, a single or ruined. The
  main gold is a line of 9 blocks buried in a tunnel behind the double-chest room.
- Loot the triple's double chest from the top, then the other rampart, and leave the single chest
  standing until last.
- Break the single chest and punch one piglin. Breaking a chest aggros piglins (makes them hostile)
  nearby, and the punch makes sure none stays half-aggroed and loses interest.
- Run down the stairs, break the blocks that let the other rampart's piglins follow, and block off
  the passage behind you.
- Mine the first gold blocks, throw a little gold down to the piglins, drop into the gold room and
  place a block above you. Dig down three blocks to make the trading hole.
- Mine the rest of the gold line and throw ingots into the hole. If you forget where the blocks are,
  keep digging straight along the tunnel.
- While they trade, dig down two blocks to reach the double chest in the lower room.
- When both ramparts are triples, open the block that lets piglins from the second triple drop down
  to you.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, pick Housing and spawn at the top.
2. Run the route with piglins off until the block placements are automatic.
3. Turn piglins on and count how many reach the hole; Calisthenicuber gets 13 in his demo.
4. Practice reaching the double chest while piglins stand on it.

When this is comfortable, the optimal version adds the three backup gold blocks.
