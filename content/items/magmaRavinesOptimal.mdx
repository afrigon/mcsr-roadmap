Kelp isn't always there. Strong players look at the sea floor directly: in third person, with the
camera tilted just under the water surface, you see the whole ocean bed without the underwater fog.

## How it works

- In the boat, switch to F5 and tilt the camera slightly below the boat. The camera clips under the water
  surface and the view clears.
- Seeing under water in third person is an allowed glitch in the Ranked rules.
- Keep hitboxes on (F3+B) to catch floating kelp and items at the same time.
- From the elevated view, judge whether a ravine is deep and wide and whether it has magma, before
  diving.
- Start with the area around the shipwreck or buried treasure; Ranked's filter places two magma
  ravines within 10 chunks.

## How to practice

1. Boat around ocean practice seeds in F5 under the surface and spot every ravine you pass.
2. Compare the view in first person to see how much you gain.
