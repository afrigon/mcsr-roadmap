Eating costs time you don't have in the End. With food in the offhand before the last eye goes in,
eating overlaps with filling the portal instead of costing its own pause afterwards.

## How it works

- Move the food to the offhand while you still stand in the portal room, before the last eye.
- Fill the last eye and eat in the same motion; the clip below shows the inputs.
- It only pays when you actually need food for the End; skip it with full hunger.

## How to practice

1. Watch the doogile clip at half speed and copy the order of inputs.
2. Repeat it in practice strongholds until the food goes to the offhand without looking.
