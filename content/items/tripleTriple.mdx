When all three ramparts are triples, you get three sets of four gold blocks and a crowd of
piglins. It's rare, and Calisthenicuber calls it probably the best stables route there is.

## How it works

- It extends double triple: after the first triple, run through to the next and repeat, letting in
  the piglins from the other side as well.
- Pre-mine the blocks as in double triple; the layout differs a little.
- Reek replaces the usual start, where you block yourself in and mine four blocks, with a faster
  sequence; his separate video shows doing the first part safely.
- Overlap time is short: Reek measured about 17 seconds on average from your last gold throw to
  fully traded. Looting two ramparts takes about 14. Keep the route linear and safe rather than
  chasing an earlier trade start.
- With so many chests, obsidian is rarely the problem.

## How to practice

1. Learn double triple first.
2. In the LlamaPag map, set three triples and run Reek's safe opening until it's consistent.
