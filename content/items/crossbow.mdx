A crossbow can be loaded before the End, so the +1 shot needs no draw time during the zero. It charges
slower than a bow, and the bow makes doubles easier, so the crossbow is a tool to get comfortable with
rather than a replacement.

## How it works

- Load the crossbow in advance, keep it in a fixed hotbar slot, and fire as the dragon approaches
  the node to start the 10-tick invulnerability timer.
- One shot per load: reloading mid-zero is slow.
- The practice map counts crossbow hits separately, not as an explosive or a +1.
- lvckyruns' y95 2+2 standing blocks: front clockwise 28 −27, back clockwise −30 25, front
  counter-clockwise 27 −28, back counter-clockwise −29 26.

## How to practice

1. Swap your bow for a loaded crossbow on front M-88 y95 and repeat the +1.
