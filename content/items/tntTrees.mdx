Desert temple and buried treasure seeds hand you TNT. One block of it between two trees gives a dozen
logs in a single explosion, much faster than chopping each trunk.

## How it works

- Pick two trees that stand close together. If none are close, chop normally rather than wander.
- Place the TNT between the trunks, raised a block or two off the ground, so the blast reaches higher
  logs too.
- Light it with flint and steel, or with the temple's pressure plate if you have no flint yet.
- Stand behind one of the trees. The trunk takes most of the blast: you lose about half a heart.
- Collect the logs and throw away the saplings and junk you pick up with them.
- A good explosion gives around 14 logs. 10 is fine.

## How to practice

1. On desert temple seeds, look for a close pair of trees while you run to the temple, so you already
   know where the TNT goes when you come out.
2. Try different heights for the TNT and count the logs you get.
3. Practice placing yourself behind a trunk before the fuse runs out.
