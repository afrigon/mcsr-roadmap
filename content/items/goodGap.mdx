Below the three ramparts, each side of the stables has a "gap" section: a good gap holds gold, a bad
gap holds one chest. With no triple rampart, the good gap is your gold source, so checking the gaps
and routing to the good one is the base of every stables route.

## How it works

- Ranked's filter guarantees at least one good gap. If one side is bad, the other is good.
- Checks along the center staircase (a single chest always sits at its bottom):
  - From the bottom chest, mine one block toward the left gap: blackstone means good, air or
    netherrack means bad.
  - On the way up, a cracked block toward the right gap means bad. Or mine the corner block: air
    behind it means good, blackstone means bad.
  - At the top of the stairs, mine toward the left gap: air means good, blackstone means bad.
- From above: a one-tall hole in the wall means a good gap below; a two-tall hole means bad.
- Basic route: run down the stairs, block the piglins off, make a pit with two placed blocks and one
  mined, then mine into the gold wall and let the piglins in. Collect the lower gold.
- Alone, a gap pulls about 7 piglins. Loot any reachable chest first for armor or distraction gold.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, set random gaps and call each one before you mine to check.
2. Run the basic route until the pit and the gold order are automatic.
