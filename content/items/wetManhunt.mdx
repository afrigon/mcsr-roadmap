Some housings generate with lava across the floor of the lower part. Manhunt still works there with
one trick: a dropped gold item keeps the piglins busy while you bridge over the lava. Without it, a
lava floor usually pushes you to top-down.

## How it works

- Start as in normal manhunt by breaking the center chest.
- Pillar up two blocks and throw one gold item on top of the pillar. Piglins go for the item
  instead of you.
- Bridge over the lava while they are distracted.
- Pick the item back up, break the block, and continue with the normal manhunt.
- Calisthenicuber pre-places blocks over the lava instead. Either way you get fewer piglins than on
  a dry floor; a hard toggle claws a few back.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, set up a housing with a lava floor in the lower part.
2. Practice the pillar and item throw until the bridge is safe every time.
3. Compare your piglin count with a dry manhunt to see what the lava costs you.
