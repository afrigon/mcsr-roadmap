A one cycle kills the dragon during a single perch with explosives, before it settles on the fountain
and the 50-damage flyaway rule applies. Five beds is the easy, consistent version.

## How it works

1. Place one obsidian beside the bedrock pillar on the fountain.
2. Wait around z70, behind the pillars, with hitboxes on (F3+B). Standing back lowers the chance
   of a strafe.
3. When the dragon snaps toward the fountain to land, run or pearl in.
4. Stand on the side the dragon's head swings toward, close to the pillar, and explode one bed each
   time the head passes over it.
5. Too early and the dragon is too high; too low and it breaks the bed. Too late and the head flips
   away, so the bed does less damage.

A Ranked one cycle takes about 1:46 on average (a frontcage sample of 20).

## How to practice

1. Use the one-cycle mode of the zero practice map, or an End practice map.
2. Do five-bed kills until the timing feels like a rhythm, one bed per head swing.
