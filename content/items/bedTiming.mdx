Explode five beds one at a time on the dragon as it descends onto the node, each just as the previous
hit's invulnerability ends. Front M-88 at y95 is the reference setup: forgiving, common, and the one
most guides teach first.

## How it works

- The dragon descends at a constant speed toward a point above the node. A bed explodes at its
  pillow, which sits on the node, so the timing depends only on the dragon's height.
- Time each bed from the dragon's head hitbox (F3+B), not its body.
- Each hit starts a 10-tick invulnerability window. A bed exploded inside it does nothing, so space
  the five singles out evenly.

## How to practice

1. Open the practice map and build front M-88 y95, standing on block 28 −27.
2. Do only five-bed singles until you hit them without thinking. Ignore the bow at this stage.
3. Watch the thecamo6 clip below at half speed and copy the rhythm.
