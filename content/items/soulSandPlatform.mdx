After clearing, build the edges of the spawn area out of soul sand. It keeps blazes on the platform and stops their fireballs from ruining your spawn space.

## How it works

- Blazes knocked or drifting onto soul sand are slowed, so they stay on the platform and are easier
  to hit.
- A blaze fireball landing on soul sand lights soul fire. Soul fire does not block blaze spawns, so
  you only have to put out fire elsewhere.
- Keep the platform small: no more than 2 blocks out from the cleared area.
- Collect soul sand in the bastion (a stack is a common target) or on the way.

## How to practice

1. In a blaze practice map, clear a spawner and build the soul sand edge.
2. Kill blazes there and watch where they land and where fire appears.
3. Time the build and try to place it while waiting for the next wave.
