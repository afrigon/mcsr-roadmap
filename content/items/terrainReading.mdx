The nether biome you land in decides how fast you can move and pearl. Reading it at a glance tells
you which way is cheap to travel and what will attack you.

## How it works

| Biome | Look | For the run |
| ----- | ---- | ----------- |
| Nether wastes | Plain red netherrack, wide open | Best: open sight lines and long pearls. |
| Crimson forest | Red trees, red ground | Wood for crafting, but nether wood can't make a boat. Hoglins: a threat and a food source. |
| Warped forest | Blue-green trees | Endermen spawn here. Twisting vines break falls. |
| Soul sand valley | Pale soul sand, bone shapes | Slow ground and skeletons that shoot you. Better with soul speed boots. |
| Basalt deltas | Grey, jagged basalt and blackstone | Worst: broken terrain and fall damage. Bastions never spawn in them. |

- When buried in netherrack with a known direction, tunnel with a boat crawl or pearl rather than
  mining a plain 2×1 tunnel.

## How to practice

1. On practice seeds, name every biome in view on entry before moving.
2. Plan routes around soul sand valleys and basalt deltas when an open path exists.
