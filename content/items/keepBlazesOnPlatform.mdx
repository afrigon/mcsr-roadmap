Every blaze you knock off the spawner platform falls into the fortress or the lava, and its rod goes
with it. Where you stand and how you hit decides which way they fly.

## Positioning

- Knockback pushes a blaze away from you. Stand between the blaze and the open edge, so hits push it
  back over the platform instead of off it.
- On a blaze that's near the edge, wait for it to drift back over the platform before you hit.
- Soul sand underfoot means less knockback for anything standing on it, which is one reason runners
  extend open platforms with soul sand (see Soul Sand Platform).

## Momentum

- A sprint hit adds extra knockback. Use it when the blaze is over the middle and will land on the
  platform anyway; switch to plain hits near an edge.
- A blaze that does fall can still be caught with a boat (see Blaze Kills).
