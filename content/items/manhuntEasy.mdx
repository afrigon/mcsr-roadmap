Manhunt is the housing route for when you arrive at the bottom of the bastion. You aggro the
piglins from the courtyard, lead them up into the gold room and trade from a hole there. It is much
faster than top-down and pulls more piglins, but it is less forgiving. This is the plain RSG
(random seed glitchless) version; the Ranked version adds a hard toggle and the upper backups.

## How it works

- Find the center chest in the courtyard and work out which way the ramparts are.
- Break the center chest, then punch a piglin closer to the ramparts than the chest. The punch
  turns any half-aggroed piglin into a full chaser.
- Bridge the gap toward the double-chest room. The MCSR wiki marks two of the bridge blocks as
  essential: without them you usually lose 2–3 piglins.
- Block the two side paths on the staircase so piglins follow you instead of wandering. Blocks are
  safest; lava or fire also work but can kill piglins. Calisthenicuber uses a block with fire on top.
- Loot the double chest, ideally put on gold armor, and open the block that lets piglins follow.
- Build into the gold room, mine the buried gold line and dig a trading hole the piglins fall into.
- The route totals 9 gold blocks plus 3 chests. If that is short on obsidian, loot the lower chests
  or go up to the triple.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, pick Housing and spawn at the center chest.
2. Learn the bridge and staircase blocks with piglins off.
3. Turn piglins on and count how many reach the hole each attempt.
4. If the lower floor is lava, play top-down instead until you learn wet manhunt.
