Ruined portal seeds are among the fastest overworlds, but the chest decides everything. Loot it
quickly, wear the gold, and know when to break the chest instead of looting it.

## How it works

- If you can't see the portal, scan for its chest with the pie chart. Ranked places it within 3
  chunks.
- If the chest holds a gold axe, take only the axe and break the chest with it. A broken chest spills
  everything, and walking over the items is faster than looting each slot.
- Otherwise fast-loot the chest as usual.
- Put on the gold armor, never a piece with Thorns. If you hit a piglin back while wearing Thorns, the
  others turn on you.
- Gold armor keeps piglins neutral in the nether.
- Take about 15 logs, using the gold axe while it lasts.

## How to practice

1. On ruined portal practice seeds, decide "break or loot" the moment you open the chest.
