A water bucket clutch (also called an MLG) places water under you just before you land, so a long
fall does no damage. It is the first clutch to learn and the easiest.

## How it works

- Look straight down while you fall. Looking forward may place the water on a block you are not
  falling onto.
- Don't start clicking right away. Wait for the block outline to appear on the ground under you, then
  right click.
- Pick the water back up after you land.
- Water doesn't work in the nether: it evaporates. Use a boat or pearl clutch there.

## How to practice

1. Jump off a tall pillar in a practice world with a water bucket and clutch every time.
2. Raise the height once it is reliable, and try it onto uneven ground.
