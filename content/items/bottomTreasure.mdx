The bottom of the treasure cube holds a lot of gold and the magma cube spawner. Going down is
situational: for extra gold when trades come up short, or to reach the spawner.

## How it works

- Go down when you still lack pearls, string or obsidian after trading all the tower gold, or when
  you need to break the spawner for pie-ray.
- If you plan to return to your trades, pearl hang first: throw a pearl up, lower render distance
  to 2, then pearl or boat down. Raise render distance to return.
- Piglins wait at the bottom, and mining gold can aggro the ones above you too.
- Distraction trick: find the lone block in the middle of the platform. Build toward it by two
  blocks and throw something golden on it. Piglins that drop down go for the item; while they are
  distracted, one hit knocks each into the lava.
- The bottom chest sometimes holds an enchanted diamond sword.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, set up the lone-block distraction and knock piglins into the lava.
2. Combine it with the pearl hang so you can leave the bottom on demand.
