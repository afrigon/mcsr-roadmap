The optimal top-down is the beginner route plus the three backup gold blocks, pulled to you with a
block elevator so they cost almost no extra time. More gold means more barters, and so more pearls
and obsidian from one visit. See the easy top-down page for the base route.

## How it works

- A block elevator: hold a block in your offhand, mine the gold and spam-place the block beneath
  you. The drop rides up with you instead of falling out of reach.
- The base route still gives the 9-block main line. The backups add three blocks on top of that.
- The MCSR Wiki version also uses a hard toggle: switch to Hard after placing the block above you in
  the gold room, so piglins drop from greater heights to reach you, and back to Easy before you
  climb out.
- Extra blocks also matter for later variants: the crossbow top-down works best when you get them,
  because they pull piglins from the lower part up the stairs.

## How to practice

1. Run easy top-down in LlamaPag's bastion practice map until it is consistent.
2. Practice the block elevator on its own, mining gold with a block in your offhand.
3. Add the backups to every run and compare your piglin count with the easy version.
