Sometimes Ninjabrain Bot can't decide between two candidate chunks. If those chunks are next to each
other, one second portal built between them covers both, so you don't need another eye.

## How it works

- Look at the bot's top results after measuring. If the top two chunks are adjacent and share most
  of the probability, the uncertainty is small in distance.
- Build the second portal at the nether coordinates between the two chunks.
- Coming out, throw an eye or dig at the more likely chunk's 4, 4 spot; the other is a short walk
  away.

## How to practice

1. When the bot splits between neighbouring chunks in practice, build between them and note how far
   you walked.
