If the crystal on your tower is destroyed, often by your own pearl, the dragon strafes instead of
descending to the node. The usual answer is to abandon the zero, but you can still try to kill it
while it fires at you.

## How it works

- Without the tower's crystal the dragon switches to a strafe: it flies at you and shoots fireballs.
- The fireballs leave purple dragon's breath clouds that block your view and deal damage.
- The standard advice is to bail to a one cycle, half bow, oneshot or ground zero.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, break the crystal on purpose and practise the fallback you would choose.
