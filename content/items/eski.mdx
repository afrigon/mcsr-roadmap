Eski is Lucento's route for a right-side double triple, built with Reek. It moves the trading hole
and adds micro-optimisations; in Lucento's save-state comparison it finishes trades about 2 seconds
earlier than the standard right-side route.

## How it works

- It frontloads setting up the hole with gravel and clearing the inventory while waiting for
  pearls, so a pearl hang is ready, yet still loots the chests earlier.
- Reek's micro: throw a gold ingot and block yourself off, then stand in the corner to pick it back
  up. Break the key block at once, throw your gold, then open the hole so trades start early.
- It's mechanically hard. It works on a left-side double triple with distraction gold, but is
  slower there.
- Lucento named it after the music genre Eski-beat, and says the name may change in a later
  version.

## How to practice

1. Learn standard double triple first.
2. In the LlamaPag map, set right and middle triples and copy the showcase step by step.
