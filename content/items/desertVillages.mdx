Desert villages have almost no trees, so wood becomes the problem. Plan for it before you reach the
blacksmith.

## How it works

- Type 1, trees near spawn: chop three logs before going to the village. That gives a crafting table
  and sticks.
- Type 2, no trees anywhere near: punch dead bushes on the way for sticks, and find the house with a
  crafting table.
- In Ranked, the sticks dead bushes drop are standardized between both players.
- Without trees, don't make shears. Make a shovel or a sword instead, and dig down for stone.
- With no wood at all, enter without logs and get them in the nether from a crimson forest, or later
  on the blind travel.
- The desert blacksmith is the only stone building, with cobblestone and iron bars.
- Knowing woodlights changes the route: you can skip the golem and save the flint-and-steel iron.

## How to practice

1. On desert village practice seeds, decide type 1 or type 2 at spawn.
2. Practice the dead-bush stick route until you have tools without a single log.
