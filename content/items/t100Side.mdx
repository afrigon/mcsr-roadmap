T-100 takes the same side setup as Tall Boy: the same standing blocks, at y90. If you know the Tall
Boy side, this is one more tower for free.

## How it works

- Stand on 29 −29 front or −30 28 back, at y90. kohout's overlay shows 29 −30 front; his pinned
  correction gives 29 −29.
- As on every side setup, bed order depends on the rotation.
- The same standing block also works on T-97 at y86.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set T-100 and alternate rotations at y90, front and back.
2. Watch the build clip first.
