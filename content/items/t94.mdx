T-94 is the shortest tall tower, and the normal standing blocks still work on it at y99, front and
back. On back, respawn anchors can only be used as the setup's obsidian.

## How it works

| Node | Standing | Heights |
| ---- | -------- | ------- |
| Front | 28 −27 | 99, 104 |
| Back | −30 25 | 99 |

- On back T-94, an anchor anywhere other than the obsidian position is in the wrong place. Compare
  the two anchor clips below.
- A y95 setup on 26 −28 front and −27 27 back exists, mainly for buried spawns.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set T-94 and alternate front and back at y99.
