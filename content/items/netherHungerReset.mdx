Healing costs food, and food is often scarce. A hunger reset sets your spawn, kills you on purpose,
and brings you back with full health, hunger and saturation. In the nether, a respawn anchor sets the
spawn, since beds explode there.

## How it works

- **The anchor**: craft it from crying obsidian and glowstone, place it and charge it with glowstone.
  Using it sets your spawn; each respawn uses one charge.
- **Keep your items**: die in a two-block hole, or boxed in by blocks, right beside the anchor, so
  your items stay together. You respawn in a fixed direction from the anchor, so dig the hole there.
- **Ways to die**, from the MCSR wiki:

| Method | Damage | Note |
| ------ | ------ | ---- |
| Lava | 8 per second | Needs at least 5 health. Remove it before you die so items survive. |
| Pearls into a corner | 5–6 per second | Each throw can spawn an endermite. |
| Fire | 1–2 per second | |
| Gravel suffocation | 2 per second | |
| Fall | n damage from an n+2 block drop | |

- **After respawning**: pick up the items, put armor back on and reapply fire resistance.

## How to practice

1. Practice the full sequence in a practice world: anchor, charge, hole, die, collect, re-equip.
2. Time it, and compare with how long eating the same food would take.
