With two triple ramparts, you can trade both triples' gold without going down to the gaps. It gets
many piglins trading very fast, but leaves you only 8 gold blocks, so it's a high-roll route.

## How it works

- **Adjacent triples**: come down between them and find the lowest point of the basalt hole. Mine
  three blocks to the left and one to the right.
- **Right and middle triples**: watch for a hole under the left side where piglins get stuck;
  pillar two blocks to give them a staircase.
- Mine into the first four gold and dig a two-deep trading hole. Dig down one more and place a
  block above you so no piglin can reach you. Throw the gold in, then fetch the other triple's
  four gold through the hallway you dug.
- Late piglins, especially sword piglins, can hit hard. Wait near the hole or throw a distraction.
- While they trade, loot the chests: 8 gold blocks may not cover 20 obsidian.
- **Split or divorced double triple**: the two triples are on opposite sides with the middle
  rampart between them. MCSR Wiki shows versions with a middle lantern and a middle single.
- Reek's guide fixes stuck piglins: where a three-wide area funnels into one, place a block so it
  becomes two wide.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, set two adjacent triples and run both sides.
2. Add Reek's pathing fixes one at a time and count piglins.
