The End island is full of endermen, and looking at one makes it attack. Learning where to point your
camera keeps a finished run from ending in a fight you never needed.

## How it works

- An enderman becomes hostile when you look at it. Keep your crosshair about 20° above or below the
  horizon while you move across the island, and it rarely lands on one.
- If one aggros anyway, trap it in a boat placed on a block next to you. A trapped enderman can't
  teleport or reach you.
- The danger lasts until you are in the exit portal: endermen keep wandering while the dragon dies.

## How to practice

1. In an End practice map, cross the island to the fountain with your crosshair held below the
   horizon, without ever looking straight ahead.
2. Aggro an enderman on purpose and trap it with a boat until it takes one try.
