The biggest spike isn't always the spawner. Reading the shape of the numbers tells a pure spawner apart
from mob noise and false positives, and tells you when to stop trusting the pie chart.

## How it works

- **Pure reading**: a spawner alone reads about 45–62% (orange plus green). A chest behind the spawner
  gives a "big orange" of about 54–60%.
- **Mob readings**: mobs lower the total. A `blockentities` to `unspecified` ratio of about 2–4 still
  points to the spawner.
- **False positives** (about 15% of strongholds): mineshafts, cave spider spawners, dungeons, a portal
  room chunk that isn't loaded, or a portal room out of range. If the reading doesn't fit, fall back
  to normal navigation.

## How to practice

1. In a preemptive practice map, write down the reading for every spike and whether it was the
   spawner.
2. Practice strongholds with mobs nearby and work out the ratio by hand until it is quick.
3. Study the examples in the Mikeyboie chapters below, especially the trial-and-error case.
