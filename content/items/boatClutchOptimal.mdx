Drag clicking makes the boat clutch close to certain. Many top runners use it for boats and blocks,
because one drag gives several right clicks in a fraction of a second.

## How it works

- Drag clicking is dragging a finger across the mouse button so it registers many clicks. It is done
  physically, not by a macro or rebind, which the rules forbid.
- FrizzClips' sequence with food in the offhand: switch to the boat, swap the food out of the offhand,
  switch to a tool such as an axe or pickaxe, drag click to place the boat and get in, break the boat,
  switch back to the boat slot, and put the food back in the offhand before you pick up the boat.
- Moving the food first stops the game from eating instead of placing the boat as you approach the
  ground.
- The 2×2 flat area rule still applies.
- **Legality**: the sticky boat clutch is an allowed glitch. The shift clutch is **not legal** under
  the speedrun rules; never use it in a run.

## How to practice

1. Learn to drag click on your mouse.
2. Drill the full sequence from your normal hotbar layout, with food in the offhand, off tall pillars.
