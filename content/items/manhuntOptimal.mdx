The Ranked manhunt adds two things to the plain route: a hard toggle when you break the center chest
and the three upper backup gold blocks. Calisthenicuber calls housing the only bastion that
consistently gives 20 piglins, and gets 23 in his demo of this version. See the RSG manhunt page for
the base route.

## How it works

- Break the center chest and switch to Hard right away, punch a piglin, then switch back to Easy.
  It only needs to be on for a moment: on Hard, piglins drop from greater heights to chase you.
- Continue as in the base route: block the staircase paths (Calisthenicuber places a block with fire
  on top so they path away), mine the block that would let lava reach you, and build up into the
  gold room.
- Go up for the three backup gold blocks. They are optional, but Calisthenicuber recommends them.
- The extra gold usually means more barters than the base route; if you still end up short on
  obsidian, pearl up to the triple from the back.

## How to practice

1. Run the RSG manhunt in LlamaPag's bastion practice map until it is consistent.
2. Add the hard toggle on its own and practice switching back without getting hit.
3. Add the upper backups and track your piglin count; aim for 20 or more.
