The stronghold's starter staircase always sits at block 4, 4 of its chunk. Digging down there lands
you on it, instead of somewhere random in the stronghold.

## How it works

- Ninjabrain Bot shows the stronghold chunk and the block coordinates of its 4, 4 spot.
- Go to those coordinates using the F3 "Block" line, then dig straight down.
- Anywhere from block 3, 3 to 5, 5 of the chunk usually hits the staircase.
- The "Eye Spy" advancement pops when you enter the stronghold, confirming you are in.
- If you followed eyes instead, dig where the eye flies into the ground.

## How to practice

1. In practice seeds, walk to the 4, 4 coordinates and dig in on every run.
2. Learn what the starter staircase looks like so you know at once whether you hit it.
