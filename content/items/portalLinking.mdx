If the stronghold is close to your blind, the second portal you build in the nether can send you
straight back out of the blind portal instead of to the stronghold.

## How it works

- When you arrive in the overworld, the game looks for an existing portal near the matching
  coordinates and uses it instead of making a new one. A close blind portal gets picked.
- Ninjabrain Bot warns when the stronghold is close enough for this to happen.
- When it warns, travel to the stronghold in the overworld instead of double travelling.
- The other option is to break the blind portal before going back to the nether, so there is
  nothing to link to.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, build a portal pair a short distance apart and see where you come out.
2. In practice runs, read the bot's warning every time before deciding to double travel.
