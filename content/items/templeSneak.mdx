The basement entry depends on mining the right block and falling straight through without touching
the pressure plate. Lining yourself up precisely makes it work the first time, every time.

## How it works

- Mine a block beside the centre of the temple floor, not the centre itself. The middle block of a
  side mines slightly faster.
- Stand on the block you are mining, so you fall the instant it breaks.
- Below, mine the block under the pressure plate without stepping on the plate.

## How to practice

1. Repeat the entry on desert temple practice seeds and watch where you land each time.
