The first seconds of a seed decide its route. Switch to third person (F5) as you spawn and look
around: from above you see much more than in first person, and you can plan while running.

## How it works

- Look for the structure, then everything around it: lava pools, water, trees, animals, gravel, and
  extra structures such as ruined portals that may hold a light.
- Make mental notes as you run: "lava to the left of the temple, trees behind me". Decide which way you
  exit before you even arrive.
- Fire in a forest or a patch of stone often means a lava pool.
- Keep looking around in F5 during dead time, such as while fighting the golem.
- Hitboxes (F3+B) help spot animals and the golem from far away.

## How to practice

1. On every practice seed, name out loud where you get wood, food, flint and lava before you move.
2. Check afterwards whether you missed something closer.
