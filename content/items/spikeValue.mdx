How big a chest spike looks on the pie chart depends on your computer and settings. Learn what one
buried treasure chest looks like on your machine, so you can tell it apart from noise, spawners or a
shipwreck.

## How it works

- Load a practice seed with a known buried treasure and look toward its chest. The size of that slice
  is your spike value.
- Your value only holds for the same settings. Changing render distance or entity distance changes
  how spikes look.
- A spawner spikes differently from a chest. Some runners memorise the values; others watch how fast
  the spike loads.
- Shipwrecks and ocean ruins have chests too, so they spike like treasure. In Ranked, other wrecks and
  treasures are kept away from spawn.

## How to practice

1. Load seed 4946542200205253057 with your usual settings and note your spike value.
2. Repeat after any settings or hardware change.
