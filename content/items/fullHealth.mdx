The End is where low health ends runs: dragon hits, fireballs, fall damage and endermen. Arrive with
full health and full food so you can take risks on the setup instead of playing around your hearts.

## How it works

- Eat to full before entering. Saturation keeps health regenerating through the fight.
- If you are low and have no food, a hunger reset (dying on purpose after setting your spawn with a
  bed) restores full health, hunger and saturation.
- Put food in your offhand before throwing the last eye so you skip the eating animation later.

## How to practice

1. In practice runs, check health and food before the portal room every time.
2. Practice the death reset in the portal room, so it is quick when you need it.
