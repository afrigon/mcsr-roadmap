Every zero setup is built the same way, so you only learn one build and change where you stand. Once
the build is automatic, your attention goes to the dragon instead of your hands.

## How it works

1. Staircase up twice.
2. Bridge out one block.
3. Place two blocks with an indestructible block on top.
4. Step back and place a second indestructible block.
5. The bed goes between the two indestructible blocks.

- Indestructible blocks are obsidian, crying obsidian or respawn anchors.
- Setups are named by the tower, front or back node, and your standing block and height, because
  the bed's position relative to you is fixed. "M-88 front y95 at 28 −27" means: stand at y95 on
  block 28 −27.
- Coordinates are block coordinates, X then Z: the integer `Block:` line in F3, not the decimal
  XYZ line.

## How to practice

1. In the practice map, build the setup on one tower until you no longer look at your hotbar.
2. Check your standing block in F3 after every build.
