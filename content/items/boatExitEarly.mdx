At a few exact fall heights (12, 13, 49, 51, 111 blocks and some higher ones), a boat breaks on
landing and its rider takes the full fall damage, which can kill. You rarely know the exact height
of a nether drop, so many runners get out of the boat right before it hits the ground.

## The move

- Ride the boat down. Just before it lands, sneak to get out.
- Get out low: once you leave the boat it no longer protects you, and you take fall damage for the
  distance you fall after that, from about three blocks up.

## When it matters

Long drops of unknown height, especially in the nether. On short drops well below 12 blocks, staying
in is safe.
