While piglins barter you mostly wait. Running the pie-ray in that time means you leave the bastion already knowing which way the fortress is.

## How it works

- Switch the pie chart to `tick`, `level`, `entities`, `blockEntities` during the nether portal
  animation, so the spawner check is ready the moment you arrive.
- Find N and set N−1 between gold throws. Mikeyboie runs his two-crossing scan while trading.
- Crossing chunk borders needs a few blocks of movement. Use borders near your trading spot so
  piglins stay in your hole.
- A treasure bastion has its own magma cube spawner, which shows up in the same chart slice.
- The fortress is never in the bastion's own structure region (see Structure Regions), which
  rules out directions before you scan.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag bastion map, turn on chunk borders and note where borders cross your usual
   trading holes.
2. Run your trade route and do the scan during the barter wait, without losing piglins.
3. Check how much time the scan costs; if trades finish first, start the scan earlier.
