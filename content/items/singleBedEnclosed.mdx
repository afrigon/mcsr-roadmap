On a buried spawner (one inside a netherrack or nether brick roof) you can still blaze bed with a single bed by using the roof above the spawner as the bed's space.

## How it works

- Place a hard block on top of the spawner to shield it.
- Mine up into the roof above it to make space for the bed.
- Place the bed in that space and detonate it from a step away.
- The roof keeps more blocks on the floor than on an open spawner, and there is a lot of fire.
  Put it all out, then mine the leftovers.

## How to practice

1. In Mescht's Blaze Practice map, pick a buried spawner and give yourself one bed.
2. Copy the setup from the clips below.
3. Time the setup plus fire cleanup, and compare with 2 TNT on the same spawner.
