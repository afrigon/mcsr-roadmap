An ender pearl's flight is dead time. Eating during it heals and refills hunger without spending a
separate stop on the eating animation.

## How it works

- Throw the pearl, then switch to food (or keep it in the offhand) and hold right-click until you
  land.
- It works the same in the End while waiting on pearls before the dragon, and through the nether
  portal animation.
- With food in the offhand, you can also eat between swings at blazes.

## How to practice

1. On every long pearl in practice, eat during the flight, even when you don't need the food.
2. Keep food in the same slot or the offhand so you don't look for it.
