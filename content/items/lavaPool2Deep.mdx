Casting a portal from a surface lava pool with one water bucket is the first portal every runner
learns. Village and desert temple seeds in Ranked always have lava pools nearby, so this portal
carries most early runs.

## How it works

- Find a side of the pool with four lava blocks in a row. One lava on one side of your spot and two on
  the other also works.
- Place a block in the middle, pour water next to it and mine the block back out. The water turns the
  lava into the obsidian bottom of the portal.
- Pick up the water. Build a guide frame from any blocks: three up on one side and one out, two
  across on the other side.
- Pour the water at the top. It flows down over the shape of the portal.
- Scoop lava from the pool and place it into the water, one block at a time, to cast the sides and top.
- Mine away any stone left in the way, take back the water and light the frame.

## How to practice

1. Load a lava-pool setup in a portal practice map and build the same side every time until it is
   automatic.
2. Keep the crosshair still while you scoop or place lava. Moving it in the same tick causes a "ghost
   bucket", where the game shows lava you don't have.
3. Time yourself, then learn the draining trick from the next item.
