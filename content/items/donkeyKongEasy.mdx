Donkey Kong (DK), invented by doogile, is the bridge route for a left-side triple. HiTrippy picks it
as the one bridge route to learn first. The optimal version adds an auto funnel; this page covers
the beginner one.

## How it works

- Mine or TNT the chalice. Craft gold armor, turn the rest into ingots, but keep one gold block.
- Go to the far end of the bridge, toward the left rampart. Avoid hoglins or use the fire
  knockback trick.
- Build the staircase and the blocks that connect the two ramparts, so piglins from both reach you.
  Mine four blocks to make a staircase up from the right rampart.
- Mine your gold block to aggro every nearby piglin, run down your path and dig into the triple.
- Find the hole in the triple's floor, dig down one, and throw gold in slowly.
- Punch one sword piglin as they fall in, or break a gold block at the end, so no straggler sneaks
  up on you. Let up any piglins stuck below with gold.
- Loot the chests, check the other rampart's single, collect trades and leave.
- If the triple turns out to be a single, dig a two-deep hole and trade there.
- On leaves-only blocks, add the extra leaf so lava doesn't burn the build. For hoglins, pillar up
  two and out two before starting the staircase.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, pick Bridge, spawn at the chalice, set the left rampart to a
   triple.
2. Learn the staircase in creative, then with hoglins on.
3. Count piglins per run before moving to the optimal version.
