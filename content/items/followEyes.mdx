An eye of ender flies toward the nearest stronghold. Throwing eyes and walking where they go is the
slowest way to find it, but it needs no tools and teaches what an eye does.

## How it works

- Throw an eye (right click). It flies a short distance toward the stronghold, hangs, then drops.
- Once you are almost on top of the stronghold, the eye flies down into the ground instead of
  forward. Dig down there.
- In vanilla, a thrown eye sometimes shatters. In Ranked, eye breaks are standardized and the
  second throw never breaks.
- Twelve eyes fill a portal, and some frames come pre-filled, so every eye you throw away for
  direction is one fewer spare. Keep throws to a minimum.

## How to practice

1. Note the direction of each throw on the F3 screen (the "Facing" line) and walk that way.
2. Throw again only after travelling a long way, and watch for the moment the eye turns downward.
3. Once this feels natural, move on to Ninjabrain Bot, which replaces following eyes in real runs.
