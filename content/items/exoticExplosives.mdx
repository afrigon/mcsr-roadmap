More than two explosives on a single rotation, such as a 5+1 on universal back. These kills are rare
and situational: 2+2 remains the practical top target.

## How it works

- Each explosive after the first on a rotation has to skip a damage cooldown, so the timing is
  tighter than a normal double.
- kohout's catalogue includes examples.

## How to practice

1. Only after 2+2 is consistent: pick one exotic setup from the video and drill it on the practice
   map.
