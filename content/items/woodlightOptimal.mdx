Interior woodlights put the wood inside the frame, with lava right beside it. The fire forms inside the
portal on its first spreads, so it lights sooner and more reliably than a basic woodlight.

## How it works

- While building, place two extra blocks at the front where the water normally goes, then remove the
  water.
- Place lava on both sides, wood on top and below, and wood in the middle: a "sandwich".
- Switch to Hard while waiting, and back as soon as it lights.
- Kill animals or clear your inventory during the wait. Without flint and steel, kill animals with the
  lava bucket.
- Knowing woodlights lets you spend all your iron, or skip the golem on a village seed. Whether that is
  worth it is a playstyle choice; the wait is still random.

## How to practice

1. Copy the layout from the clips below block by block, then time how long it takes to light.
2. Compare it with the basic bowl so you know what you gain.
