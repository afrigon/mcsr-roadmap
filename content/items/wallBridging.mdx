When a wall runs along your path, you can bridge against its side instead of backward from the edge.
You keep running forward, placing blocks into the corner between the wall and the gap, so the
bridge grows under you at running speed.

## The idea

- Run beside the wall and look slightly down toward the wall's side at foot level. Each block goes
  against the wall face, one step ahead of you.
- Because every block has the wall to attach to, you never need to look back or crouch at an edge.
- It works where a wall lines a gap: a lava lake shore, a fortress corridor, the edge of a bastion
  bridge.

## Getting it consistent

Start on a flat wall over a one-block drop, so a miss costs nothing, and only move it over lava once
you stop missing placements at a sprint.
