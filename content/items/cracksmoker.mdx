Cracksmoker is the beginner bridge route for every case except a lone left triple: right triple
only, both triples, or two single ramparts. Its advanced version is soog. It is also the fallback
when you are short on blocks.

## How it works

- Bridge's gold is in the chalice in the middle, which holds all you need. Mine it first, or blow it
  up with TNT later on.
- Head to the far-right lava waterfall. The first two blocks you place stop hoglins (they are 1.4
  blocks wide and can't climb your gaps); the rest set up piglin pathing to the ramparts.
- Break the marked block, break a gold block to aggro the piglins, throw gold, bridge across with
  four blocks and dig the trading hole.
- Throw ingots in slowly, from the side away from where piglins arrive. Punch a sword piglin, or
  break a gold block once all piglins are in, to re-aggro stragglers.
- Open the blocks below so the lower piglins come up. Keep enough gold to keep them funnelling in;
  run out and you will have to kill them.
- To reach the left rampart and its lodestone chest, make the four-block jump: walk straight off the
  edge holding jump. In Ranked, the lodestone chest may hold the rampart iron.
- With two single ramparts, the end of the route changes; see Single Single.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, pick Bridge, spawn at the chalice, set the right rampart to a
   triple.
2. Learn the hoglin-proof blocks first, with hoglins on.
3. Practice the four-block jump both ways until it never fails.
