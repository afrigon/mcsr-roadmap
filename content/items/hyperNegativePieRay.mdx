A refined negative pie-ray, posted by ClearColdWater in December 2025 (a reply credits an earlier version to ExeRSolver). It starts from a very low render distance and pins the spawner to one chunk in a fixed sequence of moves, with no confusing far-spawner cases.

## How it works

1. Lower render distance to 3 (2 also works).
2. Raise it until the spawner loads, without overshooting.
3. Lower it by 1.
4. Move forward one chunk. If the spawner unloads, it is in the first candidate chunk.
5. Move right one chunk. If it unloads, it is in the second.
6. Lower render distance by 1.
7. Move left one chunk. If it unloads, it is in the third.
8. Move back one chunk. If it unloads, it is in the fourth; otherwise it is diagonal.

The post's diagram colours each candidate chunk; learn the steps with that picture next to you.
It also gives an alternative after step 5: move left instead, lower render distance by 3, and
check whether the spawner unloads.

## How to practice

1. Download the simulator map (v2.1) from the post. Its lever spawns two spawners within 16 chunks.
2. Run the eight steps every time, then walk to the spawner to check.
3. Once the order is automatic, use it on every scan in practice runs.
