Chest-scan settings are bad for everything after the chest: at render distance 8 and 50% entity
distance you can't see the next structure, the island or the bastion. Switch back as soon as the
chest is found, and on seed types with no chest to scan for, switch at spawn.

## How it works

- StandardSettings has no hotkey to restore settings mid-run, and no per-seed-type profiles. The
  switch is done by hand, so make it short and always the same.
- **Render distance**: F3+F raises it one step at a time without opening a menu. Press it while you
  walk away from the chest.
- **Entity distance and entity culling** have no key. Set them in Options, then Video Settings,
  during a moment you would be standing still anyway, such as crafting.
- The values to switch to are the ones on Pre-Nether Settings: high render distance, 500% entity
  distance, entity culling off.
- Decide per seed type at spawn: a chest-first route (buried treasure, shipwreck, village loot) keeps
  the start settings until the chest; a route with nothing to scan for switches straight away.

## How to practice

1. On practice worlds, time the switch from chest settings to travel settings and keep the order
   of keys and menus the same every run.
2. Load a seed of each type and decide at spawn whether to switch now or after the chest.
