Ground zero kills the dragon from a setup at the fountain, using its strafe pattern instead of a
tower. It works on any End, so it is an option when no tower suits a zero, at the cost of about 15
seconds.

## How it works

- Found by Starchomper in 2023-08; the iron-bar version is from luvvlyjude.
- It needs 7 to 8 or more explosives, plus iron bars and obsidian.
- The setup starts with a block and four obsidian on top of the fountain.
- The first explanation video shows an outdated setup; use it for the idea and the iron-bar video for
  the build.

## How to practice

1. Build the iron-bar setup on a practice End until you can place it from memory.
2. Repeat full kills and count how many explosives you need.
3. On each seed, compare it with a zero and a oneshot before committing.
