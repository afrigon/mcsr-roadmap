Ruined portal chests and gold blocks can hold items that change your whole run. Recognise them and
decide in a second whether they are worth the time.

## How it works

- Enchanted golden apple: 5 minutes of Fire Resistance. That makes going to the fortress first,
  before the bastion, a real option.
- Golden carrots: one of the best foods in the game. Take them.
- Gold blocks: only with an iron pickaxe, and only when they are easy to reach. They become gold
  pickaxes in the nether, gold armor, or gold to throw at piglins as a distraction.
- Gold tools: a gold axe for a fast wood grab, a gold pickaxe for netherrack and blackstone. They are
  fast but break quickly.
- Never wear armor with Thorns.

## How to practice

1. On ruined portal practice seeds, list what in the chest changes your plan, before you loot it.
