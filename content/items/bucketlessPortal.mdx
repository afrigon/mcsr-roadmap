Building a nether portal without a bucket is a random-seed (RSG) skill for seeds where you find no
iron. In Ranked it never comes up, so learn it only out of curiosity.

## How it works

- A normal portal needs a bucket to carry water or lava, and the bucket needs 3 iron.
- Every Ranked seed type guarantees enough iron for the bucket: villages, shipwrecks, desert temples
  and buried treasure give at least 7 iron (or 4 iron and 3 diamonds), and ruined portals give enough
  nuggets beyond what the bucket needs.
- If you are short on iron in Ranked, the iron to save is the flint and steel's, not the bucket's:
  see the woodlight portal.
