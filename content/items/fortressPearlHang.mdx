The last few trades are dead time. A pearl hang lets you throw your pearl toward the fortress during that wait and release it the moment you are done, so the pearl's flight overlaps the trades.

## How it works

- Throw a pearl, then lower render distance (pausing makes it instant). Once the pearl is in unloaded chunks it freezes in the air.
- Finish the bastion, then raise render distance: the pearl resumes and lands.
- Lowering render distance also unloads your piglins. Use render distance 4 so the ones in your hole keep trading; doogile lost his piglins to a hang when he crossed into the other rampart.
- A portal keeps a 3×3 chunk area loaded for 15 seconds after use. Near a portal, throw the pearl horizontally away so it leaves the loaded area.
- A typical finish: leave spare gold in a corner, wait on the hole for the last trades, grab everything, release.
- Render distance changes are normal settings and legal in Ranked.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, throw a pearl at the end of a route and drop render distance to 4.
2. Check that the piglins in the hole keep trading, then raise render distance and follow the pearl.
