Instead of pushing withers off, you can trap them for good in an enclosed hallway. Once they are stuck they never bother you again.

## How it works

- In a buried part of the fortress, build a short pathway: one block in front of you, then a line,
  then cap it so they cannot jump over.
- The withers follow you in and gather at the end.
- Block the other end at any height. They are stuck forever.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, switch to survival in a fortress hallway with several withers.
2. Build the pathway, lead them in, and seal it.
3. Repeat until the whole trap takes only a few placements.
