The BeefSalad hole, named after the runner BeefSalad, is an auto-funnel for the stables good gap:
piglins path into the trading hole by themselves while you mine the gap gold. The Crispy hole
replaced it as the default, but it remains a good fallback.

## How it works

- In the classic gap finish you block yourself in and mine blocks to let piglins reach the hole.
  In the BeefSalad version you break one block and place a few, and the piglins funnel in on their
  own, so your mining and their trading overlap.
- Block off the two spots shown in the guides, or the piglins path badly.
- Break the block, walk backwards to clip through the floor and place the block before any piglin
  escapes the hole. Hold D while pressing W to bounce out without jumping.
- Watch for late piglins coming up from below. They are rare but happen.
- It combines with TNT on the gap gold.
- The Crispy hole is about 2.3 s faster because piglins reach the hole sooner.

## How to practice

1. Learn the classic gap blocks first.
2. In the LlamaPag bastion map, repeat the BeefSalad block placements until the clip is reliable.
3. Then learn the Crispy hole and keep BeefSalad as a backup.
