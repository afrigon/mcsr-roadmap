The zero starts with a pearl onto the obsidian tower under the node. Hitting the tower's end crystal
with that pearl almost always makes the dragon fly away, so the throw has to land on top cleanly.

## How it works

- Use a standstill jump pearl: stand still, jump, and throw at the top of the jump.
- Aim so the pearl lands on the tower without touching the crystal.
- Read the node and rotation during the flight.

## How to practice

1. In the practice map, pearl onto each tower height from the spawn platform.
2. Repeat on the tall towers, where the crystal sits highest and is easiest to hit.
