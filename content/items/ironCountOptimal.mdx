Extra iron beyond seven buys better tools, but only some are worth it. Knowing the answer for each
count lets you craft without hesitating at the table.

## How it works

| Iron | Craft                                                           |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 6    | Bucket, shears, flint and steel, when diamonds cover the pickaxe |
| 7    | Pickaxe, bucket, flint and steel                                |
| 8    | Add an iron shovel                                              |
| 9    | Add shears instead of the shovel                                |
| 10–12 | Shears and shovel                                              |
| 13+  | Add an iron axe                                                 |

- **Diamonds**: three make a pickaxe and free three iron for other tools; two make a sword, useful
  for blazes; one makes a shovel.
- **Shears** clear leaves fast while getting wood and shear sheep for bed wool. Skip them when you
  have TNT, which can blow up trees instead.
- With exactly six iron, hotkey the stack into the table, split it with a right-click and place it
  so one craft gives the bucket and shears together.
- Convert nuggets into ingots before deciding.

## How to practice

1. Use a practice map that regenerates structure loot and decide the full craft list before
   opening the table.
2. Practise the six-iron split until it takes one motion.
