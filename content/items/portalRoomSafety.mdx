The portal room has two run killers right at the finish: a silverfish spawner and a lava pool under
the portal. Deal with both before filling any eyes.

## How it works

- **Break the spawner** as soon as you enter. It stops new silverfish from spawning while you fill the
  frames.
- **Block the lava**: place a block in the lava pool under the portal, so a misstep or a knockback
  doesn't drop you into it.
- Only then fill the frames.

## How to practice

1. Make it one fixed sequence in every practice run: enter, break the spawner, block the lava, fill.
2. Keep a block in your hotbar for the lava so you never have to search for one.
