Villages are the one seed type that hands you plenty of food and beds. Hay bales become bread, and
beds come from the houses.

## How it works

- Plains, desert and savanna villages have hay bales. One bale crafts into 9 wheat, which makes 3
  bread.
- Take about 7 bales. Beginners can take more.
- Beds are in the small houses. Grab two on your way through.
- Beds explode in the nether and the End, which makes them a weapon against blazes and the dragon.
  Placed underwater, a bed also gives an air pocket.
- Snowy and taiga villages have no hay, so food comes from chests or animals there.

## How to practice

1. On village practice seeds, route past hay and small houses on your way to the exit.
