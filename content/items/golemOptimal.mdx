The tower kill is safe but slow. With the right weapon and timing, the golem dies in a few jumps.

## How it works

- With an axe, crit and then wait for the attack cooldown to refill before the next crit.
- If the blacksmith chest holds an iron sword, use it. The sword has a shorter cooldown and more damage
  per second, so you can crit on every jump.
- Count your iron before the fight: you may not need the golem at all.

## How to practice

1. Practice jump-crits on a golem in a test world with both an axe and an iron sword.
2. Learn the cooldown bar so you never swing early.
