Boomer is Safe Boomer with aggressive gold placement and throws. It pulls more piglins into the
hole sooner, but you are very unlikely to finish it without a hit, and it saves only a few seconds.

## How it works

- Mine the first bridge gold, then jump and place a block mid-air. Craft one gold block into
  ingots and put the other in your offhand.
- Jump across the pillars instead of bridging. Break the same key block as in Safe Boomer.
- At the second block: mine it, place your offhand gold block, and mine it again. Breaking gold
  aggros every piglin in range at once.
- Swap to ingots fast and throw before piglins reach you. Throw plenty while mining through the
  wall; piglins will push you.
- Build the bridge between the towers, then finish like Safe Boomer.
- HiTrippy advises against learning it for a 10 to 20 minute personal best.

## How to practice

1. Master Safe Boomer first.
2. Drill only the gold place-and-mine and the throws in the LlamaPag map until the swap is
   automatic.
