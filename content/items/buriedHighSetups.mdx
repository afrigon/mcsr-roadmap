Deep buried spawns used to mean a safe low setup. At the top level you still take high setups from
O59 and deeper, when your clip and build are fast enough for the remaining lenience.

## How it works

- Each O level costs about 1.1 s. At O58 a top runner has about 4.75 s of spare time on front
  M-88 y95, and none on front M-88 y99 or back T-97 y99.
- Setups that keep the most spare time on deep spawns are the side setups (Tall Boy y94 side: about
  6 s front and 5 s back at O58) and back M-91 y95 offset (about 3.4 s).
- Use the lenience sheet to see which high setups are still possible at your depth.

## How to practice

1. Set O59 or deeper spawns on the practice map and time your clip and build against the sheet.
