Hourglass is a triple to gap route for when the gap under your left triple turns out to be bad:
you take the piglins through the bad gap to the good gap on the right.

## How it works

- Start as left triple to left good gap. Dig down and find the bad gap.
- Throw gold first, because piglins below are already aggroed. Mine through, drop down, and place a
  block on your head as the first checkpoint.
- Place two blocks and continue down. You can block the piglins off here and loot the bad-gap
  chest, which sometimes holds the obsidian you need.
- Run through to the right side. Block off openings where piglins like to jump down, then mine
  into the good gap and play it as usual.
- Ranked guarantees a good gap, so with a bad left gap the right one is good.
- meebie's own version only works on exposed bastions. The chloe and pavkin route linked below
  also works on buried ones.
- HiTrippy finds it pulls fewer piglins; use it as the fallback when you read the gap wrong.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, set a left triple and a bad left gap and run it to the right good gap.
