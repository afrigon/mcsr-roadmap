Every Ranked match follows the same route through the game. Knowing what each part is for tells you what to gather and when to move on.

## How it works

1. **Overworld**: gather iron (at least enough for a pickaxe, bucket, and flint and steel), wood, food and flint, then build a nether portal from lava or a magma ravine.
2. **Nether**: find a bastion and a fortress. Ranked places both close and reachable.
3. **Bastion**: trade gold with piglins for ender pearls, obsidian, fire resistance and string.
4. **Fortress**: kill blazes at the spawner for blaze rods. Rods plus pearls make eyes of ender.
5. **Blind**: build a portal from the nether back to the overworld toward the first stronghold ring, before throwing any eye.
6. **Stronghold**: throw and measure eyes, let Ninjabrain Bot calculate where the stronghold is, travel there and find the portal room.
7. **End**: fill the portal, jump in and kill the dragon, usually with exploding beds.

- Ranked's match timeline marks these splits: nether entry, bastion, fortress, first eye, End and completion. Comparing your splits with the winners' medians at your rank shows where you lose time.
