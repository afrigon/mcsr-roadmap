When you fall through a hole or off a fortress, throwing a pearl straight down teleports you to the
ground before you hit it. You take the pearl's 2.5 hearts instead of the full fall damage.

## How it works

- Swap to your pearl as fast as you can and throw it downward. Any throw pointing below horizontal
  lands before you do.
- Calisthenicuber looks down at a pitch of about 70° to 82° for this.
- It also works in the End when the dragon knocks you off a pillar; you take the 2.5 hearts plus any
  magma block you land on.
- Keep pearls on the same hotbar key every run. Mid-fall you have no time to look for them.

## How to practice

1. Jump off tall pillars in a practice world and pearl clutch before landing.
2. Start from a different hotbar slot each time, so you practice the swap as well as the throw.
