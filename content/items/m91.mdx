M-91 is the tallest of the middle towers. Front works at y95 and y99; back only at y99 from the normal
standing block, because a back bed at y95 breaks the crystal.

## How it works

| Node | Standing | Heights |
| ---- | -------- | ------- |
| Front | 28 −27 | 95, 99 |
| Back | −30 25 | 99 |

- Front y95 works only because 28 −27 is already one block out from the tower.
- For back y95, use the offset setup instead (next item).

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, alternate front y95 and y99 on M-91, then add back y99.
