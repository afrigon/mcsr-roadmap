description: Critical hits, weapon cooldown, and when to use an axe or a sword.
---

- Critical hits: hit while falling after a jump.
- Weapon cooldown: wait for the attack indicator to recharge; spam-clicking does little damage.
- Axe or sword: an axe hits harder but recharges slower; a sword is quicker between hits.
