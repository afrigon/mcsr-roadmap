Pigs and cows drop the same food per kill, roughly double a sheep and triple a chicken. What separates them is the chase. A hit cow panics and runs faster than you can sprint, while a fleeing pig is slower than you walk, so you can finish it before it gets away.

In a run you take what the seed gives you, so this order only matters when you have several animals to choose from. Otherwise, kill whatever is closest: going out of your way for a better animal costs more time than the food difference is worth.

> [!NOTE]
> While looting improves the effective food per kill, it does not affect the priority order.
