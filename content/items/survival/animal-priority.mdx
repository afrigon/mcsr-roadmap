per kill to every animal on average, so it never changes the order.

How hard each animal is to finish:

| Animal | Health | Flee speed | Hitbox (width × height) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pig | 10 | 0.31 | 0.9 × 0.9 |
| Cow | 10 | 0.40 | 0.9 × 1.4 |
| Sheep | 8 | 0.29 | 0.9 × 1.3 |
| Chicken | 4 | 0.35 | 0.4 × 0.7 |

Pigs and cows drop the same food, but a panicking cow runs fastest of the four, so pigs come first.
Looting only counts when your Looting weapon lands the killing blow, not the fire.
