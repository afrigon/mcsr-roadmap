Respawn anchors stretch a low bed count: one or two placed and charged before the perch save an
action when the dragon comes down. On a one cycle they are exploded first, before the beds.

## How it works

- Put one or more anchors on top of your obsidian and charge them with glowstone.
- Each anchor needs an explosion-proof block under it; the blast alone can kill you otherwise.
- Pre-placed anchors go off before any bed.
- A 3+1 with one pre-placed anchor is a standard way to reach the four explosives a one cycle needs.
- Anchors are harder to time than beds (k4yfour).

## How to practice

1. In an End practice map, pre-place one anchor and finish with three beds.
2. Then try two anchors.
