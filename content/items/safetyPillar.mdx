The run isn't over when the dragon dies: its death animation lasts about 10 s before the exit portal
opens, and endermen keep wandering the island. Get out of their reach while you wait.

## How it works

- After the kill, pillar up 2 blocks beside the fountain, or boat down if you are high up.
- Keep looking away from endermen until you jump into the portal.
- After a oneshot kill, avoid fall damage for 3 s. If you get launched, land with a boat or water
  bucket, not a pearl.

## How to practice

1. End every practice kill with the pillar, so it becomes part of the kill.
