Bridge routes depend on which rampart is the triple, and you often can't see both from where you
arrive. Two checks tell you which route to play before you commit.

## How it works

- **By eye**: a triple rampart is much taller, with upward columns and a boxy, square roof. A single
  is short and flattened.
- **E-ray**: from the chalice, E-ray toward each rampart. The side with more entities is likely the
  triple; in Calisthenicuber's example the left showed 11 and the right about 20.
- **F5 check**: start as for Donkey Kong and look up at the left rampart in third person (F5). If you
  see netherrack or air there, it is a single: break your gold block, turn around and play the
  right-rampart route.
- If you can't tell, play as for the right side: soog and Single Single start the same, so you lose
  nothing either way.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, set both ramparts to random.
2. Decide the route before you can see the ramparts, then check whether you were right.
