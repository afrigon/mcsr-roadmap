Piglins in the trading hole drop their trades at their feet, where you can't reach them without stepping in. Dropping gravel or soul sand on them lifts the items to where you can grab them from the edge.

## How it works

- Place gravel or soul sand above the piglins in the hole. The falling block lands on them and pushes their trade items up by one block.
- You can then pick up the trades from the corner of the hole without walking in among angry piglins or picking your thrown ingots back up.
- Gravel itself comes from trades, so you usually have some by the time the first wave finishes.
- Mikeyboie does this on every bastion type, whatever the route.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, throw your gold to the side of the hole, then cover the piglins with gravel and soul sand.
2. Collect every trade while standing outside the hole.
