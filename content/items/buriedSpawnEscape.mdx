About half of End spawns are buried under end stone. Instead of mining all the way out, a pearl clip
(throwing an ender pearl into the ceiling so it teleports you through) gets you out much faster. Start
with the shallow cases, O54 to O56.

## How it works

The O level is the y of the highest block above you on the spawn platform. Mine at the back of the
platform aiming backwards, and turn around once the sky shows.

| O | Clip |
| - | ---- |
| 53 or less | No clip: mine and stack out, then pearl. |
| 54 | Mine 2. |
| 55 | Mine 3. |
| 56 | Mine 3, standstill pearl, hold jump (pitch about −82.5). |

Pearl clipping is an allowed glitch. A gold pickaxe reduces the time lost.

## How to practice

1. In the zero practice map, set a buried spawn at each O level and clip out.
2. Learn the three clip fails below so you can recognise your own.
