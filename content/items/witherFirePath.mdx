Wither skeletons avoid fire. A fire placed on a bridge forces them to walk around it along the edge, where one sprint hit knocks them off.

## How it works

- With a fire charge or flint and steel, place fire in the middle of the bridge in their path.
- They route around it onto the edge. Sprint-hit them off with knockback, without spending axe
  hits.
- Mikeyboie also shows lava placed at the side of a bridge where withers are stacked: they path into
  it and fall.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, switch to survival on a fortress bridge with withers chasing you.
2. Place fire between you and them, then sprint-hit each one off as it reaches the edge.
3. Try it on narrow and wide bridges.
