The desert temple's loot sits in a hidden room under the floor, above a trap of 9 TNT wired to a
pressure plate. Getting in without setting it off, and then using that TNT, is the core of every
desert temple seed.

## How it works

- Enter through a side entrance and go to the centre of the lower floor.
- Mine one of the blocks beside the centre. The middle block of a side mines slightly faster. Stand on
  it while it breaks, so you fall as soon as it's gone.
- In the room below, mine the block under the pressure plate without landing on the plate. The plate
  drops as an item: pick it up.
- The four chests and the TNT are now safe to use. How you blow them open is the next skill.
- In Ranked, no monsters or bats spawn in desert temples.

## How to practice

1. Practice the drop on desert temple seeds until you never touch the plate.
2. Check your loot before you leave: you need it to plan crafts and food.
