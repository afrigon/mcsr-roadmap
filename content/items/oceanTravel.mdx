Ocean seeds end in a magma ravine somewhere across the water. A boat gets you there fast, and doors
give you air at the bottom.

## How it works

- Craft a boat on the island before you leave.
- Bring three doors. Doors placed underwater make air pockets: you need them at the bottom of the
  ravine for both the gravel and the magma portal.
- Placing a door inside yourself is allowed in Ranked.
- Look for the ravine in third person from the boat.
- Use the boat time to sort your inventory with the offhand and hotkeys.
- Leave the crafting table on the island until you are ready to go, so you don't pick it up and
  place it again.

## How to practice

1. Boat around ocean practice seeds while sorting your hotbar, until you can do both at once.
