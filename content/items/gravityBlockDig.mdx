Sand and gravel fall when the block under them is removed. When a column of them blocks your way,
you can mine its bottom and move through as the column drops, instead of digging out every block.

## How it works

- Mining the bottom block of a sand or gravel column makes the blocks above fall down one by one.
- Keep moving through the gap as the column falls instead of waiting for it to settle.
- Don't stand where the column lands: a falling block that lands on you suffocates you.

## How to practice

1. Find gravel or sand columns on a practice seed (beaches, rivers, gravel beaches) and practice
   moving through them.
