The dragon pushes you when it passes through your space, and its knockback can throw you off the
setup or out of position for the next bed.

## How it works

- Stand on the side away from the dragon's path.
- If you are pushed but still on the tower, step back to your standing block before the next
  rotation.
- If you are thrown off, clutch with a water bucket or boat and pearl back up.
- On a bow +1, too much knockback from the first bed delays the second.

## How to practice

1. Turn on the practice map's knockback display and watch which rotations push you.
