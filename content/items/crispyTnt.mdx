CrispyTNT is the Crispy hole with two TNT instead of mining the gold blocks. It guarantees all four
gold blocks and protects the hole.

## How it works

- Craft two buttons and light the two TNT with them. Flint and steel would aggro the piglins.
- Lucento rates it best on double-triple seeds or whenever you have 2 or more TNT.
- Lucento marks earlier versions of the video as outdated; use the showcase linked below.

## How to practice

1. Learn the Crispy hole (Variation 2) first.
2. In the LlamaPag map, carry two TNT and copy the CrispyTNT section of the showcase.
