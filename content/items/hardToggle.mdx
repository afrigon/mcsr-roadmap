Angry piglins refuse to jump down large drops to chase you, so piglins above a ledge get stuck and never reach your hole. Switching the difficulty to Hard raises the height they are willing to drop, and they follow you down.

## How it works

- How far a chasing mob will drop depends on the difficulty. On Easy, piglins won't take a drop of more than about 5 blocks; on Hard they take much bigger ones.
- Switch to Hard while they need to drop, then back to Easy. Piglins hit much harder on Hard, so don't stay on it while they can reach you.
- Typical uses: Housing manhunt (toggle after breaking the center chest), Housing top-down from the gold room, Stables triple to gap, and late piglins stuck above a Bridge hole.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, run Housing manhunt and set the difficulty to Hard right after the chest breaks.
2. Watch the piglins from the upper floor drop down to you, then switch back to Easy before going up.
3. Make the two switches quick enough that they cost almost no time.
