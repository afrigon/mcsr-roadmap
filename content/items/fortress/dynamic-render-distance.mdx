Lowering render distance to about 5 chunks (4 to 6) unloads the mobs further away, so the hostile
mob count on F3 (the M number) drops well under its cap of about 70 and the game spawns more
around you, stray blazes among them. Wither skeletons spawn more too, so keep a way to deal with
them ready.

# When

- **Before entering the fortress**, so blazes start spawning as you search it, even in a bad part.
- **Before going to the spawner**, so strays come to you on the way. It also gives you rods when the
  spawner cannot be found.

# Biomes

It works best in a soul sand valley, warped forest or basalt deltas fortress: those biomes spawn
more mobs and fewer zombified piglins and piglins, so more of the spawns are blazes. The same
biomes also give the most wither skeletons. In a nether wastes or crimson forest fortress,
zombified piglins and piglins take up much of the spawning.
