Glowdar finds the fortress with pie-ray (the debug profiler's spawner reading) from inside treasure,
without going down to break the magma cube spawner. It saves the trip down for the spawner pearl hang.

## How it works

- It uses a client bug: each time a chunk reloads, it adds a ghost spawner entry to the profiler.
- Settings: render distance 18, VSync off, unlimited frame rate.
- Reload the chunk until the `mob_spawner` row reads at least 0.01%. The video below shows how to
  read the direction from there.
- Diagonal spawners are the hard case. One guide author switched to a simple negative pie-ray
  instead, which rules out diagonals, checking the two directions he can overlap with the route
  first.

## How to practice

1. Learn normal pie-ray and the spawner pearl hang first.
2. Watch the Ranked explanation below, then try it in a treasure practice world where you know the
   fortress direction.
