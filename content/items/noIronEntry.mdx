Without an iron pickaxe you can't mine gold blocks, so no route can start. This mostly happens on ruined portal seeds, where Ranked guarantees iron nuggets but not a pickaxe.

## How it works

- Don't start the route. Enter the bastion and loot the nearest chest you know, looking for iron ingots or nuggets (9 nuggets make an ingot).
- Rampart chests are the best bet: in Ranked they hold at least 3 iron, enough for one pickaxe (on Bridge it may be in the lodestone chest).
- A gold pickaxe digs through blackstone quickly, so you can tunnel straight to a chest instead of walking around.
- Once you have 3 iron, block yourself in, craft the pickaxe, and run the route you would have run anyway from where you are. On Housing, ending up at the bottom can make manhunt the natural choice.
- Treasure has a reliable entry: count 3 blocks along the lava and dig in to a chest that always lines up.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, use a loadout with a wooden or gold pickaxe and no iron.
2. For each type, find the fastest chest to reach from the usual entry, then continue into the normal route.
