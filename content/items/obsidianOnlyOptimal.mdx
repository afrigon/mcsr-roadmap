Instead of building the obsidian frame block by block, pillar up and place the whole side in a single
jump. It turns the 10-obsidian portal into a few seconds of work.

## How it works

- Place one block to jump off. In the jump, spam right click while moving the mouse up, so you place
  three blocks, then four.
- Move slightly backwards as you leave the first block, so you are out of the way of the next ones.
  Drag clicking helps here.
- At the top of the jump, swap to a filler block for the last placement if you don't have obsidian
  to spare.
- Then add the bottom, the top and the other side, with filler in the corner.
- Top runners still misplace an extra obsidian sometimes. If you have exactly 10 and aren't
  consistent yet, build it the slow way.

## How to practice

1. Practice three-tall pillars in one jump with cheap blocks, then four-tall.
2. Add the hotbar swap at the peak once the four-tall pillar is reliable.
3. Only use it in runs when you have spare obsidian, until you stop missing.
