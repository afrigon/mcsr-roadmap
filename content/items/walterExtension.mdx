The Walter extension, also called the bridge lower back extension, is a staircase at the lower back
of the bridge that brings 2–4 more piglins. mobina posted it in March 2026, inspired by doogile's
testing. It costs 13 blocks.

## How it works

- It fits routes that start from the lower bridge, when you have the blocks to spare.
- The staircase is the key part. The trading hole in the showcase is a Mogul hole, not an auto
  funnel.
- ClearColdWater's variant puts lava in the pre-dug hole when the terrain is open, so piglins never
  pass the stairs. It needs one extra block so piglins don't push each other off. A further variant
  with the same block count is fully safe from crossbow piglins, but one mistake is fatal.

## How to practice

1. Be consistent at lower-side entry first.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, add the staircase and compare piglin counts with and without.
