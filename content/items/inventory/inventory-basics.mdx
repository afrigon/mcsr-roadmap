# Split

Right-click a stack to pick up half of it. Hold left-click and drag across slots to spread the stack
evenly over every slot the cursor enters.

# Deposit

Right-click with a stack to place one item. Hold right-click and drag across slots to place one item
in each slot the cursor enters.
