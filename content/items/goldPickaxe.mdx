Gold tools are weak but very fast. A gold pickaxe digs through netherrack and blackstone far quicker than iron, which adds up across the nether, the bastion and the stronghold.

## How it works

- Gold has the fastest mining speed of any tool material, so a gold pickaxe breaks netherrack instantly and blackstone much faster than iron.
- It cannot mine gold blocks or obsidian. Keep your iron pickaxe for gold blocks.
- It costs 3 gold ingots and 2 sticks, and it breaks quickly. Craft a new one rather than relying on one.
- Some runners craft their gold helmet and gold pickaxe as soon as they enter the bastion; ruined portals can also provide the gold.
- It also helps outside the nether, for example when digging out after a pearl clip in the End.

## How to practice

1. In practice runs, craft a gold pickaxe from your first gold block and use it for every netherrack and blackstone block.
2. Put it on a fixed hotbar slot next to your iron pickaxe so swapping between them is automatic.
