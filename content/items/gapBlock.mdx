A sprint jump clears a 3-block gap but not a 4-block one. Placing a single block mid-jump on the far
side shortens the gap to 3, so you make it without stopping to bridge.

## The move

- Sprint-jump toward the gap with a block in hand.
- While in the air, look down at the far edge and place one block against it, on the side facing
  you. You land on that block instead of the original edge.
- It is not the same as the Block Clutch, which saves you after an ender pearl onto a wall. Here
  you place the block to extend a jump you would otherwise miss.

## Where it's used

The advanced treasure bastion route (Boomer) has a 4-block gap where this saves a bridge.

## Getting it consistent

Build 4-block gaps over a safe drop and drill the jump until the placement happens at the top of
the jump without slowing your run-up.
