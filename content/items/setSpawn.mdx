Placing a bed near the portal room and setting your spawn there is cheap insurance. A death in the End
or the stronghold then puts you back next to the portal instead of at the world spawn, thousands of
blocks away.

## How it works

- In the overworld, use a bed to set your respawn point. You don't need to sleep: using it sets the
  spawn even during the day.
- Leave the bed in place. If it's broken, you respawn at the world spawn.
- Respawning restores full health, hunger and saturation, which also makes deliberate hunger resets
  possible (see Hunger Reset).
- Items you carried drop where you died. A death in the End can cost them for good.

## How to practice

1. In every practice stronghold, place the bed next to the portal room and set spawn before filling
   the eyes.
2. Pick one spot for the bed (for example on the portal room stairs) so it costs no thought.
