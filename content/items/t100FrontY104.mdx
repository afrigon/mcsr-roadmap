The highest front setup on T-100, at y104. It is a long build, but the extra height gives the dragon
more time and keeps you clear of the crystal.

## How it works

- Stand on 27 −26 at y104. Couriway's guide uses 27 −27, and kohout's 31 −30 is a different,
  modern-style offset. All three work.
- y104 leaves little spare time even on an open spawn, so take it only with a fast pearl.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set front T-100 and build to y104 until the build is quick.
