In the nether a water bucket evaporates, so the fall-damage clutch there is a boat: place it under you
and get in before you hit the ground. Butterfly clicking is the way to do it without a drag-click
mouse.

## How it works

- You need a flat 2×2 area to place a boat. It won't place in a 1×1 spot, so pick flat ground as you
  fall when you can.
- Butterfly click: alternate two fingers on the right mouse button as fast as you can. The first click
  places the boat, the next gets you in.
- The further down you look, the more time you have to place and enter. Calisthenicuber looks slightly
  ahead rather than straight down and holds forward.
- In 1.16.1 you pop up slightly when the boat is placed, which helps you get in.
- **Legality**: the sticky boat clutch is an allowed glitch. The shift clutch is **not legal** under
  the speedrun rules; never use it in a run.

## How to practice

1. Jump off tall pillars onto flat ground in a practice world and boat clutch every time.
2. Practice in a zero-cycle practice map, where you boat down after the dragon kill.
3. Raise the height once it is reliable.
