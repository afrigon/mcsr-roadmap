At least four explosives are needed to kill the dragon before it lands. The optimal one cycle uses
exactly that: four single beds, or a 3+1.

## How it works

- Four beds: one per head swing, each timed tightly so none is wasted.
- 3+1: if the dragon is very low after an explosion, a second explosion about half a second later
  on the same head turn (the "+1") can finish it.
- Every bed has to land close to full damage, so this needs the same timing as five beds with no
  spare.

## How to practice

1. Once five-bed one cycles are consistent, take one bed less and keep the timing.
2. Practice the +1 on the last swing when the dragon is low.
