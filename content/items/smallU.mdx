The Small U is a small ruined portal shaped like a U, with one pillar taller than the other. It needs
three obsidian, cast from its three lava blocks.

## How it works

- Recognise it by the U shape and the uneven pillars.
- It has one gold block, three lava and one chest, on the tall-pillar side.
- Cast the three missing obsidian from its lava. If a lava source generated as magma, there isn't
  enough lava and the completion fails.
- The reference completion takes about 9.6 seconds without crying obsidian.

## How to practice

1. Copy the reference completion in a practice map.
2. Check the lava count before starting, so you notice a magma block in time.
