A pearl hang freezes a pearl in mid-flight so you can finish what you are doing and teleport later.
You throw toward where you are going next, keep killing blazes or trading, then release the pearl when
you are ready.

## How it works

- Throw the pearl, then lower render distance to 2 before it lands. Once the pearl is in a chunk that
  is no longer loaded, it stops moving.
- Check with chunk borders (F3+G) and hitboxes (F3+B) that the pearl is more than two chunks away.
- Raise render distance again to reload the pearl. It continues and teleports you.
- Be ready to block clutch or boat clutch when it lands, and drink fire resistance first if it might
  land near lava.
- Common uses: at the blaze spawner, pearl toward your blind direction and keep killing blazes for the
  last rods; in a bastion, pearl toward the fortress and finish trading.

## How to practice

1. Bind a key to change render distance, so you don't need the menu.
2. In a nether practice world, throw a pearl, hang it, walk around, and release it. Repeat until it
   never lands before you lower the render distance.
