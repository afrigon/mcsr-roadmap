Sometimes the flying eye jumps back and forth between two pixel positions while you hold still. You
can't tell which one is right, so you measure both and only trust an answer they agree on.

## How it works

- Eye wiggle is a rendering effect. It has nothing to do with your position (that is desync).
- Measure as late as possible; the wiggle often settles.
- If the eye only flickers to the other offset briefly, use the position it shows most.
- If it alternates evenly, press F3+C and try both pixel offsets in Ninjabrain Bot.
- If both offsets give the same top stronghold, with at least one at 99.9–100%, go. Otherwise throw
  another eye from somewhere else.
- With standard settings it rarely matters.

## How to practice

1. When you see wiggle in practice, deliberately enter both offsets and compare the results.
2. Build the habit of pausing on the eye a moment longer before pressing F3+C.
