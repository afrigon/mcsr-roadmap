Before you can build anything you need to know where the dragon will come down. Read it during your
first pearl toward the tower, so the setup choice is made before you land.

## How it works

- **Front or back** is decided by the taller of the two middle pillars and is fixed per seed.
- **Diagonal or straight**: the node is either diagonal (7 times in 8) or straight between the
  fountain and a middle pillar (the "1/8"). This roll is not fixed per seed.
- Node positions: front diagonal 28 −29, back diagonal −29 28, front straight 20 0, back straight
  −20 0.
- **Rotation**, clockwise or counter-clockwise, is 50/50 and read from the direction the dragon
  approaches. It doesn't change bed placement on normal setups, but it does on offset, side and
  multiblock setups.

## How to practice

1. In the practice map, randomise node and rotation and name both aloud before your pearl lands.
2. Watch the two rotation clips below until you can tell them apart instantly.
