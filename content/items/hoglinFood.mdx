Hoglins are dangerous, but they drop cooked porkchops when they burn to death. When the overworld left you without food, a crimson forest full of hoglins fixes it in seconds.

## How it works

- Hoglins spawn in crimson forests. Anger them, then pillar up 4 blocks so they can't reach you.
- Pour lava so it flows down onto them. They burn and drop cooked porkchops. Mikeyboie got 10 from three hoglins.
- Place a block first so the lava only flows into one spot and doesn't spread everywhere.
- In Ranked, hoglin food drops are standardized for both players.
- To avoid hoglins instead: they flee from warped fungus, nether portals and respawn anchors (charged or not).
- Setting a hoglin on fire just before it hits you cancels its knockback.

## How to practice

1. In a practice world, find a crimson forest and try the 4-block tower and lava on a group of hoglins.
