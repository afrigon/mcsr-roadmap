The recipe book fills the crafting grid for you, so you never have to place ingredients by hand.
Every faster crafting method in this category builds on it.

## How it works

- Open your inventory or a crafting table and click the green book icon to show the recipe list.
- Click a recipe to move its ingredients into the grid, then shift-click the output to take it.
  Shift-clicking the recipe instead fills the grid with as many sets as you have materials for.
- The filter button next to the search bar switches between every known recipe and only the ones
  you can craft with what you carry. Showing only craftable recipes keeps the list short.
- Recipes unlock when you first touch the right item. A boat only appears after you touch water,
  for example.
- The 2×2 grid in your inventory handles small recipes such as planks, sticks, eyes of ender and
  wool. Anything bigger needs a crafting table.

## How to practice

1. In a casual world, craft a full set of wooden tools using only the recipe book.
2. Repeat with the craftable-only filter on, and note how much shorter the list gets.
3. Pay attention to which page each early recipe lands on; you will look for them every run.
