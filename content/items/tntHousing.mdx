TNT housing blows up the buried gold line instead of mining it block by block, once the piglins are
already trading. Calisthenicuber calls it rarely used. It works on top-down, manhunt and half-down,
as long as you are taking the upper blocks.

## How it works

- Before dropping into the gold room, place three blocks above the drop. They stop the explosion
  from opening the roof and letting piglins fall on you.
- Toss a few extra gold ingots toward the hole side, so the piglins are busy when the TNT goes off.
- Set up the hole and start trading as usual.
- Mine the next gold block and the one behind it, and place the TNT in the gap.
- Light it with a button, never flint and steel: flint-lit TNT counts as your damage and aggros
  piglins.
- Mine the three blocks in front of you and block yourself off before it explodes. The blast
  breaks almost all of the gold line; collect the drops and throw them into the hole.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, do the TNT part in creative first to learn the placements.
2. Run it in survival on top-down until the button craft and block-off fit inside the fuse.
