Default binds put hotbar slots 6 to 9 out of reach of your left hand, which leaves half your hotbar unusable mid-run. There is no "best" layout, but every runner moves the keys they press most onto keys they can hit without looking.

## How it works

- **Hotbar slots 6–9**: rebind to keys near WASD. HiTrippy uses Z, X, C and V. If a new key clashes with a creative-mode bind (such as saving or loading a hotbar), clear that bind: Ranked is played in survival.
- **Offhand swap** (F by default): put it somewhere comfortable; some runners use the key above Tab.
- **Pick block** (middle mouse by default): keep it reachable. It pulls a block from your inventory into the hotbar without opening the inventory.
- **Toggle perspective** (F5): reachable, since you check behind and above yourself often.
- Turn auto-jump off.
- Ranked follows the speedrun.com rules: one key per game input and one input per key, buttons only (no scroll wheel).
- Save the binds in StandardSettings so every world starts with them.

## How to practice

1. Change one group of binds at a time, starting with the hotbar.
2. Play a few practice runs or MiniPracticeKit splits and only switch slots by key, never by scrolling.
