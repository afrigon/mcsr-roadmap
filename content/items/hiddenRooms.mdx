Some strongholds hide a branch behind a wall. Without knowing where to look, a hidden room can hold the
only way to the portal room.

## How it works

- Hidden rooms are only off five-ways, and only on the −X or −Z side relative to the entrance.
- Look for an indentation (a dugout spot) in a five-way's wall. Mine through it and there's a room
  behind, up or down the stairs from it. This is the only place in a stronghold where you mine through
  walls.
- Late five-ways sometimes show an indentation with no room behind it.
- Water behind the indentation means an ocean stronghold.

## How to practice

1. In StrongholdTrainer, check every five-way for an indentation on its −X or −Z side.
2. Mine into each one you find and note whether a room was behind it.
