Three TNT clears an open spawner about as well as anything: Calisthenicuber measured 95.5% available space right after the blast, with no fire to put out. It is JackoWacko62's default on an open spawner whenever you have 4 or more TNT.

## How it works

- Two TNT go at the back of the spawner and one at the front.
- **Light the back pair first, then the front.** Order matters: the wrong order can destroy the
  spawner.
- Put the back TNT on top of the soul sand, not behind it. A viewer of Jacko's video lost two
  spawners that way.
- Overlap: build the setup, light the back pair, then place and light the front TNT.
- It usually leaves 1 to 3 blocks to mine. Craft during the fuse.

## How to practice

1. In Mescht's Blaze Practice map, give yourself 3 TNT and copy Jacko's setup.
2. Do the overlap version until the front TNT always goes in after the back pair is lit.
3. Mine the leftovers and build the platform before the first wave.
