Llama's Bastion Practice is a 1.16.1 world that generates any bastion you choose, as many times as you want. It is the standard way to learn routes without spending runs on them.

## How it works

- Download the latest release, put the extracted folder in your `saves` directory, and open it as a normal world. It does not work in 1.16.2 or later.
- Options let you pick the bastion type, turn the surrounding terrain on or off, choose your spawn point, and control variants such as the rampart layouts.
- A loadout lets you start with the items your route assumes (gold armor, pickaxe, blocks).
- Generation is close to the real game; the author lists only minor differences, such as no mushrooms on the bastion.

## How to practice

1. Start with one type and one beginner route. Turn terrain off at first so the structure is easy to read.
2. Repeat the route until the hole fills the same way every time, then turn terrain on.
3. Switch the type to random and practice recognizing it and choosing the route.
4. Run new routes here first, never in Ranked.
