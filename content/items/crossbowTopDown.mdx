A top-down variant shown by the MCSR Wiki channel that reached 24 piglins, more than any other
housing route on this roadmap. It is a high-end refinement; learn the optimal top-down first.

## How it works

- It is the top-down route with a crossbow added to its aggro.
- It works best when you take the extra gold blocks, because the piglins from the bottom of the
  bastion then run up the stairs toward you.
