Buried treasure chests often hold TNT. Use it on the island's trees and you get your wood in one blast,
so the iron you'd have spent on shears goes elsewhere.

## How it works

- When the chest has TNT, don't make shears.
- Craft a trigger: a pressure plate from spare iron or gold, or a button from one log.
- Place the TNT between two trees and stand behind a trunk: you only take about half a heart.
- Collect the logs, then make your tools, boat and doors.
- A gold pressure plate or button also gives the air pocket for looting the chest.

## How to practice

1. On buried treasure practice seeds with TNT, pick the best pair of trees before you dig the chest up.
