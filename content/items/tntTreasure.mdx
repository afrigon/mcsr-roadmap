TNT treasure breaks the gold wall in the second tower with an explosion instead of mining it.
Calisthenicuber puts the saving at about 5 seconds on average and 6 to 8 on a good run.

## How it works

- Pick-block some of the tower's brick when you reach the second tower.
- Place a soft block (sandstone, which you often carry from desert temples; dirt is also mentioned)
  in the wall, then TNT on top of it.
- Light it with a button. Flint and steel counts as you damaging the block and aggros piglins.
- While it burns, place three blocks, break one, then break two more so piglins can't path to you.
  Stand clear.
- The blast often breaks both nearby gold blocks, but not always; mine what's left.
- The same setup works on the dry tower of a lava-filled treasure.

## How to practice

1. Learn the TNT position in creative in the LlamaPag map.
2. Add it to full Boomer runs and compare split times with and without it.
