A hunger reset sets spawn, dies on purpose and respawns with full health, hunger and saturation, which
is faster than eating. In the stronghold it comes after the preemptive read, and a reset in the portal
room before a zero cycle is common.

## How it works

- **Preemptive first**: dropped items and mobs ruin pie chart readings.
- **Set spawn** with a bed next to where you'll die.
- **Keep your items** by dying in a 2-block hole beside the respawn point, so the drops stay together.
- **Ways to die**: pearls into a corner (about 5–6 damage per second), lava or fire (remove it before
  you die so the items survive), gravel suffocation (about 2 per second), or fall damage (a drop of
  n+2 blocks deals n damage).
- After respawning you have about 3 s of damage immunity; pearls thrown in that window cost no health.

## How to practice

1. In a practice stronghold, do the preemptive read, then set spawn and reset in the portal room.
2. Time the reset against simply eating, and pick the death method you do fastest.
