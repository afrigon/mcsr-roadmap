Two community rules of thumb describe where portal rooms generate. They tell you how deep to search and
how to spot a portal room in a broken-up stronghold.

## How it works

- **forsen's law**: the portal room is most often exactly 6 rooms from the starter staircase, and less
  likely the further you go. There is no maximum.
- Why: the portal room is big and needs open space. Generation starts trying to place it from about
  six rooms out, so leads with room to spare (five-ways, four-ways, stairs going down) are better than
  small turns, chest rooms and libraries.
- If the starter leads in only one direction, count from the first split.
- **Fyroah's law**: caves and ravines cut through other rooms, libraries included, but the portal room
  stays intact. A pristine large box inside a cave or ravine is the portal room, even with no iron
  bars in sight.

## How to practice

1. In StrongholdTrainer, count rooms from the starter on every attempt and note where the portal room
   was.
2. Search caved strongholds and look for the intact box before following corridors.
