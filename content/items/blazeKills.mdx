Blaze rods turn into blaze powder, and every eye of ender needs one powder. Getting enough rods at the fortress is the last thing you need from the nether.

## How it works

- One rod makes two blaze powder, so 6 rods make 12 eyes. Take 7 if you can, for margin.
- Not every blaze drops a rod. Hax estimates about 12 kills for 6 rods at average rates.
- Fire resistance makes blaze fireballs harmless; drink it before fighting.
- Hit blazes with critical hits (attack while falling from a jump). With an axe, one crit and one
  normal swing kills a blaze.
- Blazes knocked off the bridge are lost unless you can reach them.

## How to practice

1. Load a blaze practice map and kill blazes at the spawner until you have 7 rods.
2. Count how many kills it took and how much damage you took.
3. Repeat until you kill each blaze in two hits.
