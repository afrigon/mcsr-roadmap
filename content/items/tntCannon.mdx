When no two trees stand close together, one TNT's explosion can launch a second TNT onto a distant
tree. It is a fun, optional trick: slightly faster than walking over, and it brings back a lot of
logs and dirt.

## How it works

- Build two short pillars. The first TNT sits where its blast reaches the nearer tree. The second sits
  where the first explosion pushes it toward the farther tree.
- Light the second TNT a moment after the first. Lit too close together, they explode side by side
  and the second one never flies.
- You usually have no flint and steel yet on a desert temple seed, so the temple's pressure plate is
  the trigger.
- Aim for about 14 logs in total; 10 is enough.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, place a TNT next to a lit one and watch where it lands. Change the gap
   between the pillars until it lands on the trunk.
2. Practice the delay between the two lights: watch for the second TNT being thrown, not blown up in
   place.
