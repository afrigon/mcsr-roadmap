A spawner nobody has been near yet fires its first wave about a second after you come within range. If a second spawner is close, walking up to it gets you a free wave while your first spawner is still on cooldown.

## How it works

- A spawner only works while a player is within 16 blocks of it.
- A spawner that has never been active spawns its first wave about 1 s after you first get in
  range. Later waves follow its normal cooldown.
- Up to 4 blazes spawn per wave. A wave is skipped while 6 blazes are already around the spawner,
  so kill the blazes you have before triggering more.
- Each extra rod is worth about 7 s of spawner time (ClearColdWater), so a free wave is a big gain.
- In Ranked, blaze spawn timing and positions are the same for both players, so triggering the
  second spawner gives you the same blazes your opponent would get.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, find a fortress with two spawners close together.
2. Clear the first spawner, then walk into range of the second right after a wave.
3. Time how soon its first wave appears, and kill both waves.
