Section crafting uses the recipe book's category tabs to cut the list down to a few items, so you
find a recipe without paging. It needs no setup and is a good step before search crafting.

## How it works

- The tabs on the side of the recipe book are tools, weapons and armour; building blocks;
  miscellaneous (functional blocks, food and materials); and redstone, which also holds doors.
- Useful sections:
  - **Building blocks**: nether bricks, wool and glowstone together, the crafts after the bastion.
  - **Miscellaneous**: beds, respawn anchors, buckets and ingots.
  - **Weapons**: bow and shield.
  - **Redstone**: TNT, pressure plates and doors.
- Picking up a new item shifts the list, so you can't always pre-aim a click. Putting an item in the
  offhand can remove junk from a section.
- k4yfour calls this "section crafting"; "tab crafting" is also used for pressing Tab and Space to
  jump between the search bar and the grid (see Search Crafting).

## How to practice

1. Learn which tab holds each recipe in the list above.
2. Craft the post-bastion set (nether bricks, wool, glowstone) and the fortress set (beds, bow) from
   the tabs only.
