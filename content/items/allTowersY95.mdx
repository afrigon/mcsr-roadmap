Fill in the rest of the low towers: every non-offset front and back setup at y95, plus y99 on front
M-85 and M-88. Higher standing heights give the dragon more time, so they are faster when you have
the pace to reach them.

## How it works

| Tower | Front (28 −27) | Back (−30 25) |
| ----- | -------------- | ------------- |
| Small Boy (76) | 95 | 95 |
| Small Cage (79) | 95 | 95 |
| Tall Cage (82) | 95 | 95 |
| M-85 | 95, 99 | 95 |
| M-88 | 95, 99 | 95 |

- A non-offset setup needs a standing height of at least tower height + 5, otherwise the bed breaks
  the crystal.
- Stay on the standard heights (y86, y90, y95, y99, y104). The dragon crosses the node at fixed
  heights, and other heights risk breaking the first bed.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, randomise tower and front or back, always at y95.
2. Add y99 on front M-85 and M-88.
