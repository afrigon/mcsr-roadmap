2+2 from y104, the highest standing height: two rotations, each with a double. Standing this high
gives the dragon the longest descent, so it has the most room to be corrected with double shot.

## How it works

- Both rotations use the bow +1, with extra arrows (double shot) to correct the dragon onto the node
  and shed excess height.
- y104 setups leave little spare time, so this is only on when the pearl and build are fast.

## How to practice

1. Get 2+2 consistent at y95 and y99 first.
2. On the practice map, move to y104 and drill both rotations.
