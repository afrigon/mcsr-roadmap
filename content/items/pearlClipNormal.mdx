Buried spawns at O56 and O57 are the most common clips worth learning after the shallow ones. A pearl
clip throws an ender pearl into the ceiling so it teleports you up through the end stone. Pearl
clipping is an allowed glitch.

## How it works

The O level is the y of the highest block above you on the spawn platform. Mine at the back of the
platform aiming backwards.

| O | Normal clip |
| - | ----------- |
| 56 | Mine 3, standstill pearl, hold jump (pitch about −82.5). |
| 57 | Build up 1, then clip. |

- After a clip you can see whether it is a back dragon.
- Common fails: bad aim, shifting early, jumping early. The clips below show each one.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set O56 and O57 buried spawns and clip until it works first try.
