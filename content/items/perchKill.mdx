When the dragon lands on the fountain (a "perch"), it is an easy target. The beginner kill is to wait
for perches and hit it with your weapon, or explode beds on it, as many times as it takes.

## How it works

- After each flight path the dragon rolls to perch or to strafe (fly at you shooting fireballs).
  More crystals alive means fewer perches.
- On a perch the dragon flies to the fountain at 0 0 and circles down onto it.
- A perched dragon that takes more than 50 damage flies off again, so a melee kill usually takes
  several perches.
- A bed or respawn anchor used in the End explodes. Any number of beds on a perch helps.
- The MCSR wiki notes that critical hits add no extra damage to the dragon, so there is no need to
  jump; use fully charged hits, ideally with an axe.

## How to practice

1. In an End practice map, wait at the fountain for a perch and melee it with full-charge hits.
2. Add beds: place one by the fountain and right-click it as the dragon's head passes over.
