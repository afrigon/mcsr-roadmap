Speed bridging (also called ninja bridging) is sneak bridging where you let go of sneak between
blocks, so you move at walking speed instead of sneaking speed. Calisthenicuber puts it at about three
times faster than sneak bridging, which matters on seeds where you bridge a long way to a bastion.

## How it works

- Hold back and one strafe key (S and D) the whole time, with your view at about 45° (44.3° to 46°
  works). This keeps you straight without touching the mouse.
- Crouch at the edge, place the block, and let go of crouch as soon as it is placed. Walk back and
  crouch again just before the edge.
- Re-crouching too early only costs a little speed. Re-crouching too late makes you fall. When in
  doubt, crouch early.
- Place by tapping right click (Calisthenicuber's choice), holding it (slower) or spam clicking (less
  consistent). You can hear from the placement rhythm whether you are fast or slow.

## How to practice

1. Do single blocks one at a time: crouch, place, release, re-crouch. Don't chain them yet.
2. Start chaining two or three, then a full row. Speed comes from rhythm, not from rushing.
3. Add an occasional jump to step up one block while bridging; this leads into speed staircasing.
