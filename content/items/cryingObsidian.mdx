Each obsidian block of a ruined portal has a 15% chance to generate as crying obsidian, which doesn't
count as part of a frame. A portal with crying obsidian looks broken, but most types can still be
completed.

## How it works

- Crying obsidian is the darker obsidian with purple specks. Check every frame block before you plan.
- Always check whether you can still complete the existing portal before building a lava portal.
- The completion reference shows a separate crying-obsidian route for most types. Each one takes
  longer than the clean version: for example about 16 seconds instead of 3.6 for the Half Portal, and
  about 15 instead of 6.6 for the Hanging Portal.
- Big Bertha with crying obsidian is completed using its lava pools.

## How to practice

1. For each type you know, learn its crying-obsidian completion from the reference video.
2. In a practice map, spot the crying block before your first click.
