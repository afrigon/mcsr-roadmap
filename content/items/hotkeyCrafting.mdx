Hotkey crafting moves materials with the number keys and mouse drags instead of picking stacks up
one by one. It is the fastest way to turn logs into a table, sticks and tools in the overworld.

## How it works

- Hover a grid slot and press a hotbar key to move that hotbar stack into the slot. Shift-click the
  output to craft everything at once.
- With only two logs: make the crafting table, then hold left click and drag down the grid column
  to lay out planks for sticks in the same motion.
- With three logs: make the table, then right-click drag down and right-click drag up to get two
  sets of sticks and the table.
- Hover an output and press a hotbar key to send it to that slot, which keeps your hotbar organised.
- Shift and double-click a leftover stack in the grid to pull it all back into your inventory.
- **The button trap**: shift-clicking planks too many times keeps crafting and turns them into
  buttons. Stop after the clicks you need.
- With iron to spare, double-clicking the iron in the grid sets up a bucket and shears in one craft
  (shown in the video below).

## How to practice

1. Practise the two-log and three-log sequences with the HiTrippy video at half speed.
2. Craft a full overworld kit (table, sticks, pickaxe, bucket, flint and steel) from a chest of logs
   and iron, and time it.
