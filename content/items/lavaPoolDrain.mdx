Waiting for the water to drain out of a finished frame wastes seconds on every lava pool portal.
Move the water before the frame is done, and the last blocks go in while it drains.

## How it works

- Build the frame as usual, but stop after one side and the top are cast.
- Pick up the water and put it lower: inside the portal itself is the shortest movement, or anywhere in
  the pool.
- Cast the last two blocks while the old water is still flowing away.
- Light as soon as the inside is empty.

## How to practice

1. Do the normal 2-deep portal until you can build it without thinking. Then switch to this order
   only.
2. Watch the order closely in the clips below: the water moves after one side and the top, not before.
3. Keep the crosshair still on every bucket use to avoid a ghost bucket.
