Not every perch comes from the north or south. When you build on the east or west side, the dragon
moves slowly on its way down, which gives you extra time to get into position.

## How it works

- k4yfour recommends standing north or south "to avoid slow dragon movement and for easier bed
  timing". The east–west side is the slow one: more time to reach the fountain, but the timing is
  less regular.
- The obsidian goes on the side the dragon perches from, and you stand where its head swings.
- Learn east–west first as the forgiving version, then move to north–south.

## How to practice

1. In an End practice map, build an east–west setup and do five-bed one cycles from it.
2. Watch where the dragon perches from before choosing your side.
