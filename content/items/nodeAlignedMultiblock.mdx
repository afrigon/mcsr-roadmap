hasbiWalks built a full set of zero setups where every one is multiblock: three obsidian give two bed
positions, the standing block sits diagonal to both, and you pick the bed that matches the dragon on
each rotation. The set claims better alignment with the dragon's actual node.

## Why it's here and not in Zero Cycle

On normal (non-offset) setups multiblock costs more than it gives:

- The build is measurably slower, about 2.50 s against 2.05 s for the two-obsidian version, and uses a
  third obsidian.
- pion: multiblock there "would trend towards being less consistent as it is a slower setup … way
  easier to mess up … shouldn't be used there". 2+2 doesn't need it either: double arrows adjust the
  dragon on the plain hybrid stands.
- You have to memorise the bed side per rotation for every tower.

Offset and side setups are different: there multiblock is how the setup is built, and those pages
cover it.
