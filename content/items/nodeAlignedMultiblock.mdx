hasbiWalks made a full set of multiblock setups that claim better alignment with the dragon's actual
node. It is an optional optimisation: later guides from doogile and Couriway still teach the standard
coordinates.

## How it works

- Every setup in the set is multiblock, so you memorise the bed side per rotation for every tower.
- Examples: front universal 27 −29 at y95, 1/8 multiblock 21 1 front and −22 −2 back at y88 and y93.
- hasbiWalks rates his own 1/8 y97 and Tall Boy side worse than the normal setups on back.
- Several overlays in the video are wrong. Corrections from the description: 3:20 is clockwise, the
  front universal is 27 −29, 0:57 Tall Cage is y90, and the two M-88 back clips labelled "85" are
  M-88. The back universal was never recorded, and the back T-97 coordinates are unverified.

## How to practice

1. Learn only the setups that beat the ones you already know, one tower at a time.
