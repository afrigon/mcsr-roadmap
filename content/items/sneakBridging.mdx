Sneak bridging lets you cross a gap or a lava lake safely without looking where you are going. It is
slow, but it is the safe base that speed bridging is built on.

## How it works

- Stand on the last block, face away from the direction you want to go and hold sneak (crouch).
  Sneaking stops you from walking off an edge.
- Walk backward to the edge, look down at the side of the block under you and place the next block.
- Keep walking backward and placing. You never let go of sneak, so you can't fall.
- Holding back and one strafe key with your view at about 45° keeps you lined up on the bridge
  without drifting.

## How to practice

1. Bridge a straight line of 30 blocks over the void or water in a practice world.
2. Do it again over lava once you stop missing placements.
3. Count how long a 30-block bridge takes; speed bridging should later get that down to about a third.
