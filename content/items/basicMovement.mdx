Sprinting, jumping, pillaring and bridging are how you move through every split of the run. Getting
them smooth early saves seconds everywhere and makes every later movement skill easier to learn.

## How it works

- **Sprint jumping**: hold forward, sprint and jump. It is the fastest normal way to cross open ground.
- **Jump timing on slopes**: if you jump at the wrong moment going uphill, your foot clips the block
  edge and you lose your momentum. Walk a step between jumps, or turn the mouse slightly left or right,
  so each jump clears the block cleanly.
- **Pillaring**: look straight down, jump and place a block under yourself at the top of the jump.
  Tapping jump and tapping place for every block is faster than holding both buttons.
- **Basic bridging**: crouch at the edge of a block so you can't fall, look down at the edge, place
  the next block against its side and step onto it.

## How to practice

1. Sprint-jump up uneven hills in a creative world until you stop bumping block edges.
2. Pillar 20 blocks with taps instead of held buttons, then compare how long it takes.
3. Bridge across a short gap, crouched the whole time, until you never fall off.
