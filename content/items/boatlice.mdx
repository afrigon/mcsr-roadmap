Boatlice is a trading-hole design by Lucento (May 2026) for triple-to-chalice routes. It comes in a
standard and a TNT version, each for open and buried bridges. It has not been compared against the
holes in Lodesteal and Bank Heist.

## How it works

- Use it in place of the chalice hole of a triple-to-chalice route.
- Pick the variant from the bridge (open or buried) and from whether you TNT the chalice.
