Zero the low front towers (s76, c79, c82, m85, m88) with one standing block, 28 −27, and the two
easiest heights, y90 and y95. One coordinate covering five towers makes this the first set worth
memorizing.

## How it works

| Tower | Standing | Heights |
| ----- | -------- | ------- |
| s76 | 28 −27 | 86, 90, 95 |
| c79 | 28 −27 | 86, 90, 95, 99 |
| c82 | 28 −27 | 90, 95, 99 |
| m85 | 28 −27 | 90, 95, 99 |
| m88 | 28 −27 | 95, 99 |

- A non-offset setup needs a standing height of at least tower height + 5, otherwise the bed breaks
  the crystal. That is why m88 starts at y95.
- Older guides use 28 −26 and y87 or y88. 28 −27 and y86 are the current values, confirmed in
  doogile's and kohout's pinned corrections.
- Front is more forgiving than back because the front node is further from its tower.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, cycle through the five towers at y95, then at y90.
2. Read the node and rotation during the first pearl.
