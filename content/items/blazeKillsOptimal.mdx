Spawner time is spent waiting for waves, so every second saved per kill adds up over the dozen or so blazes a fortress takes. The best kills mix weapons to avoid waiting for a full cooldown.

## How it works

- **Axe only** (stone or iron): one crit, then one swing. Faster than two crits.
- **Axe plus sword**: crit with the axe, switch to an iron or stone sword and crit again. Two jumps,
  and faster than the axe alone. Hax estimates about a second saved per blaze.
- **Diamond sword**: two crits, no cooldown wait. The fastest weapon for blazes.
- **Boat catch**: when a blaze is knocked off the platform, place a boat below it. It often drops
  into the boat, where you can kill it.
- **Overlaps**: with food in the offhand you can eat between swings, and you can do a quick craft
  between crits.

## How to practice

1. In a blaze practice map, give yourself an iron axe and iron sword and kill only with
   axe crit then sword crit.
2. Knock blazes off on purpose and catch them with a boat.
3. Add one inventory craft between kills once the rhythm is automatic.
