Boosting a thrown ender pearl with an explosion is an idea, not a known speedrun technique. No
speedrun source shows it in use.

## How it works

- Explosions push nearby entities, so a bed or respawn anchor going off next to a flying pearl
  could in principle add speed to it.
- No ruling on its legality exists. As normal physics rather than a glitch it would likely be
  allowed, but treat that as unconfirmed.
- Related, confirmed tech: minecart boosting (an allowed glitch) and pearl clipping.
