Treasure's chests hold the iron, obsidian and gold armor that make the route safe. Knowing which
chests to open first, and when, saves you from routing blind into angry piglins.

## How it works

- **Wall chest by the moat**: a chest can spawn in the wall near the lava moats, on the side where
  you see the bridge gold on the right of your screen. Dig a couple of blocks in to check. It only
  spawns sometimes, but it can hold gold, obsidian or iron, and the lava-moat entry passes right by
  it.
- **Tower chests**: the right tower holds two single chests with a double chest on the same floor. These
  are the ones you loot while the piglins trade.
- **No iron pickaxe**: you can't mine gold yet, so loot first. At the lava basin, count three blocks
  along the lava and dig in: a chest always lines up there and you can open it from its corner
  without breaking anything. If that gives no iron, work up through the tower chests. Daniel_H212
  notes there are always at least 3 iron between these four chests, which matches Ranked's rampart
  guarantee.
- Ranked's filter also puts at least 5 obsidian in the rampart chests, so loot them before trading
  more for obsidian.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, start at the lava basin and check the moat wall every run.
2. Practice the no-pickaxe opening: the counted chest, then each tower chest, without taking hits.
