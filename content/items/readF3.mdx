The F3 debug screen tells you where you are and which way you face. You read it constantly: to travel to Ninjabrain Bot's stronghold coordinates, to build the blind portal, and to follow coordinates in zero-cycle setups.

## How it works

- **XYZ**: your exact position with decimals. Y is height.
- **Block**: the whole-number coordinates of the block you stand in. Use this line when you follow coordinates; Ninjabrain Bot and setup guides expect it. On negative coordinates, the rounded XYZ values read one off from Block.
- **Chunk**: which 16×16 chunk you're in and your position inside it (0–15 on each axis). Buried treasure and stronghold staircases sit at fixed positions inside a chunk.
- **Facing**: the compass direction, the axis it points along, and your yaw and pitch in degrees.

| Facing | Axis |
| --- | --- |
| South | +Z |
| North | −Z |
| East | +X |
| West | −X |

- Nether coordinates are one eighth of overworld coordinates on X and Z.

## How to practice

1. In any world, pick a coordinate pair and walk to it using only the Block line and Facing.
2. Turn until your yaw matches a target angle, as you will when following a Ninjabrain Bot angle.
