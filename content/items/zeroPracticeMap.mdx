Every zero cycle skill is learned in Mescht's Zero Practice map. It lets you pick the tower, node and
rotation, so you can repeat one setup dozens of times instead of waiting for it in real runs.

## How it works

- Control over tower type and location, front or back, and dragon rotation (clockwise or
  counter-clockwise).
- Damage and knockback display, and a summary of each kill in damage notation (for example 5+0).
- Customisable loadouts. Hold shift while clicking a loadout sign to rename it.
- An inventory randomizer, for practising sorting.
- Control over your spawn and saturation.
- A one-cycle mode and a +1 timing assistant.
- If you break something in the lobby, type `/trigger repair`.

## How to practice

1. Download the latest release and open it with the same mods you run with.
2. Save a loadout that matches your real End hotbar.
3. Pick one tower and one height, and stay there until the setup is automatic.
