Jungle temples appeared as a Ranked seed type on 2026-07-30, described by players as an event-mode
seed. The temple has an arrow-trap hallway leading to a chest and a lever puzzle that opens a hidden
second chest.

## How it works

- Go down the stairs. The lever room and the trapped hallway can be on either side.
- Walk the hallway hugging the outer wall to dodge the arrow traps, then loot the chest.
- Lever puzzle: flick the first lever, flick the next one twice, break the far one. The hidden chest
  by the stairs opens. Watch a video for which lever is which.
- Mine 2 logs before entering so you can craft a pickaxe inside. With 3 diamonds, craft a diamond
  pickaxe right away. Mining cobblestone for stone tools also opens shortcuts.
- The dispensers give extra arrows; melons are backup food.
- Switching to Hard difficulty makes lava-pool fires easier to spot.

## How to practice

1. Watch greenFishy's guide once for the layout and the lever order.
2. Play the seed type whenever it is offered and loot both chests every time.
3. Compare your looting with Literal's optimal route.
