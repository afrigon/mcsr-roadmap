The advanced Donkey Kong replaces the beginner route's trading hole with an auto funnel: a hole
shape late piglins path into on their own, so you loot the chests while they keep arriving.
Calisthenicuber gets 16 piglins trading within a minute. See the easy Donkey Kong page for the base.

## How it works

- The start is the same, but you break the wall block earlier.
- After aggroing with the gold block, jump up above your bridge instead of crossing it. Mine two
  blocks to let the upper piglins down, then place a block to let the lower ones in.
- Throw one gold to the side as a distraction and the rest into the hole.
- Dig the hole two deep, place two blocks above you, and punch a piglin to aggro the rest.
- Loot the chests while the piglins funnel in. When all are in, climb out and go for the other
  rampart.
- With trades running, use the wait to search for the fortress and craft beds.
- On two triples, DK can still beat soog, for example when the fortress is on the left.

## How to practice

1. Be consistent at easy DK first.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, build the funnel in creative once, then practice it live.
3. Repeat until the run is damageless; Calisthenicuber's demo is.
