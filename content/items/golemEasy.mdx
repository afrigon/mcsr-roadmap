When the blacksmith doesn't give enough iron, the iron golem makes up the difference. In Ranked it
always drops exactly 4 iron, so you can plan your crafts before you kill it.

## How it works

- Turn on hitboxes (F3+B). The golem has the only tall, square, three-block hitbox in the village, and
  it stays near the centre and the bell.
- Hit it once, then move somewhere it can't climb up to you. Don't build next to a ledge it can step
  onto.
- Tower up three blocks and crit it with an axe from above: jump and hit while falling.
- Wait for the attack cooldown bar to fill between crits.
- Look around in third person while you fight, to plan the rest of the route.

## How to practice

1. Kill the golem on every village practice seed, even when you don't need the iron.
2. Practice finding it with hitboxes as you enter the village.
