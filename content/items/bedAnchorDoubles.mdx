A double can be a bed and an anchor instead of two beds. Place the anchor first, then the bed on the
same rotation.

## How it works

- The double follows the bow +1 pattern: the arrow starts the invulnerability timer, one explosion
  just before it expires, the other just after.
- An anchor explodes from its own block, so it needs slightly earlier timing than a bed.

## How to practice

1. On front M-88 y95, do bow +1 doubles with the anchor first and the bed second.
