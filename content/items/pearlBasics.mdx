An ender pearl teleports you to where it lands. Pearls are your main way to cross the nether, reach
ledges and save yourself from falls, so throwing them well matters on every seed.

## How it works

- Right click throws the pearl. When it lands, you teleport there and take 2.5 hearts of damage.
- A pearl does not change which way you are facing: you land looking where you were looking when it
  landed.
- A standing throw goes the shortest distance, a sprinting throw goes further, and a sprint-jump throw
  goes furthest.
- Keep pearls on the same hotbar key every run, so you can reach them without looking in an emergency.

## How to practice

1. In a practice world, throw pearls standing, sprinting and sprint-jumping, and watch where each
   lands.
2. Practice reaching your pearl slot without looking at the hotbar.
