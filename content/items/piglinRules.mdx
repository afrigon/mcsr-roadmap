Piglins guard every bastion, and the whole bastion route is built on controlling when they attack
you and when they trade. Learn these rules before your first bastion, or the run ends there.

## How it works

- **Gold armor**: a piglin attacks any player it can see who wears no gold armor. One piece is
  enough. Wear a gold helmet rather than boots, since soul speed boots can come from trades.
- **Breaking gold or chests**: breaking a chest, gold block, gilded blackstone or nether gold ore
  angers every piglin within about 16 blocks of you, with or without gold armor. Opening a chest
  angers the piglins that see you do it.
- **Hitting a piglin**: angers it and nearby piglins for 20–30 seconds, and nothing calms it down.
  A piglin you hit won't trade until its anger wears off.
- **Gold calms them**: a piglin angered by a chest or gold block stops chasing you when it sees a
  thrown gold ingot. Gold armor does not calm it.
- **No upgrades**: a piglin already angry only because it saw you without gold armor stays at that
  level when you break gold. Putting on gold armor then calms it.
- Ranked spawns no extra monsters inside bastions, so the piglins and hoglins there are the only
  threats.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world or the LlamaPag bastion map, walk into a bastion with and without a gold
   helmet and watch which piglins react.
2. Break a chest in view of piglins, then throw an ingot and watch them stop chasing.
3. Punch one piglin and count how long it refuses to trade.
