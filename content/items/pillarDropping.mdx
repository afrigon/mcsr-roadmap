Top runners throw away junk items while they pillar out of a desert temple or a hole, so cleaning the
inventory costs no extra time. It looks hard but takes only a small change to how you hold your blocks.

## How it works

- Put your blocks in the offhand. Placing then comes from the offhand while your main hand is free.
- Pillar as usual, and while you do, switch hotbar slots to the items you want to get rid of and drop
  them.
- The usual moment is climbing out of the desert temple after the TNT, dropping what you don't need
  from the loot.

## How to practice

1. In a practice world, fill the hotbar with junk, offhand a stack of blocks, and pillar 10 blocks
   while dropping every junk slot.
2. Keep the rhythm of the pillar steady; if it slows down, drop fewer items per jump.
