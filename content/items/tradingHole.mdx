A trading hole is a small pit that angry piglins fall into while chasing you. Once they are in, they stay together, can't reach you, and trade every ingot you throw in. Setting it up is the core of every bastion route.

## How it works

- Anger the piglins (break a gold block or chest, or punch one), lead them to your spot and dig a pit one or two blocks deep.
- They drop in after you. Step out, then throw ingots into the hole.
- Trapped piglins can't wander off with your gold or get stuck somewhere else in the bastion.
- Keep throwing gold in waves and loot chests while they trade.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, run a beginner route on each type and build the hole the route calls for.
2. Count the piglins in the hole each time. More piglins means faster trades.
3. Watch for piglins that stop following; see Piglin Aggression for why.
