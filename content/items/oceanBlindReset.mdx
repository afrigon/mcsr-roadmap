When the blind portal comes out onto water, a normal hunger reset fails because your items sink or
float away. The ocean blind reset sets up the bed so you respawn in a contained hole every time.

## How it works

- Place the bed so its pillow hangs off the edge of the obsidian, then die and respawn in the
  prepared hole, whatever side the bed or portal is on.
- Drink fire resistance at the end of the reset.
- Soul sand works for the setup if you jump while setting spawn, but items tend to spill.
- If items do fall into the water, pearl down after them rather than swimming.

## How to practice

1. Find an ocean in a practice world, build a portal frame on the water and repeat the reset until
   no item escapes.
