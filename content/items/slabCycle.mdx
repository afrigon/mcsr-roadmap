Before obsidian and water were standard, runners built one cycles from slabs: slabs on the east and
west sides of the fountain pillar, jumped on to reach the height where beds hit the perched dragon.
It's slow and needs good height, but it is a fun way to kill the dragon with almost nothing.

## The setup

- Place slabs on the east and west sides of the fountain pillar.
- Jump on them to place beds on top while the dragon perches.
- Clips show four-bed slab cycles; they only work with very good height.
