An arrow can change the dragon's rotation. Shooting early in a rotation keeps it; shooting late, over
the bed, flips it from clockwise to counter-clockwise or back. Use it to get the rotation your setup
wants.

## How it works

- Rotation decides bed placement on offset, side and multiblock setups, so a flip can turn a bad
  rotation into a good one.
- Shoot early when you want to keep the rotation, late when you want to flip it.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, shoot at different points of a rotation and watch which way the dragon
   comes back.
