Piglins trade random items for gold ingots, and those trades are where the run gets its ender pearls, string and fire resistance. Every bastion route is a way to get gold into as many piglins as possible at once.

## How it works

- Gold blocks need an iron pickaxe; a gold pickaxe can't mine them. Craft each block into 9 ingots.
- Throw an ingot (Q) near a piglin. It picks it up, admires it for a few seconds, then drops one trade. One ingot, one trade.
- Wear at least one piece of gold armor, or piglins attack on sight.
- Breaking a gold block, chest, gilded blackstone or nether gold ore angers every piglin nearby, gold armor or not. Opening a chest angers the ones that can see you.
- An angry piglin still trades when it gets gold, unless you hit it. A punched piglin keeps chasing you and won't trade for a while.
- Baby piglins take gold and give nothing back. Keep them away from your ingots.
- In Ranked, trades are the same for both players: 8 gold blocks (72 trades) always give exactly 3 pearl trades and at least 6 obsidian.

## How to practice

1. Load the bastion practice map and do a beginner route, such as top-down Housing.
2. Mine the gold, throw it at the piglins following you and collect the trades.
3. Count your pearls and string at the end. Repeat until you never lose ingots to a baby piglin or a punched one.
