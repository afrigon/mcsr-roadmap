Boat TNT treasure is Lucento's concept for improving TNT treasure on the treasure bastion. It
sometimes misses a block, so it stays a concept.

## How it works

- Plain TNT treasure lights TNT on a soft block in the second tower's gold wall, saving 5 to 8
  seconds of mining.
- The concept adds a boat to the setup; the video shows how and where it misses a block.
