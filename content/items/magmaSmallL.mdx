Ocean seeds have no surface lava, so the portal is built at the bottom of a magma ravine, where a
layer of lava sits under the magma blocks. The small L is the beginner magma portal: easy to learn
and low risk. Bring a water bucket, three doors and some blocks.

## How it works

- Find three magma blocks in an L. Mine one of the side blocks, not the corner. The lava underneath
  turns into obsidian against the water.
- Stand beside that block while you mine it, not on top of it, or you can fall into the lava.
- Place a door in the hole to get an air pocket, and two doors behind the other two magma blocks.
- Put a block on the magma in front of you, then mine that magma. The block and door keep the water
  out.
- Scoop four lava: the two under the first two magma blocks, then the two behind them. Place the corner
  lava on top of a door while sneaking.
- Block the open side, mine the leftover blocks and drain the water. Pick it up at the top once, then
  double-click it twice lower down instead of waiting for it to flow away.

## How to practice

1. Offhand the bucket on the way down so you don't have to hotkey it at the bottom.
2. Practice in a portal practice map until the four scoops come in the same order every time.
3. Then learn the 2×1, which is faster and usable on more ravines.
