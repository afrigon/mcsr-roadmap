A plain boat crawl leaves you crawling at walking speed. If you enter the crawl while sprinting, you
keep the sprint, and a sprint crawl mines through a wall much faster. Sprinting while crawling is an
allowed glitch under the speedrun rules.

## How it works

- Mine a short one-tall gap into the wall and get into the boat facing it.
- Hold forward and sprint so you move in the boat. When you reach the wall, press sneak to get out
  while still holding sprint. You come out sprint crawling.
- Hit the ground before you break the boat: hitting the boat with a fully charged axe swing cancels
  the sprint.
- Don't let go of forward, and don't press sneak again: either one ends the sprint. You can check that
  you are still sprinting by jumping, which moves you forward.
- Turning while sprint crawling is possible but hard without touching the tunnel walls.
- k4yfour's method gets into and breaks the boat at the same moment, with the boat already in the
  tunnel. It is the fastest version.

## How to practice

1. Learn the plain boat crawl first.
2. Practice the sneak-out while holding sprint until you come out sprinting every time.
3. Mine a straight 20-block sprint crawl, then try one with a turn.
