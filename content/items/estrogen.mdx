Estrogen is a left-side bridge route by Laysar and chloe (June 2025), a variant of the Hamazon
route. Its authors claim 1–2 more piglins than Donkey Kong and a hole filled 10–15 seconds earlier.

## How it works

- From the chalice you need three pre-crafted stairs. They stop piglins jumping and pushing each
  other off the bridge.
- Don't sneak over the edge while building the bridge.

## How to practice

1. Be consistent at the optimal Donkey Kong first.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, set the left rampart to a triple and copy the route from the
   video at reduced speed.
