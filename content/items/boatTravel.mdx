A boat is the fastest way across water, and it is also your fall-damage tool, eye-measuring tool and
crawl tool later on. It costs five planks of overworld wood: nether wood can't make a boat.

## How it works

- Place the boat on water, get in, and steer with the movement keys and the mouse.
- Boats keep you from taking fall damage when you boat down a drop, unless the boat hits the ground at
  the wrong height. Then the boat breaks and you take the full fall. That is why runners often get out
  of the boat just before they reach the ground.
- Getting out with sneak and holding forward carries you further than boating all the way down.
- If you fall into lava near land, a boat placed on the lava gets you out. A boat placed on a block
  that touches lava breaks almost at once, so you can get in and get it back without switching to an
  axe.
- Breaking the boat with an axe and picking it up lets you carry it on.

## How to practice

1. On ocean seeds, always cross water by boat and pick the boat back up on shore.
2. Practice getting in and breaking the boat quickly with your axe slot.
