The blacksmith's chest is where a village seed's iron comes from. Spotting the building from a
distance and looting it first decides how the rest of the seed goes.

## How it works

- Plains blacksmith: the only building with smooth-stone slabs on its roof.
- Acacia (savanna) blacksmith: covered in smooth-stone blocks, which no other village house has.
- Desert blacksmith: the only building made of stone, with cobblestone walls and iron bars. From one
  side it is hard to tell; learn its outline with the window on the left.
- Taiga and snowy villages have their own blacksmith variants.
- In Ranked the blacksmith is usually a weaponsmith. Desert and taiga villages can have a toolsmith
  instead, and snowy villages an armorer.
- After looting, count your iron first, including the golem's 4, and plan your crafts from that number.
- Put on any armor in the chest, unless it has Thorns or Curse of Binding.

## How to practice

1. Play village practice seeds and call out the blacksmith from spawn before you can read its chest.
2. Study the blacksmith shapes from every side in the house reference below.
