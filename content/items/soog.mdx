Soog, by doogile and silverrruns, is the advanced right-side bridge route. You work from on top of
the triple, aggro the left rampart's piglins early and end in an auto funnel, so you loot while they
arrive. It is fast and high-aggro; when you are low on blocks, play cracksmoker instead.

## How it works

- Get on top of the triple: place two blocks, triple stack (place one block before the jump, then
  jump and double stack), and go out three. Place blocks so hoglins can't climb up diagonally.
- Break a gold block near the left rampart to aggro its piglins. Give them a second, jump-throw a
  gold ingot, and build the next blocks. If they get too close, turn around and throw gold.
- Bridge across as in cracksmoker, using the L-shaped ledge to land your blocks, then drop down to
  let the lower piglins up.
- Throw your gold into the trading hole, dig your own spot (down one, down two, down two, forward
  two), place two blocks above you and punch a piglin.
- Loot the chests while they funnel in, then pillar up one, mine the block above you, throw the
  rest of the gold in and grab the other chest.
- The hardest part is mining the basalt while the piglins chase you.
- Calisthenicuber's guide teaches the safe version ("safe soog").

## How to practice

1. Be consistent at cracksmoker first.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, drill the triple stack and the L-shape placement alone.
3. Run it at full speed; it doesn't work slowly, because the piglins are on you.
