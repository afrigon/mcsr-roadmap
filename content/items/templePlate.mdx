The faster basement blast: place the pressure plate straight down first. The TNT lights much earlier
than in the overhead method, and it explodes below the floor, so you get more cobblestone.

## How it works

- Go down the same way, but place the pressure plate on the floor first to trigger the trap.
- Then break the blocks around the TNT that's lit, and get clear.
- Because the blast happens below the floor, more of the floor turns into cobblestone you can collect.
- Collect the loot and the leftover TNT as usual.

## How to practice

1. Switch to this order on desert temple practice seeds once the overhead method is safe.
2. Compare how much cobblestone you get with each method.
