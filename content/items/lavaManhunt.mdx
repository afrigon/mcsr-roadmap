Lava housing is a housing where lava flows down through the bastion. Most players don't know a route
for it, so knowing one wins seeds your opponent gives up on. Calisthenicuber notes that Ranked's seed
filter does not exclude lava housing, so it can happen at any Elo. There is a manhunt version and a
top-down version.

## How it works

- **Lava manhunt**: start as in normal manhunt, then follow a fixed set of blocks that keeps the
  lava off your path. Loot the chests, go through the wall, open the hole, and mine and throw gold
  as usual. Calisthenicuber gets about 11 piglins, sometimes 14–15, and suggests a boat for the
  descent.
- **Lava top-down**: loot the chests, break the single chest and punch a piglin, go down the
  rampart, and mine through to the gold. Craft ingots, throw them straight up and place a block
  immediately: the block elevates the ingots to the piglins above, so they trade while you keep
  mining.
- Both routes depend on exact block placements; watch the videos at reduced speed.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, set up a lava housing.
2. Learn the lava manhunt first; it is the one you will meet most when entering from below.
3. Practice the ingot throw-and-place until no gold falls back to you.
