Big Bertha is the large ruined portal with a stone-brick pillar and up to 6 gold blocks. It is
completable, but the gold is the trap: take only what's exposed.

## How it works

- Recognise it by its size and the stone-brick pillar.
- Gold can be at the top, two at the edge, one under stone bricks and two in the lava. In a race,
  take only the exposed gold.
- Gold blocks need an iron pickaxe.
- The reference completion takes about 9.4 seconds without crying obsidian; with crying obsidian it is
  completed using the lava pools.

## How to practice

1. Copy the reference completion in a practice map.
