You often reach a bridge bastion from the low terrain rather than at the chalice. The lower bridge
is also safer: hoglins can spawn on the upper bridge, never the lower one. This page covers getting
in from below and keeping the piglins you pass.

## How it works

- Short on blocks? A hallway leads down from the main part to the lower bridge.
- From the bottom terrain on the side opposite the chalice, run along the lower part, which pulls a
  few extra piglins. Make a staircase up and block both sides of it, or the piglins push each other
  off trying to reach you.
- TNT the chalice as usual, then let your piglins up and continue the route.
- From the other bottom approach, head-hitter jumps take you to a blackstone block; pillar from it,
  staircase up, then place two blocks on one side and one on the other.
- On a buried bridge, go to the second chunk in front of the main part and dig down at roughly chunk
  coordinates 8, 8: you land on top of the chalice.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, enable terrain and start from the bottom.
2. Learn which blocks you need on each side of the staircase so no piglin falls.
