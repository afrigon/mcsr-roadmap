Riding a boat down a slope or off a ledge cancels fall damage. Getting out and straight back in
mid-descent gets you noticeably farther than riding it down normally, and it is something you see
top runners do on almost every nether descent.

## Inputs

- While the boat is falling, sneak to get out, then immediately right-click the boat to get back in,
  holding W throughout.
- The higher the drop, the bigger the difference.

## Why it works

Getting into a boat resets the boat's momentum (Mojira MC-174877). The speedrun rulesets list this as
an allowed glitch, "boat velocity reset". Exactly why it gains distance isn't documented beyond that.
