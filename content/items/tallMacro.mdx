Measuring an eye of ender precisely means seeing its pixels, which a normal window shows far too small. A tall-resolution macro stretches the window to an extreme height and an eye zoom overlay magnifies the area around the crosshair, so you can line up on a single pixel.

## How it works

- The rules allow one window size outside your monitor's bounds, up to 16384 pixels. Eye measuring uses it: a window 16384 pixels tall.
- Toolscreen's EyeZoom mode (Ctrl+I opens its settings) does the resize and shows a magnified strip with pixel offset numbers on each side.
- The usual measure: throw an eye, look at it at its peak, pause, set FOV 30, press the eye zoom key, and line the crosshair up with the edge between the left and middle pixels of the pupil. Note which offset number lines up, press F3+C, then press Ninjabrain Bot's +1 or −1 pixel hotkey that many times.
- Set your mouse feel with Toolscreen's sensitivity slider, never with the in-game sensitivity: Ninjabrain Bot's precise measurements depend on the in-game value staying exactly what you entered in the bot. Toolscreen has a separate sensitivity for eye zoom.
- Turn FOV and the window back to normal after measuring.

## How to practice

1. Use MiniPracticeKit's nether exit split to throw and measure eyes repeatedly.
2. Check each answer with the locate command in practice worlds until your first measurement is reliably right.
