Every Ranked ruined portal seed can be entered, but there are two ways: complete the ruined portal's
own frame, or use the bucket to cast a portal from lava. Always check the existing portal first.

## How it works

- Ranked splits these seeds about 80/20 between obsidian enters and bucket enters.
- The general completion method: put a placeholder block (planks) where obsidian is missing, pour water
  against it, and cast the gap with lava. Use the portal's own lava pools when it has them; scoop them
  first.
- There are 10 small and 3 large ruined portal shapes. Each has a known fastest completion, covered in
  the items that follow.
- Crying obsidian in the frame breaks the portal, but most types can still be completed.
- When it can't be completed, use its obsidian in your own frame, or build a lava portal nearby.
- A light is always guaranteed: flint and steel, a fire charge, or flint with extra nuggets.

## How to practice

1. Learn to name each portal type at a glance.
2. Practice completions in a practice map, both with and without crying obsidian.
