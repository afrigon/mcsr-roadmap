A blaze bed clears the spawner with an exploding bed: beds explode when used in the nether. Two beds clear far more than one, so when you have them, this is the bed setup to use.

## How it works

- Shield the spawner with hard blocks (nether brick), then place two beds above it:
  "hard block, hard block, bed, bed". Calisthenicuber's order from the back: block, bed, pick
  block, block, bed.
- Detonate both beds, then **put out every fire**. Bed explosions leave fire; TNT does not.
- Almost nothing is left to mine. Build the soul sand platform.
- Calisthenicuber measured about 95% available space for two beds, against about 90% for one bed
  after the fire is out.
- JackoWacko62 adds 3 blocks at the front, placed while moving, so the cleared floor is level.
- Village seeds and bastion string make getting two beds easy.

## How to practice

1. In Mescht's Blaze Practice map, give yourself 2 beds and nether bricks.
2. Copy the setup from the clips below, detonate, and put out all fire.
3. Repeat on open and buried spawners until setup and cleanup flow without pauses.
