When parts of a bastion are buried in netherrack, you can't see where to go in. Memorized dig spots and pearl lineups get you to the chests and gold without searching. Ranked guarantees open terrain from the nether entry, but ramparts can still be buried.

## How it works

- **Buried triple rampart** (any type): dig in on the 7th block out from the middle part of the rampart.
- **Buried Stables with no information**: check to the right. If there is terrain, you are in the right rampart.
- **Lower Treasure**: the bridge entrance is at Y68 and Y73 is the last useful entrance. The lower back has brick squares; dig in there to reach the inner paths up.
- **Bridge from the top**: three ways to the front, between the "eyes" or through either "nostril".
- **Housing pearl, gold area to the top triple**: crouch to the edge of the black zone, look straight up, jump and throw while holding forward.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, turn terrain on and spawn buried.
2. Practice each dig spot and lineup until you hit it first try, then use it in full routes.
