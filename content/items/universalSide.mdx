A universal side setup stands far enough from the crystal to work on several tall towers at once:
T-94, T-97 and T-100. One standing block covers three towers and four heights.

## How it works

- Stand on 28 −30 front or −29 29 back, at y86, 90, 95 or 99.
- These are the multiblock-adjusted standing blocks. doogile's video shows 28 −31 / −29 30, the
  plain stand; his pinned comment gives the multiblock one.
- Bed order depends on the rotation.
- Back universal is shown with a bow in the example clips.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set T-94, T-97 and T-100 in turn and keep the same standing block.
2. Start at y95 front, then add back and the other heights.
