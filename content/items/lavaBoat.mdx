Lava boating is the trick from Dream's 2020 manhunts: cross lava on boats that burn under you. It is
fun to try but not used in Ranked routes.

## How it works

- Place a boat on lava, hop in, place the next boat ahead before the one under you burns, and repeat.
  Baablu did it with only two boats by recycling them, which is much harder than it looks.
- Related tricks that runners do use:
  - **Lava boat clutch**: landing in a boat on lava cancels fall damage. This is the sticky boat
    clutch, an allowed glitch.
  - **Fire boat**: light a boat for one tick with flint and steel (right-click, then left-click
    immediately). It survives 8 fire hits and falls slower, giving up to about 20 extra blocks from
    drops of 70 or more. It is an allowed glitch for crossing nether lava lakes from a height, but
    2 to 5 s slower than a normal boat drop.
