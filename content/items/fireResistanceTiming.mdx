Some seeds give you only one or two fire resistance potions from bartering. Ranked guarantees
obsidian and pearl trades, not fire resistance, so both players face the same shortage. Save the
potions for the fortress, where blazes deal mostly fire damage.

## How it works

- **The fortress is the priority**: under fire resistance, blaze fireballs do no damage. Spend the
  scarce potions there, not on the way.
- **Travel without it**: cross lava with blocks or a boat instead of drinking a potion. Avoid lava
  routes, such as a lava-flooded bastion, when you are short.
- **Drink as late as possible**: drink the first potion only when the first blaze is about to shoot
  at you, so its whole duration covers the spawner fight.
- **Fort without fire resistance**: hold a shield in the offhand. Blazes fire in bursts of three, so
  block the burst and attack between bursts. An enchanted golden apple also gives fire resistance.
- **Clearing the spawner is a trade-off**: clearing it spawns more blazes, which is faster but means
  more fire coming at you.

## How to practice

1. In blaze practice, leave the bastion with one potion and drink it only at the spawner.
2. Do a few spawner fights with no potion and a shield, and learn the three-shot rhythm.
3. Keep the potion in a fixed hotbar slot so the late drink takes no thought.
