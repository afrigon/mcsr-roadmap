The dragon doesn't always come down exactly on the node. A multiblock setup has two bed positions with
a standing block diagonal to both, so you can choose the bed that matches the dragon and keep the
damage consistent.

## How it works

- The standing block sits diagonally between two possible bed spots.
- Which bed you use depends on the rotation, so you have to memorise the bed side per rotation for
  each setup.
- Many side and universal setups have a multiblock-adjusted standing block. The universal side's
  28 −30 / −29 29, for example, is the multiblock stand; 28 −31 / −29 30 is the plain one.
- pion's catalogue lists multiblock variants for most towers.

## How to practice

1. Pick one setup you already hit, learn its multiblock variant, and drill both rotations.
