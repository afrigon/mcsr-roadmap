Back dragons hit hard, and a low-health back zero can kill you. Eating during the zero keeps it alive
instead of forcing a bail.

## How it works

- Couriway's guide calls a low-health back zero survivable "if you eat food, use bow, or have good
  armor".
- Eat between rotations, when no bed is due.
- Food in the offhand lets you eat without changing hotbar slots.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, lower your saturation, start back zeros and eat between rotations without
   losing bed timing.
