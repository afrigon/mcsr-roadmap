Tall Boy is too tall for a normal setup on top. The standard way to play it is a side setup: you
build on the side of the tower, below the crystal, so the bed can't reach it.

## How it works

| Node | Standing | Heights |
| ---- | -------- | ------- |
| Front | 29 −29 | 90, 94 |
| Back | −30 28 | 90, 94 |

- doogile's overlay shows y90, 95 and 99; his pinned correction gives 90 and 94.
- On side setups the bed order depends on the rotation, so read clockwise or counter-clockwise
  during the pearl.
- Side setups leave a lot of spare time: about 12 s front and 11 s back at y94 on an open spawn for
  a top runner.
- Without a bow, side setups can need more than five explosives.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set Tall Boy and alternate rotations at y94, front and back.
