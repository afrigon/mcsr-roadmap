When you don't have enough obsidian for a blind portal, go back out through the portal you entered
the nether with and find the stronghold from the overworld instead.

## How it works

- Return through your home portal and measure eyes in the overworld next to it.
- Then travel to the stronghold: in the overworld if it is close or you have no obsidian left, or
  back through the nether with a new portal at the stronghold's nether coordinates if you can build
  one.
- A diamond pickaxe lets you mine obsidian back out of a portal you no longer need.
- This is more consistent than a blind portal but usually slower, which is why it is the fallback
  and not the default.

## How to practice

1. When a practice run leaves the bastion with under 10 obsidian, take the home portal on purpose
   and finish the run.
2. Compare the measured distance with what a blind would have given you.
