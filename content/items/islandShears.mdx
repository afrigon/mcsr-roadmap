With shears, leaves become instant building blocks, and the island's wood only has to cover a boat,
doors and beds. With 9 or more iron, shears beat every other extra tool.

## How it works

- With iron to spare after pickaxe, bucket and flint and steel, shears are better than an iron axe.
  Shears and a shovel is a good use of 4 spare iron.
- Shear about a stack of leaves, more if they come quickly.
- Take about 8–9 logs: the wood only goes into doors, a boat and beds.
- Shear leaves while you chop, and pick them up as you go.
- Shear sheep on the island for wool, which means free beds.
- You can leave the shears on the island. If you die, you can come back for more leaves.
- Skip shears when you have TNT: blow up the trees instead.

## How to practice

1. On ocean practice seeds, run the island routine with a timer: leaves, logs, crafting.
