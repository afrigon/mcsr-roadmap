A nether portal frame only becomes a portal once fire appears inside it. Flint and steel is the
usual light: it costs one iron ingot and one flint.

## How it works

- Craft flint and steel from one iron ingot and one flint. Flint comes from breaking gravel.
- Use it on any obsidian block on the inside of the finished frame.
- The frame has to be complete and the inside empty: remove leftover water and blocks first, or the
  portal won't form.
- Keep the flint and steel after lighting. It lights animals for cooked food and helps against
  hoglins and other mobs later.
- Fire charges from ruined portal chests light a portal the same way, but only once.
