Sometimes lava floods the tower you normally dig the trading hole in. The fix is to run the same
route mirrored and put the hole in the other tower.

## How it works

- Spot it early: lava pouring through the route between the towers means the normal path is
  blocked.
- Instead of crossing the lava, turn left, then right, then go up. Everything after that matches
  Safe Boomer.
- Connect the two towers as usual and dig the trading hole in the dry one. Mining through two
  blocks on the right side keeps piglins from walking into the lava on the way.
- Watch the flow: lava can run down into the trading hole. If you see it coming, use the other side.
- Expect fewer piglins on the flooded side. TNT treasure still works on the dry tower.

## How to practice

1. Learn Safe Boomer first; this page only changes where the hole goes.
2. In the LlamaPag map, fill one tower with lava in creative and walk the mirrored path until you
   don't have to think about the turns.
