The fewer end crystals are alive, the more likely the dragon is to perch. Shooting most of them, but
not all, shortens the average wait for a one cycle.

## How it works

- After each flight path, the dragon perches with a chance of 1 in (crystals + 3).
- Destroying a crystal triggers a strafe, so shoot them in quick succession.
- The common version is the Automatt half bow: pillar up next to your fountain obsidian and shoot
  from the middle.
- It needs good execution: done slowly, it is slower than just waiting (k4yfour).

## How to practice

1. In an End practice map, pillar beside the fountain and shoot crystals from there.
2. Time the whole sequence and compare it with a plain wait.
