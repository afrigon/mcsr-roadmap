Kill the dragon in three rotations, two of them with a double: 3+2. This is the realistic target for
most players: nearly as fast as 2+2 and much easier.

## How it works

- In N+M notation, N is the number of rotations used and M the number of rotations with two
  explosives.
- 3+2 combines a bow +1 double on two rotations with a single on the third.
- Learn it on the standard heights y90, y95 and y99.

## How to practice

1. Get bow +1 consistent on F m88 y95 first.
2. Add the second double, then move the same pattern to y90 and y99.
