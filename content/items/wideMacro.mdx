Nether fog hides anything more than a short distance away, including bastions and fortresses you could have run straight to. A wide-resolution macro stretches the game window into a wide strip, and the edges of that strip show terrain past the fog.

## How it works

- A macro tool resizes the Minecraft window on a hotkey. Window resizing is one of the two macro types the rules allow.
- Minecraft's fog is planar: it depends on distance along your view direction, so at the far sides of a very wide window you see terrain much farther away than in the middle. Planar fog abuse is an allowed glitch.
- Look toward where a structure might be and press the hotkey: a bastion or fortress at the screen edge becomes visible. Press again to return to normal.
- Tools: Toolscreen (Windows) has a Wide mode, and Waywall (Linux) handles resizing through its config.
- Use it on nether entry and while travelling, alongside your other structure-finding methods.

## How to practice

1. Install your tool and bind the wide macro to an easy key.
2. Load a nether in the practice map or MiniPracticeKit, turn slowly with the macro on and spot every structure you can.
3. Use it at every nether entry in real runs until it is a habit.
