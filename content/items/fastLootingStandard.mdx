The version most runners use: four quick inputs while holding Shift loot the whole chest. It is
more consistent than shift-click spam.

## How it works

- Hold Shift over a stack, then press the offhand key, left-click, the offhand key again and
  left-click again, quickly.
- A hotbar key can stand in for the offhand key.
- It works with a full hotbar, so it is the method for bastion chests too.

## How to practice

1. Open a regenerating chest in a practice map and repeat the four inputs until the chest clears
   every time.
2. Practise on a chest whose loot mixes many item types, like a buried treasure or bastion chest.
