With 10 obsidian you need no lava at all: stack the blocks into a frame and light it. Taiga villages
in Ranked always offer an enter with 10+ obsidian, and blacksmiths and ruined portals can hold enough
too.

## How it works

- The smallest nether portal is 4 wide and 5 tall. Its corners don't need to be obsidian, so the frame
  takes 10 blocks: 2 on the bottom, 3 up each side, 2 on top.
- Use any filler block to stand on or hold the frame in place, then mine it away.
- Light it with flint and steel.
- Count the obsidian before committing. A blacksmith with 8 or 9 obsidian still needs lava for the
  rest.

## How to practice

1. Build the frame on flat ground in a test world until the placement order is automatic.
2. Practice building it against a wall or on a slope, where you have to place filler first.
