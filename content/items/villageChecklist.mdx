A village seed has many things to pick up, and forgetting one sends you back across the village. A
fixed checklist keeps the route short and the nether enter complete.

## How it works

Leave the village with:

- a bucket and a pickaxe (from the blacksmith and the golem),
- at least 10 logs,
- 2 beds,
- about 7 hay bales.

Seven hay bales craft into 63 wheat, which stays in one stack and is easy to turn into bread. Some
runners take around 9 bales. Beginners can take more food and logs: there's nothing wrong with that.

On the way out you also need flint and a lava pool, so look for gravel and fire while you loot.

## How to practice

1. Play village practice seeds and check the list before you leave the village every time.
2. Note which item you forget most often and grab it earlier in your route.
