Bank Heist is the right-side triple-to-chalice (TTC) route: aggro the right triple's piglins, the
side with the area called the bank, and lead them to the chalice to trade. Subway is a competing
route for the same case.

## How it works

- Build the bridge first, then pillar up three blocks from the hole so you can loot the double chest
  safely, or throw a distraction gold.
- Break the single chest and punch a piglin. Punch without a weapon, which can affect the piglin's
  drops.
- Block the alley with four blocks and place one behind you. Mine four blocks and break the one
  behind you to let the piglins up.
- Dig down, place a block above you and punch again to re-aggro. Mine down two, then break the dirt
  block to let the piglins down; toggling to Hard lets the ones behind you drop further.
- At the chalice, block the wrong hole, build the auto funnel and mine the chalice, throwing ingots
  down as you go.
- One extra block on either TTC route keeps hoglins off you; kill them with lava before collecting.

## How to practice

1. Be consistent at an auto-funnel route first.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, set the right rampart to a triple and copy the route.
