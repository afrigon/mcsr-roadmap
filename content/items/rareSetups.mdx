Some runs give you an odd mix of explosives: one TNT, or a TNT and a bed. JackoWacko62's video covers a setup for each combination, so you never have to improvise at the spawner.

## How it works

| Explosives | Setup | Notes |
| ---------- | ----- | ----- |
| 1 TNT | Option 1: TNT behind the spawner, mine the rest | Faster, clears less. Use when you already have rods. |
| 1 TNT | Option 2: a nether brick fence holds the TNT at the right height | Open and buried versions. Hug the spawner during the blast to take the least damage. |
| 1 TNT + 1 bed | TNT first, bed second | Light the TNT and craft during the fuse, then place the bed. |
| 1 TNT + 1 bed, bed uncrafted | Crafting table beside the TNT | Craft the bed during the fuse (Zuzurie's overlap). |
| 2 TNT + 1 bed | TNT on both sides, bed above | Buried spawners only (DanielH212). On an open one, 1 TNT + 1 bed is already enough. |

- Bed setups leave fire; put it out. TNT setups leave none.
- In crossroads198x's timings, 1 TNT + 1 bed setups were among the fastest open setups to reach
  90% available space.

## How to practice

1. In Mescht's Blaze Practice map, learn one row of the table per session.
2. Build each setup on both open and buried spawners where it applies.
3. Check the available space overlay against the main setups to see what each mix gives up.
