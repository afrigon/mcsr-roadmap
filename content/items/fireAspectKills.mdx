Ruined portal seeds don't always come with food in the chest. Ranked makes up for it with nearby
animals and a way to cook them: flint and steel or a Fire Aspect sword.

## How it works

- A Fire Aspect sword sets the animal on fire with each hit, so the drops come out cooked, just like
  lighting it with flint and steel.
- A burning animal still runs for water. Finish it quickly.
- With flint and steel, use the same kills as on other seeds: light, then axe; or one axe hit, then one
  tick of fire.
- Take enough: around 11–12 food is a comfortable amount on these seeds.
- A gold sword with Looting is worth keeping for later in the run.

## How to practice

1. On ruined portal practice seeds, check the chest for food before deciding whether to hunt.
