Putting the same item in the same slot every run lets your hands select it without looking. There
is no standard layout; what matters is that yours never changes.

## How it works

- Give each recurring item a fixed slot: pickaxe, axe, blocks, food, bucket, flint and steel,
  pearls.
- Put the items you switch to under pressure (blocks, bucket, pearls) on the easiest keys.
- When you pick up a tool, move it to its slot right away with a hotbar key.
- MiniPracticeKit lets you save a hotbar and load it in practice worlds, so you can rehearse with
  the real layout.

## How to practice

1. Write down your layout and set it up in MiniPracticeKit.
2. Play a few practice runs and correct every item that ends up out of place before moving on.
