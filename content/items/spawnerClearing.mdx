Blazes spawn in a box around the spawner and need empty space with low light. A spawner left as generated has only about half of that space available, so clearing it is the biggest speed-up at the fortress.

## How it works

- Every solid block or fire in the spawn box takes away spawn spots. One fire near an untouched
  spawner took it from about 49% to 11% available in Calisthenicuber's test.
- Soul fire does not block spawns. Normal fire does: put out every fire, including fireballs blazes
  shoot onto the platform.
- With a pickaxe: break the blocks next to the spawner first, then work outward (k4yfour). After an
  explosion, Calisthenicuber mines two blocks out the back and one down the sides.
- Beds and TNT clear faster than mining. See the setup skills below this one.
- In Ranked, a spawn spot blocked by a block, fire, an entity or you loses that blaze, and both
  players get the same spawn attempts. Clearing quality is a direct difference between you and your
  opponent.

## How to practice

1. Use Mescht's Blaze Practice map and turn on the available and blocked spawn space overlays.
2. Clear a spawner with a pickaxe and check the percentage.
3. Repeat until you reach over 90% without looking at the overlay.
