Switching between two different items while mining restarts the block's break progress. It mostly
bites on the first logs of the run.

## How it works

- Swapping between two separate items resets the progress. Swapping between an empty hand and
  another empty slot does not.
- Punching logs with slot 1 selected: the first log goes into slot 1, which changes what is in
  your hand, and the second log can restart.
- Select an empty slot that isn't slot 1 before punching the first tree.

## How to practice

1. At spawn in practice worlds, select slot 2 before punching your first tree, every time.
