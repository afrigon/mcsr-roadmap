A pearl clip takes you through the housing wall on your way out, as a faster exit than walking
around. Pearl clipping is allowed under the run rules.

## How it works

- Throw the pearl so it lands at the wall and clips you through; copy the exact spot from the clip
  below.
- You can do it without breaking the blackstone there, but breaking it first makes the clip much
  more consistent.
- Some housing generations have no blocks where you come out, so be ready to block clutch (place a
  block to catch yourself).
