When the dragon comes in lower than your bed, the explosion misses most of its hitbox and the damage
drops. Move the bed down to meet it.

## The save

- Place the bed 1–2 blocks lower, beside the obsidian, instead of in its usual spot.
- Or drop down and bed where you were standing.
- Bed later than usual on a low dragon: a late bed gives more knockback, which pushes it back up for
  the next pass.

## When it's not enough

From low heights the dragon flies away more often, and a zero that keeps coming in low usually
ends as a 4+2 or 5+2 instead. Take that and finish the kill.
