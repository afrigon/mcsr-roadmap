Wither skeletons cannot fit through a 2-block-high gap, but you can. In a hallway, one block in the right place lets you walk on while they stay behind.

## How it works

- Block either the top or the bottom of the passage so only a 2-high gap is left. You fit through;
  withers do not.
- Hoglins and normal skeletons still fit, so this only stops withers.
- A small wall across a closed hallway works the same way.
- Calisthenicuber often prefers building a short pathway past the withers to killing them.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, switch to survival in a fortress hallway with withers following you.
2. Block the passage behind you in one placement and keep walking.
3. Practice with both a ceiling block and a floor block.
