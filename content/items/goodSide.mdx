A nether fortress has two kinds of sections, and blaze spawners only generate in one of them. If you can tell the sides apart, you never waste time searching hallways that cannot hold a spawner.

## How it works

- **Good side**: open nether brick bridges, or the same bridges running through terrain with a
  netherrack roof. Blaze spawners generate here.
- **Bad side**: enclosed hallways with a nether brick roof, chests and nether wart rooms. It has no
  spawners.
- When the fortress is open you can usually see the good side from outside. Pearl straight to it.
- When you arrive in the bad side with no view outside, follow the hallway rules from the next
  skill to reach the good side.
- Once on the good side, head down: k4yfour notes spawners are most common at the lowest level.
  Rarely, a spawner sits on a balcony off the hallways.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, use `/locate fortress` on a few seeds and fly to each fortress.
2. Before you land, name the side you are looking at and where the bridges lead.
3. Repeat until you can spot the good side from a distance in every nether biome.
