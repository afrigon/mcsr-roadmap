PvP players steer knockback on purpose, and the same trick lets you send a blaze back over the
platform instead of off the edge. It comes from PvP; no speedrun guide covers it yet.

## The mechanic

- The direction of knockback follows the direction you are looking at the moment of the hit, not
  only where you stand.
- The steerable part is the extra knockback from a sprint hit or a Knockback weapon; a plain hit
  pushes straight away from you.
- So you turn toward where the blaze should fly, then flick back onto it and hit. The turn has to
  happen before the hit lands; turning after the hit does nothing.

## Getting it consistent

Practice on a mob in an open area first: sprint-hit while looking left or right of it and watch which
way it goes, then move to blazes on a platform.
