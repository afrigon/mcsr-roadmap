The Crispy hole is an auto-funnel trading hole (late piglins path into it by themselves) for the
good gap, on either side. It replaces the BeefSalad hole: trades start and finish sooner, and the
hole sits in a better spot for a pearl hang. This page covers Variation 2, the default; Variation 1
is on the Hard page.

## How it works

- Hole by CrispLake, route by Lucento, with help from Pavkin.
- It works with TNT; see CrispyTNT.
- Lucento's info dump covers the decisions: which gap piglins to handle and how, blocking off late
  piglins, the order of the four gold blocks and where to throw them, and pushing trades.
- If the hole goes wrong, Pavkin's backup route and the L-gap route save the bastion.

## How to practice

1. Learn the classic gap blocks first.
2. In the LlamaPag map, copy the Variation 2 showcase until the hole is right every time.
3. Then watch the info dump and add one preference at a time.
