Opening the recipe book shifts your inventory so the cursor lands right on its top-left slot. That
slot works like an extra hotbar slot that is one keypress away.

## How it works

- With the recipe book open, opening the inventory puts the mouse over the top-left inventory slot.
- Press a hotbar key right away to swap that slot's item into your hotbar.
- Runners keep fire charges or a flint and steel there, but any item you need now and then works.
- The recipe book has to stay open in the inventory screen for the cursor to line up.

## How to practice

1. Put a flint and steel in the top-left slot, close the inventory, then open it and swap it to your
   hand in one motion.
2. Use it every time you light a portal until it is automatic.
