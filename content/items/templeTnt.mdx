Blowing up the temple basement opens all four chests at once and leaves a pile of cobblestone, so
the blast is both the loot and your stone. This overhead version is the one most runners learn
first.

## How it works

- After taking the pressure plate, mine down, go back up, and set off a TNT above your head while you
  hide underneath.
- The explosion breaks the chests and spills their items. Collect everything, including the leftover
  TNT. Break the final TNT block once you are back up, or it can still blow up under you.
- Pillar out. Top runners drop junk while pillaring: offhand your blocks, pillar with the offhand, and
  hotkey to the items you want to throw.

## How to practice

1. Do the full basement routine on desert temple practice seeds until the TNT never hurts you.
2. Practise pillaring while dropping items on flat ground first.
