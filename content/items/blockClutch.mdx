A block clutch is a pearl thrown at the side of a wall, followed by a block placed under you the moment
you land, so you stay on the wall instead of falling. It is how you reach the top of a fortress, and
it comes back in zero-cycle setups.

## How it works

- A pearl doesn't change which way you face. Face the wall's direction when the pearl lands, so you
  can place against it. This matters when you release a pearl hang.
- Jump just before the pearl lands. You keep that upward momentum after the teleport, which gives you
  more time to place.
- Look down and slightly toward the wall, not straight down, or you can't place against it.
- Mikeyboie holds crouch and back and spams right click as he lands.

## How to practice

1. Pearl onto a fortress wall in a practice world and block clutch every time.
2. Practice in a zero-cycle practice map too, where pearling, block clutching and pillaring come
   together.
