Fast looting empties a whole chest in a couple of inputs instead of one click per stack. It is an
allowed glitch under the speedrun rules, and the simplest version only uses Shift and the mouse.

## How it works

- Hold Shift and click a stack, release, then shift-click again in quick succession. Done fast
  enough, this pulls the rest of the chest into your inventory.
- It takes everything, junk included, so throw away what you don't need afterwards.
- It is simple but less consistent than the offhand method in Fast Looting (Hotkey); move on to
  that once this works for you.

## How to practice

1. Loot the same chest repeatedly in a practice map that regenerates loot, and count how many
   attempts clear it in one go.
2. Keep the rhythm even rather than as fast as possible.
