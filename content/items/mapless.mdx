Buried treasure is one of the fastest seed types, and in Ranked you find it without a map. The pie
chart tells you which chunk holds the chest, and the chest is always at the same spot inside that
chunk.

## How it works

- Lower render distance (around 5–8) and entity distance (50%), turn off entity culling, and turn on
  chunk borders (F3+G).
- Open the pie chart (Shift+F3) at `root.gameRenderer.level.entities`. The orange block entities slice
  spikes when a chest is on screen.
- Basic method: turn until it spikes, then scan from the other side, so the chest is between those
  two screen edges.
- Walk into the candidate chunk and check all four directions. If it spikes in all four, you are in the
  right chunk.
- Optimal method: use a thin window. The spike appears the moment you cross the chest's chunk border,
  so you can read the chunk directly.
- Go to chunk coordinates 9, 9 (shown in F3) and dig down.
- Raise your settings again afterwards for the ravine search.

## How to practice

1. Use a practice seed and repeat the scan until you pick the right chunk first time.
2. Learn the difference between a chest spike and a spawner spike.
