The shift clip replaces building up with sneaking: at O57 you mine one block further instead of
building, then sneak before the pearl. It is the version that scales to deeper spawns.

## How it works

- **O57 shift clip**: mine 4, sneak, pearl, jump.
- Sneaking too early is one of the common fails; watch the clip below.
- The shift clip is a pearl clip, not the shift clutch: the shift clutch (cancelling fall damage by
  sneaking) is banned, pearl clipping is allowed.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set O57 and alternate the normal clip and the shift clip until the shift
   clip is as reliable.
