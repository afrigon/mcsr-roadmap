A boat turns nether drops and walls into shortcuts. Landing in a boat negates fall damage, and a boat
lets you sprint-crawl through netherrack faster than mining a normal tunnel.

## How it works

- **Bring one**: nether wood can't make a boat, so craft it in the overworld, whatever the seed type.
  You will need it later for boat eye measuring anyway.
- **Big drops**: ride the boat off a ledge instead of building down or taking the damage.
- **Lava lakes**: Mikeyboie recommends the boat for getting across large lava lakes; keep fire
  resistance ready in case you end up in the lava.
- **Boat crawl through a wall**:
  1. Mine a 2-long, 1-high slot into the wall.
  2. Place the boat facing the slot, get in, hold forward and sprint.
  3. At the wall, press sneak while still holding sprint. You leave the boat crawling, and still
     sprinting.
  4. Mine the floor block first; hitting the boat with a fully charged axe swing cancels the sprint.
  5. Keep mining straight ahead. Jump to check that you are still sprinting.

## How to practice

1. On practice seeds, find a tall drop and boat off it until getting in at the edge is quick.
2. Boat-crawl 20 blocks into netherrack and race a plain 2×1 tunnel.
