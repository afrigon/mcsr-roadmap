The hardest save in a zero: detonate an explosive and place the next one in the same spot on the same
tick. It keeps a zero going when there's no block left to target for the next explosive.

## The move

- Aim at the block holding your current bed or anchor, with the next one in hand.
- Detonate it and place the next explosive within the same game tick, so the placement uses the
  block that's about to disappear.
- If the last explosion left fire, Fire Place does the same job with far looser timing.

## When you need it

A zero gone wrong, usually after a misplaced block or a bed break, with the dragon still coming in
and nothing to place against.
