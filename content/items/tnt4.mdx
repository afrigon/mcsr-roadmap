The best 4-TNT setup, Fyroah's, is the default on a buried spawner when you have 4 or more TNT. It clears more than the donut, but the original version can destroy the spawner about once in 360 tries, so learn it with Daniel_H212's fix.

## How it works

- Order: **both side TNT first, then the rear, then the front.**
- Why it can fail: lit TNT jumps a little in a random direction. If both side TNT jump backward
  and the rear one jumps sideways, the blasts push the rear TNT past its nether brick and it
  breaks the spawner. Daniel measured 28 failures in 10,000 tries.
- **Fix B (recommended)**: add a block that stops a side TNT bouncing backward. One side is enough,
  since the failure needs both to jump back. It clears as well as the original.
- **Fix A**: soul sand beside the rear TNT so it cannot drift. Keep the soul sand on the side of
  the first side TNT you light; only the other one can be left out.
- If you light the sides one at a time, light them within half a second of each other.

## How to practice

1. In Mescht's Blaze Practice map, build Fyroah's setup with Fix B from Daniel's video.
2. Say the order out loud while lighting: sides, rear, front.
3. Practice on a buried spawner until the build is as fast as 3 TNT.
