When a zero or one cycle leaves the dragon on low health and it flies away, you don't have to wait
for the next perch. Chase it and finish it in the air.

## How it works

- Shoot the dragon with your bow while it flies.
- Shoot the end crystals that are healing it; a dragon being healed by a crystal undoes your damage.
- If the chase fails, fall back to shooting the remaining crystals and waiting for a perch.

## How to practice

1. In the zero practice map, deliberately leave the dragon low and let it fly off.
2. Practice spotting the healing beam and shooting that crystal first.
