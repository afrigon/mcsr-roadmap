Withers that arrive while you wait at the spawner are dangerous and waste time. On an open bridge, a line of blocks across it keeps them from reaching you.

## How it works

- Dig up two blocks on one side of the bridge and build a line of blocks all the way across. The
  withers cannot walk through it.
- Calisthenicuber blocks the hallway entrances to the spawner area before they become a problem.
- Mikeyboie runs past withers he has aggroed and builds a barrier across their path.
- Do it while waiting for a blaze wave, so it costs no extra time.

## How to practice

1. In a blaze practice map or a creative world, clear a spawner and build the line across the
   bridge leading to it.
2. Spawn or lure withers and check that none get through.
3. Time the build and fit it into one wave's wait.
