Gravel and sand that generate in the air stay floating until a block update reaches them. Placing a
block against the side of floating gravel is enough: it falls straight down, onto you if you are
underneath.

## Where it bites

- Nether gravel and overworld cave ceilings, when you bridge or pillar next to them.
- It can cover a hole you meant to drop into, or bury you under a column of gravel.

## Using it

The same update can be used on purpose: place a block beside floating gravel to bring it down where
you want it, for example to fill a gap.
