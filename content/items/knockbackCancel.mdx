Setting yourself on fire just before a hit cancels its knockback, so a hoglin or an explosion doesn't
throw you off a bridge or into lava. It is used on bridge bastions and when mining the chalice with
TNT.

## How it works

- Light yourself on fire (flint and steel at your feet) shortly before the hit lands. The hit then
  pushes you nowhere.
- **Hoglins**: on a bridge bastion, light yourself just before a hoglin hits and run straight through
  it. This is faster than killing it with lava.
- **TNT**: light yourself shortly before your TNT explodes and walk toward it, so the explosion
  doesn't push you away.
- Fire still burns you. Put it out or keep fire resistance up.

## How to practice

1. On a bridge bastion in the MCSR Practice Map, let hoglins come at you and practice the timing.
2. Practice lighting yourself right before a TNT chalice explodes.
