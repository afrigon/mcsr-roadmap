A boat is a parachute you already carry. Ride it off a ledge, a ravine wall or a pillar and you land
without taking fall damage, even on dry ground, so you can drop straight down instead of
staircasing or clutching.

## How it works

- Place the boat at the edge, get in and drive it off. While you ride it, fall damage goes to the
  boat, and a boat cancels it for itself and its riders.
- The exception is a long-standing bug (MC-119369, present in 1.16.1): at a few exact fall heights
  the boat breaks on landing and the rider takes the full fall damage instead. The known heights
  are 12, 13, 49, 51, 111, 114, 198, 202, 310 and 315 blocks. A 49-block drop kills.
- The deadly heights are fixed, not a random chance; the risk comes from not knowing the exact
  height of the drop. Short drops and anything well away from those heights are safe.
- With a player inside, the 12-block case is reported not to trigger every time.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, drop a boat off pillars of different heights, including 12, 13 and 49, to
   see the break happen.
2. Practice placing the boat at an edge, getting in and driving off in one smooth motion.
