On shipwreck seeds the portal comes from a magma ravine, and Ranked's seed filter places ravines near
the wreck. Search where the filter searched.

## How it works

- Ranked guarantees two magma ravines within 10 chunks on shipwreck and buried treasure seeds, never in
  a frozen ocean.
- The filter starts its search at the shipwreck, so the ravine is very often in the same area.
- Raise render and entity distance while crafting on the island and look for floating kelp.
- Pick up gravel on the way: at the ravine floor in cold oceans, at the shore in warm ones.
- Check ravines from above in third person: wide, deep and with magma at the bottom.

## How to practice

1. On shipwreck practice seeds, look around the wreck first before searching further out.
