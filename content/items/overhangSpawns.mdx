On an overhang spawn, end stone hangs over the spawn platform and blocks the pearl you would normally
throw. Get clear of it before throwing.

## How it works

- Run to the back of the platform and pearl from there, or build up first.
- For a severe overhang, staircase out about 3 blocks before pearling.
- Treat an overhang that encloses the platform as a buried spawn and clip out.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set overhang spawns and practise both the run-back pearl and the build-up.
