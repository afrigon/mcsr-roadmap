When you are low on hunger at the fortress, build the blind earlier than usual, reset hunger on the
overworld side, then come back. You trade a little nether time for a full bar instead of eating.

## How it works

- Build the blind as soon as you have enough rods to be safe, rather than after the last blaze.
- On the overworld side, do the normal blind hunger reset with a bed and a 2×1 hole.
- Return to the nether to finish rods, or continue if you already have enough.
- Blinding with 5 rods is generally considered fine.

## How to practice

1. In blaze practice, blind early on purpose when hunger is low and compare against eating.
