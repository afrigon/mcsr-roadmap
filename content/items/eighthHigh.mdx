The higher 1/8 setup, at y97, gives the dragon more time on a straight node than y88 or y92. With a
bow it supports a 3+2 (three rotations, two of them with doubles).

## How it works

- Standing blocks are the same as the low 1/8: front 23 0, back −23 0 (some guides write −24 0).
- 1/8 heights differ from diagonal ones: y84, 88, 92/93, 97 and 102.
- y97 leaves less spare time than y92/93, about 6 s front and 5 s back on an open spawn for a top
  runner, against about 10 s and 9 s.
- Back 1/8 is precise above y92: small errors in your standing position cost damage.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set a 1/8 node and build to y97 front and back.
2. Add the bow +1 for the 3+2 once five singles are consistent.
