Buried fortresses often drop you into the hallways with no view of the bridges. A few rules about the bad side's rooms always point toward the good side, so you can navigate without guessing.

## How it works

| Room | Rule |
| ---- | ---- |
| Nether wart room with a staircase | Go down the stairs and straight through. Hax phrases it as going the opposite way. |
| Balcony | Go the opposite direction from the balcony. |
| Plain stairs | Always go up. |
| Lava-well room (one lava block in a well in the middle) | The transition: good side on one side, bad side on the other. |

- The rules only take you out of the bad side. On the good side, look low first, where spawners
  are most common.
- The MCSR wiki notes the lava-well room joins an intersection where spawners tend to generate.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, find a fortress with `/locate fortress` and start inside its hallways.
2. Walk to the good side using only the rules, saying each rule out loud as you apply it.
3. Repeat from different starting rooms until you reach the lava-well room without hesitating.
