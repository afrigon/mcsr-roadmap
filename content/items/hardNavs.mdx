Once normal strongholds are routine, the losses come from the rare ones. The hard navs spreadsheet
collects seeds with exactly those strongholds so you can meet them in practice first.

## How it works

- The sheet, compiled by Jahzz and shared on frontcage in September 2026, lists seeds with multiple
  spawners, deep or tricky navigations, portal rooms cut by caves, and spawners more than 64 blocks
  away. It also mixes in easier seeds so the set doesn't become predictable.
- Many seeds rely on zombie spawners, which Ranked replaces. Play them on an RSG (random seed
  glitchless) instance so they generate as listed.
- The sheet is view-only: make a copy, and read its info section before playing.

## How to practice

1. Copy the sheet and play its seeds in an RSG instance, from entry to portal room.
2. Do the preemptive read on every seed and note where it misled you.
3. Replay the seeds you failed until the trap is obvious.
