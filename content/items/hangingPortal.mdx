The Hanging Portal hangs from iron bars over a pool of lava. It needs four obsidian, and the pool
underneath supplies all of them.

## How it works

- It is the only small ruined portal that hangs from iron bars, with a slab overhang at the front.
- One gold block sits at the top and one in the lava. The chest is on the back side, opposite the
  overhang.
- Cast the four missing obsidian from its own lava pool.
- The reference completion takes about 6.6 seconds without crying obsidian and about 15 seconds with
  it.

## How to practice

1. Copy the reference completion in a practice map, then the crying-obsidian version.
