After the bastion, you need blaze rods from a fortress. Sometimes you can see it from the bastion,
which skips the search entirely, so look before reaching for the pie chart.

## How it works

- **What it looks like**: long dark-red nether brick bridges on tall pillars, plus roofed hallways.
  Fortresses can generate in any biome, basalt deltas included.
- **Where to look**: bastions and fortresses share structure regions, and each region holds only
  one of them. The fortress is never in the bastion's own region.
- **Ranked guarantee**: the fortress starts within 16 chunks of the intended bastion, with open
  terrain from the bastion toward it.
- **Look while you route**: check the horizon from the bastion's highest point while waiting on
  trades.
- **Reach the right side**: open bridges lead to blaze spawners; roofed hallways lead to chests.
- When nothing is in sight, use the pie chart (pie-ray) to get a direction.

## How to practice

1. On practice seeds, look around from the top of every bastion before leaving, and note whether the
   fortress was visible.
2. When you find a fortress, check which side you arrived on and walk to the nearest open bridge.
