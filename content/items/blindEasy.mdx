The blind portal is the first portal back to the overworld once you have your blaze rods, built
before throwing any eye. Where you build it decides how far you will travel afterwards.

## How it works

- Strongholds in the first ring sit 1408–2688 overworld blocks from 0, 0. One nether block equals 8
  overworld blocks.
- Ninjabrain Bot's best blind distance is about 226 nether blocks from 0, 0 (about 1808 overworld),
  measured in a straight line, not x plus z.
- Press F3+C in the nether with no eye entered. The bot gives a blind rating (excellent, good for
  highroll, okay for highroll, bad but in ring, bad, not in any ring), the chance of a stronghold
  within 400 blocks, and a direction to head for better coordinates.
- Build once the rating reads good or excellent.
- A full portal without corners takes 10 obsidian.

## How to practice

1. Press F3+C at the fortress in every practice run and read the rating before travelling.
2. Get used to travelling toward a good rating instead of building wherever you stand.
