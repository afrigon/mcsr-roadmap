The shipwreck itself is made of wood. Instead of swimming back to shore for logs, take the planks and
logs from the ship, and make an air bubble so you can craft and breathe down there.

## How it works

- Loot the iron chest, then take wood from the ship to make your crafting table and tools.
- On a tilted wreck there is no dry spot to stand in. If you have spare iron, craft an iron pressure
  plate and place it: it gives an air bubble you can swim into and crouch in.
- From there, craft and keep going without surfacing.

## How to practice

1. On shipwreck practice seeds with a far shore, try the full route from the wreck alone.
