When there is no lava pool on the surface near your route, there is often lava underground. Dig down
toward its sound and build the portal where you find it.

## How it works

- Turn subtitles on. They show not only that lava is nearby but also which direction the sound comes
  from.
- Dig toward it, and bring a water bucket down with you.
- Underground lava rarely has a clean shape. Mine out as few blocks as possible to recreate the setup
  you know from lava pools: an L of lava with room for the water to fall into the frame.
- A specific plan for each cave isn't worth learning: they are too random. Look for the nearest
  version of the familiar shape instead.

## How to practice

1. Leave subtitles on all the time, so lava sounds become familiar.
2. In a portal practice map, try the cave setups and look for the fewest blocks to mine.
