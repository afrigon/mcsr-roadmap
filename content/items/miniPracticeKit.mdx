MiniPracticeKit (MPK) drops you into realistic mid-run situations, such as entering the nether or leaving it with rods and pearls, with a preset hotbar and inventory. Repeating those splits with your own layout is how a consistent hotbar becomes muscle memory.

## How it works

- MPK is a single command block saved in a creative hotbar file, so it needs no mods.
- **Install**: close the game, back up any existing `hotbar.nbt` in your instance's `.minecraft` folder, and put the MPK `hotbar.nbt` there.
- **Load**: create a new creative world and load saved hotbar 1 (hold the load-hotbar key, X by default, and press 1).
- **Run a split**: drop the barrel for the split you want, then place the command block. It teleports you (to the nether, near a bastion or fortress, to the stronghold staircase) and gives you that split's items.
- **Customize a split**: place its barrel, take out the chest and white shulker box and place them. Chest items go into your inventory in exactly that order; shulker box items come in random order. Edit them, then Ctrl+pick block each container, put them back in the barrel, Ctrl+pick block the barrel, and save the hotbar (hold C, press 1).

## How to practice

1. Edit each split's chest so the items sit in the slots you use in real runs.
2. Run the nether exit split repeatedly to practice blind portals and eye measuring.
3. Rename split barrels with an anvil so you can tell them apart.
