Goose is a manhunt variant made by Reek and optimized by doogile (v1.1, March 2026). It was built
to fix three problems of standard manhunt: the block you mine to guard against lava housing, fall
damage on the drop into the gold room, and the hard toggle needed to bring the upper piglins down.
It needs 3 blackstone.

## How it works

- The route skips the hard toggle and protects the route from lava housing.
- It uses chloe's "−1 blackstone" mining trick, shown at 2:38 in the v1.1 video.
- Its costs, as Daniel_H212 lists them: it is mechanically hard; fast lava can still burn some
  piglins and trades; and it doesn't fit the crossroads hole, a faster trading hole than the
  standard one it uses.
- Quickhunt was designed later as an easier, faster alternative; compare both before committing.

## How to practice

1. Be consistent at the Ranked manhunt first.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, copy the v1.1 video block by block at reduced speed.
3. Drill the −1 blackstone trick separately until it is reliable.
