Safe Boomer is the treasure bastion route to learn first. Treasure is the only linear bastion: the
gold sits in the same places every time, so one route covers almost every seed.

## How it works

- Enter at the lava moat by the bridge. Dig a couple of blocks into the wall and pillar up, leaving
  a one-block gap so piglins can't hit you without gold armor.
- Mine the two gold blocks on the bridge. Place two blocks to cross the gap: a one-block path lets
  piglins push each other into the lava, and every lost piglin slows your trades.
- Jump across and break the key block on the far side. If you leave it, piglins get stuck below and
  arrive late.
- Climb the stairs and block the way behind you. Mine through the wall and place two blocks to
  connect the two towers (ramparts), then dig the trading hole and throw your gold in.
- Collect the tower gold: the three middle blocks first, then the two on the left, and the
  bottom-right block last. Mirror this in the other tower.
- While the piglins trade, loot the chests and scan for the fortress from the roof.

## How to practice

1. Open the LlamaPag bastion map, choose Treasure, and spawn at the lava basin.
2. Walk the route in creative until you know every gold location in both towers.
3. Switch to survival and run it with no armor. Count late piglins and find the block you forgot.
