The portal opens the moment the last eye goes in, and from then on there's no quiet moment to sort
your inventory. Everything the End needs has to be in place before that eye.

## How it works

- **Eye count**: in Ranked every stronghold of the seed has the same number of pre-filled frames.
  Count the empty frames against the eyes you hold before you commit to the portal room.
- **Hotbar**: arrange the hotbar for your End strategy (pearls, blocks, explosives, bow) before
  placing the last eye.
- **Safety first**: spawner broken, lava blocked and spawn set (see Portal Room Safety and Set Spawn).
- **Food**: put food in the offhand before the last eye (see Portal Food Overlap).

## How to practice

1. Write down your End hotbar once and arrange it the same way every run.
2. In practice runs, make the order fixed: spawner, lava, bed, hotbar, food, last eye.
3. Check the frame count on arrival in every run until it's automatic.
