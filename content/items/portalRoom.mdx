The portal room is the end of every stronghold. Knowing the room types tells you which leads are worth
following and lets you recognize the portal room the moment you see it.

## How it works

- **Good leads**: five-ways (crossings with five exits) are the best, then four-ways. Stairs and spiral
  staircases are good because they are long and lead far.
- **Poor leads**: small turns and chest rooms lead nowhere much. Libraries are dead ends.
- **The portal room** is a large room with iron-barred windows and light on both sides. Inside are a
  silverfish spawner on a staircase and a lava pool under the 12 end portal frames.
- **Filling the frames**: use an eye of ender on each empty frame. When all 12 are filled, the portal
  opens; jump in.

## How to practice

1. Watch the first chapter of the k4yfour video and name each room as it appears.
2. In StrongholdTrainer, walk strongholds and say each room's type out loud before entering it.
3. Prefer five-ways, four-ways and stairs; back out of turns and chest rooms early.
