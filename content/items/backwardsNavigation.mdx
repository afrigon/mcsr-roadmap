Some stronghold rooms always generate the same way relative to the starter staircase. Learning them
lets you walk back to the start from anywhere and try the branches you haven't explored.

## How it works

- **Stairs**: spiral and normal stairs always go **down** when heading away from the starter. To go
  back, go **up**.
- **Five-ways**: the main opening always leads back toward the starter.
- **Doors and iron bars**: recessed doors lead toward the starter, flush ones away (see Door Direction).
- **Libraries** are dead ends: turn around.
- **Silverfish**: turn subtitles on. A silverfish hiss is often the portal room's spawner, and the
  sound is easy to miss without subtitles.
- Use it when you entered off target, hit a dead end or lost track: go back to the starter or a known
  five-way and take an unexplored branch.

## How to practice

1. Turn subtitles on in your practice instance and leave them on.
2. In StrongholdTrainer, start at random rooms and walk back to the starter using only stairs, doors
   and five-ways.
3. After each dead end, name the rule that would have told you earlier.
