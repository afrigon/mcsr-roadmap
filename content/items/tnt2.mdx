With two TNT you can clear a spawner almost as well as the donut, with fewer blocks to place. It is a common setup on seeds that give a little TNT, such as desert temples.

## How it works

- One TNT goes on each side of the spawner, raised on soft blocks (soul sand, dirt or sand), with
  hard blocks protecting the spawner. Calisthenicuber's build: hard block, soft, soft, hard block,
  soft, soft.
- Light both TNT together.
- It leaves a few blocks around the edges, such as fence at the top. Mine those, then build the
  platform.
- crossroads198x's timings put 2 TNT among the faster open setups to reach 80–90% available space.

## How to practice

1. In Mescht's Blaze Practice map, copy the build from the Jacko and Calisthenicuber clips below.
2. Light both TNT and check the available space overlay.
3. Repeat until the build takes no thought, on both open and buried spawners.
