A bed blast or the dragon itself can destroy the block you are standing on. Plan the setup so that
losing it doesn't drop you off the tower.

## How it works

- The indestructible blocks of the setup (obsidian, crying obsidian, anchors) survive the blasts;
  the blocks under you might not.
- Keep an indestructible block below or beside your top position. A block under the top anchor
  stops you being knocked off.
- The staircase you built on the way up leaves a lower ledge that can catch you. From there, place
  a block and step back up.

## How to practice

1. In the practice map, break your standing block mid-zero and get back into position.
