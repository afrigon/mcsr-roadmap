Wither skeletons hit hard and inflict the wither effect, and fortresses spawn plenty of them. Rather than fighting, use the fact that they path toward you and fall off bridges easily.

## How it works

- Wither skeletons are too tall to fit through a 2-block gap, and they walk straight toward you.
- On an open bridge, pillar up three blocks beside the edge. The withers gather at the edge below
  you, and you punch them off the bridge.
- Mikeyboie's variant: build up two blocks beside the fence, so they stand two blocks below you.
- Aggro several at once before you pillar, so you clear them in one go.
- Soul sand valley fortresses and low render distance bring more of them.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, set survival mode in a fortress full of wither skeletons.
2. Pillar beside the edge and knock every wither off without taking a hit.
3. Repeat on different bridge shapes.
