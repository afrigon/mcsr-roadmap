Never walk into the nether with an empty bucket. A lava bucket is a weapon and a tool there, and the
portal animation is free time to eat.

## How it works

- After lighting the portal, scoop one more lava from the pool or ravine before you step in.
- In the nether, lava handles hoglins and helps on bridges, treasure and stables bastions. Without it,
  hoglins become a real problem.
- The portal takes a few seconds to teleport you. Eat during that time, so you arrive with full hunger
  without stopping.
- Rotten flesh is the food to eat here. Keep the better food for the nether.

## How to practice

1. Make "scoop lava, then step in" a fixed habit at the end of every portal build.
2. Keep food in your offhand so you can eat in the animation without opening the inventory.
