From 900 Elo, Ranked adds snowy and taiga villages to the seed pool. They look different and lack
some of what plains villages give, so they need their own plan.

## How it works

- Both have their own blacksmith designs; learn what they look like before you meet them.
- Taiga villages always have an enter with 10+ obsidian, so you don't need a bucket portal. The lava
  pools still exist.
- When a village has 10+ obsidian, the golem is the only guaranteed iron.
- Snowy villages can have an armorer instead of a weaponsmith.
- Food is thin. Kill animals, or plan for hoglins in the nether.

## How to practice

1. Look up both blacksmith designs in the house reference.
2. Practice the obsidian-only portal, which taiga seeds rely on.
