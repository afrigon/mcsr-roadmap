Sometimes the End spawns you inside the main island's end stone. A gold pickaxe is the fastest way
out: it breaks end stone in 0.4 s, against 0.6 s for diamond and 0.75 s for iron.

## Digging out

- Keep a gold pickaxe in your End hotbar for this.
- Dig at the back of the platform, aiming backward toward the island, rather than straight up, so you
  come out where the fight starts.
- Combine it with the clip table from Buried Spawn Escape: at some heights a pearl clip is still
  faster than digging.
