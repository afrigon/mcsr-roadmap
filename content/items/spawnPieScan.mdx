When you can't see the shipwreck from spawn, the pie chart finds it. A chest entering your view
shows up as a spike, which points you in the right direction.

## How it works

- Press Shift+F3 to open the pie chart, and go to `root.gameRenderer.level.entities`.
- The orange slice is block entities, which includes chests. When a chest comes into view, it spikes.
- Turn slowly. With a thin macro (a window resized very narrow) or a low FOV, the spike tells you
  the direction more precisely.
- Ranked removes other wrecks, buried treasures and irrelevant block entities near spawn, so a spike is
  almost always the structure you want.
- Several runners report that Ranked wrecks and buried treasures are always toward positive X and
  positive Z from spawn. The filter documentation doesn't say so; use it as a hint, not a rule.

## How to practice

1. Preset the pie-chart directory in StandardSettings so it opens in the right place.
2. On shipwreck practice seeds, scan before looking around, and check how close you were.
