On some village seeds the blacksmith holds enough obsidian to skip the lava pool. Recognising it
saves the walk to the pools.

## How it works

- Ranked guarantees one of two enters on village seeds: three lava pools near the village, or 8+
  obsidian in the blacksmith.
- With 8 or 9 obsidian, use the blacksmith's own lava to cast the rest of the frame. The weaponsmith
  has two lava blocks built into it.
- With 10 or more, build an obsidian-only portal and light it.
- If the blacksmith has 10+ obsidian, the golem is the only guaranteed iron.
- Otherwise head for the lava pools after looting.

## How to practice

1. On village practice seeds, decide "blacksmith enter" or "lava pools" as soon as you open the chest.
2. Practice casting the missing blocks from the blacksmith's lava.
