After manhunt you sometimes lack obsidian, and the triple chest sits high up. If the terrain behind
the bastion is open, one pearl puts you on the triple's rampart and saves the long climb.

## How it works

- From the back of the bastion, bridge out two blocks with one block on top. Sneak to the front edge
  of that block, look straight up, then a tiny bit toward the bastion, jump-throw the pearl and hold
  W. You land at the triple rampart.
- From the gold area, ThugMCSR's lineup: crouch to the edge of the black zone, look straight up,
  jump-throw and hold W.
- Pearl paths vary slightly, so be ready to block clutch (place a block to catch yourself) on the
  wall.
- A door placed where you land stops fall damage; Calisthenicuber shows it with a door from a
  shipwreck seed. Doors placed inside the player are allowed by the run rules.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, enable terrain and practice the back lineup.
2. Throw it 20 times and note how often you need the block clutch.
