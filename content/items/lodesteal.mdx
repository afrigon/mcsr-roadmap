Lodesteal is a triple-to-chalice (TTC) route: you aggro the piglins at the left triple, the one with
the lodestone chest, and lead them to the chalice to trade, instead of carrying the gold back to the
triple. Pavkin named it; kindred's showcase (April 2026) builds on BeefSalad's "Jimbo James" route.
kindred warns that below Diamond rank it probably won't save time.

## How it works

- Best when your closest entry is the left triple.
- Break the single chest with an axe, throwing a distraction gold first.
- Bridge out five blocks before dropping down, so the piglins can follow.
- Place the blocks that let the right rampart's piglins down, punch a piglin, and head to the
  chalice.
- Block the spots where piglins would fall into the wrong hole, then build the auto funnel. Place the
  bottom dirt block as early as possible so they go into the two-deep hole.
- Mine the chalice, crafting ingots now and then and throwing them down to the piglins.
- To collect trades, place two dirt blocks and tuck into the corner.
- It costs under half a stack of blocks. Buried-hole and chimney-hole variants exist.

## How to practice

1. Be consistent at the TNT chalice and an auto-funnel route first.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, set the left rampart to a triple and copy kindred's video.
3. Watch for piglins pathing into the wrong hole; standing on the dirt block longer helps.
