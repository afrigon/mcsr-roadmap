Sometimes the fortress is much closer than the bastion. Going there first means fighting blazes without fire resistance, so you need protection: a shield, or an enchanted golden apple.

## How it works

- **Shield**: hold it in the offhand to block fireballs. Blazes fire in predictable bursts, so you
  can learn when to block. The cost: no food in the offhand.
- **Enchanted golden apple**: eat it to get 5 minutes of fire resistance, enough for the fortress.
  Desert temple and ruined portal chests can contain them.
- Clearing the spawner is still faster, but more blazes means more fire to handle without fire
  resistance. Without an apple, skipping the clear is the safer choice.
- It is situational. Calisthenicuber warns against it in general, but some seeds make it clearly
  better.

## How to practice

1. In a blaze practice map, take only a shield and an axe, and kill blazes to 6 rods without
   dying.
2. Learn the blaze shooting rhythm until you raise the shield before each burst.
3. Repeat with an enchanted golden apple and a cleared spawner.
