A boat crawl puts you into the crawling pose so you can move through a one-block-tall gap, usually
while digging through a nether wall toward a fortress or your second portal coordinates.

## How it works

- Mine a short one-tall gap into the wall, place the boat in front of it, and get in.
- Get out of the boat or break it while your head is under the low ceiling. The game can't stand you
  up, so you start crawling.
- From there, keep mining forward. A crawl tunnel is one block tall, so you mine half as many blocks as
  a 2×1 tunnel.
- When you have a pearl, a pearl crawl does the same thing without the boat.

## How to practice

1. In a practice world, cut a one-tall gap into a netherrack wall and practice getting into the crawl
   from a boat until it works first try.
2. Then mine a 20-block crawl tunnel and compare it with a 2×1 tunnel.
