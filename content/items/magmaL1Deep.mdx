Some ravines have only one layer of lava under the magma. From above they look normal; you notice it
when stone shows up under the first lava you take. The portal still works if you replace that stone
with lava while building.

## How it works

- Build the small L normally and grab the same four lava.
- Look under the first scoops: stone means the ravine is 1-deep, and those two spots must become
  obsidian.
- Mine out the two stone blocks that should be obsidian.
- Sneak and lean back as far as you can, then scoop extra lava from just under the neighbouring
  obsidian and pour it into each gap.
- Finish and drain the portal as usual.

## How to practice

1. In a portal practice map, pick 1-deep ravines and check for stone on every first scoop.
2. Listen for the stone breaking before you pour, so you don't place lava onto stone.
