Toe Tickler is a TNT route on the stables bastion's gap, designed by Lucento as the theoretical
fastest way to finish it. Its author calls it unrealistic, so treat it as a showcase of what the
gap allows rather than a route to run.

## How it works

- It belongs to the same family as the gap TNT routes (CrispyTNT, DTGB), where TNT replaces mining
  gold blocks by hand.
- The video shows the full route; the timing it asks for is the reason it is not used in matches.
