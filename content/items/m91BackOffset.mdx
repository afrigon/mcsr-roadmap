A normal back y95 on M-91 breaks the crystal, so you move one block further from it: an offset setup,
still on top of the tower. It reaches back M-91 at a lower, earlier height than y99.

## How it works

- Stand on −29 26 at y95. All guides agree on this block.
- Anchors can't go in the normal spot on this setup; use them only as the obsidian.
- On offset setups, rotation changes the bed placement, so read clockwise or counter-clockwise
  during the pearl.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set back M-91 and alternate rotations.
2. Add the bow +1 once five singles are consistent.
