The blacksmith's chest sits against a wall. Instead of walking in through the door, break the wall
next to the chest and loot it from outside.

## How it works

- From the front, mine the two logs next to the chest and loot through the gap. The logs also give
  you your crafting table and sticks.
- From the back, mine the window, jump and loot through it.
- The same idea works on the acacia blacksmith: loot through a window or mine two logs.

## How to practice

1. On village practice seeds, approach the blacksmith from different sides and pick the right block
   to break.
