Some gaps are just too long to jump. If you carry a boat, jump anyway: mid-air, place the boat on the
far edge and get into it, and the boat carries you over the last stretch.

## The move

- Sprint-jump across the gap with the boat in hand.
- Before you start falling below the far edge, look at the top of the far edge and place the boat
  there, then right-click it to get in.
- From the boat, move on or get out on solid ground.

## Getting it consistent

Practice over water or a short drop first, so a late placement only costs a swim. The timing to
learn is placing the boat while the far edge is still in reach.
