Running out of food in the nether ends runs. Bring enough, and eat the bad food first, so the good
food is there when you need it most.

## How it works

- Eat rotten flesh in the overworld. It is plentiful in chests and only filling for its moment.
- Save the good food (cooked pork, steak, bread, golden carrots) for the nether, especially for
  throwing pearls from the bastion to the fortress.
- Eat during the portal animation, so you arrive with full hunger.
- Keep food in your offhand, so you can eat without opening the inventory.
- Staying alive beats playing fast. As a beginner, take more food than top runners do: 7–10 pieces of
  good food is a safe target.
- In Ranked, the chance that rotten flesh gives hunger is standardized, so both players get the same
  outcomes.

## How to practice

1. Count your food before every nether enter and note when you ran short.
2. Make "eat in the portal" a habit on every practice seed.
