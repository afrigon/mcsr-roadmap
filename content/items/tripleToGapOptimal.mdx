The side triple to gap (TTG) variants start from a left or right triple and pick the best path down
for the exact layout. They pull 16 to 18 or more piglins and are the default stables play at high
Elo. Learn the middle version on the Middle page first.

## How it works

meebie's guide covers each case:

| Variant | Notes |
| ------- | ----- |
| Left triple to middle lantern | The common fast one on exposed bastions. |
| Right triple to middle lantern | Dig down two, cap yourself off in the far corner, mine through three. |
| Left or right triple to middle single (exposed) | Go slower on purpose so late piglins catch up. |
| Right triple to middle single (buried) | For buried bastions. |
| Left or right triple to the gap below it | Classic TTG: dig straight down into a good gap. |
| Triple to the bad gap below it | Left side becomes the Hourglass route. |

- Going through the middle rampart avoids dropping into a bad gap where piglins wait for you.
- Breaking a gold block or a chest aggros every idle piglin in range, so do it where the train
  will follow you.
- Use placed blocks as checkpoints, and throw distraction gold at every choke point.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, fix one triple and middle rampart combination and run it until it's clean.
2. Watch top runners' stables routes on the Ranked stats site to see the choices in real games.
