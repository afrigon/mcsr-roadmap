A buried spawn costs time, about 1.1 s per O level, so the setups you can reach in time get lower.
Learn y86 and y90 and the offset setups, so a buried spawn still ends in a zero.

## How it works

- Low heights: y86 and y90 on the low towers, with the standard standing blocks (28 −27 front,
  −30 25 back).
- Use y86, not y87. Older guides used y87 or y88, which break the first bed more often.
- Offset and side setups often have more lenience than top setups, for example back M-91 y95 offset
  or the Tall Boy side.
- T-94 y95 on 26 −28 front or −27 27 back exists mainly for buried spawns.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set a buried spawn, clip out, and pick the setup the spawn allows.
