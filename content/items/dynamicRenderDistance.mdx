Lowering render distance changes where hostile mobs spawn. Runners use this to make stray blazes (blazes spawning around the fortress, not from a spawner) appear near them, either to top up rods or when no spawner can be found.

## How it works

- Hostile mobs stop spawning naturally once the monster count reaches the cap of 70. F3 shows the
  count as "M".
- Drop render distance to about 5 (runners use 3 to 6). Far mobs unload, M falls, and the game
  spawns new mobs in the few chunks still loaded around you, blazes included.
- It also brings more wither skeletons. Be ready to deal with them.
- It works best in fortress biomes with high spawn rates: soul sand valley and warped forest, and
  also basalt deltas, where fewer other mobs take up the cap.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, stand in a fortress, open F3 and lower render distance step by step.
2. Watch M drop and see where new blazes appear.
3. Try it in a soul sand valley fortress and in a nether wastes one, and compare.
