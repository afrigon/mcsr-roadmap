A god sensitivity is a mouse sensitivity whose smallest turning step divides the boat's snap angle
exactly. With it, entering a boat always puts you on an angle Ninjabrain Bot already knows, so boat
eye needs no extra F3+C.

## How it works

- Ninjabrain Bot assumes boat angle 0. Set its default boat mode to green boat.
- The boat angle also survives the angle wrap that happens when you go through a portal or
  teleport with a pearl.
- ExeRSolver's list gives the valid values. Commonly used ones are 0.02291165, 0.058765005 and
  0.07446537. Some also survive re-entering while the boat is still rotating, or turning past 105°
  in the boat.
- Rules: if you turn the boat itself (A/D), get in and out once more without pressing A/D. With
  0.07446537, don't turn the mouse more than 105° while in the boat.
- A god sensitivity changes how the mouse feels. Compensate with mouse DPI and cursor speed, or with
  Toolscreen's global sensitivity.

## How to practice

1. Pick a value from the list or the calculator and write it into `options.txt` exactly.
2. Adjust DPI until normal play feels the same as before.
3. Do boat measurements in a test world and confirm the boat icon is green with no F3+C on entry.
