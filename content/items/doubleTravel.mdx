Measure the stronghold at the blind portal, go back into the nether, and build a second portal at
the stronghold's nether coordinates. One nether block is 8 overworld blocks, so this is far faster
than travelling in the overworld.

## How it works

- You need 20 obsidian in total: 10 for the blind and 10 for the second portal.
- After measuring, re-enter the blind portal and travel to the nether coordinates Ninjabrain Bot
  shows.
- Use the F3 "Block" line and build the second portal standing on that exact block.
- Build low (around Y40–45), with fire resistance if there is lava around.
- Coming out, dig in at the stronghold's 4, 4 spot.

## How to practice

1. In practice runs, bring 20 obsidian and double travel every time the stronghold is far.
2. Practice reading the F3 Block line while moving so you stop on the exact block.
