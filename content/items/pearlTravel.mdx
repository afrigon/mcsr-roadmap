In the nether, a well-aimed sprint-jump pearl covers a lot of ground and skips terrain you would
otherwise walk around. Throwing at the right moment and angle gets the most distance per pearl.

## How it works

- Sprint, jump, and throw as you are reaching the top of the jump, while you are still moving upward.
- Calisthenicuber throws at a pitch of about −19° to −30° (looking slightly up). That range sends
  pearls far; aim higher only when you have to clear something.
- Open biomes like nether wastes are the best for pearling; trees and basalt deltas block pearls or
  leave you on bad terrain.
- Every pearl still costs 2.5 hearts, so watch your health and food.

## How to practice

1. In a nether practice world, sprint-jump pearl at different pitches and note where they land.
2. Practice the timing until you throw near the top of the jump every time without thinking.
