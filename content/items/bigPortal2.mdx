Big Portal 2 is a large ruined portal that can be completed. Two obsidian sit under its two gold
blocks, and about 10 lava nearby covers the rest.

## How it works

- Recognise it by the two obsidian blocks under the two gold blocks.
- It has 2 gold and 1 chest.
- The reference completion takes about 8.1 seconds without crying obsidian and about 13.5 seconds with
  it.

## How to practice

1. Copy the reference completion in a practice map.
