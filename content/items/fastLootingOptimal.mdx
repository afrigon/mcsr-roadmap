Two refinements of fast looting: a three-input version for the first chest of the run, and a
hotkeyless version that leaves your hotbar and offhand untouched.

## How it works

- **Three-input**: while holding Shift, left-click a stack, press a hotbar key, left-click again.
  Use slot 9 first, then 8, 7 and so on. It needs empty hotbar slots, so it only works on the first
  chest of the run (buried treasure, shipwreck).
- **Hotkeyless**: shift-click a stack, then hold the mouse button down on the now-empty slot,
  release Shift, then release the mouse button. Useful when your hotbar keys are on mouse buttons
  or when a full bastion inventory must stay sorted.
- Use the four-input offhand method whenever neither fits.

## How to practice

1. Practise the three-input version on a fresh buried treasure chest in a practice map.
2. Practise hotkeyless looting in a bastion chest with a full inventory, as in the osh video.
