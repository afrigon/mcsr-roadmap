L-gap is a stables gap route for bastions without a triple. Learn it as a rescue for when you
botch the Crispy or BeefSalad hole and can't finish it in time.

## How it works

- Original route by crossroads198x, optimised by Lucento.
- Lucento's comparison: on stables with no triple, L-gap is slightly faster than the BeefSalad hole.
  It needs fast, precise mechanics, though, so he still recommends BeefSalad or Crispy as the main
  route.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, set no triples and run L-gap from the start.
2. Then practise switching to it from a half-built Crispy hole.
