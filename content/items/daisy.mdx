Daisy is a safe right-side bridge route by Lucento (concept, July 2026). It goes chalice to chalice
with a difficulty toggle, and pulled 21 piglins in the showcase. Use it when lava or terrain get in
the way of soog.

## How it works

- Only for a right triple that is open, not buried.
- It is not a TNT route: you mine the chalice.
- You go from the chalice and back to it, with a difficulty toggle on the way.

## How to practice

1. Learn soog first; Daisy is its backup.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, set the right rampart to a triple and copy the showcase.
