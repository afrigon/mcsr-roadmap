In a boat, your hitbox sits lower than the boat's floor. Boating next to lava at the same level, or
over its edge, puts part of that hitbox in the lava and sets you on fire, although the boat itself
never touches it.

## Where it bites

- Boating down nether terrain past lava pools.
- Crossing next to lava lakes in the overworld or the nether.

## Avoiding it

Keep the boat a block away from lava, or have fire resistance on before you boat past it.
