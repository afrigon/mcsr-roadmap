A handful of video and audio options make the game easier to read and faster to play. Most top runners converge on the same values; field of view is the main personal choice.

## How it works

| Setting | Common value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brightness | 500% | See into caves, ravines and dark bastion rooms. |
| GUI scale | 3 or 4 (the largest your screen allows) | Bigger slots make crafting and looting clicks easier. |
| Field of view (FOV) | Personal, 100 to Quake Pro | Wider shows more around you; pick one and keep it. |
| Subtitles | On | Lava, mobs and portals show their direction on screen. |
| View bobbing | Off | Steadier aim. |
| Particles | Minimal | Less clutter and better performance. |
| VSync / max frame rate | Off / unlimited | Lower input lag; cap the frame rate later if you record. |
| Entity distance | 500% | Entities stay visible far away, which bastion finding relies on. |
| Entity culling | Off | Keeps far entities counted in F3. |
| Music | Off | Hear the game sounds clearly. |
| Auto-jump | Off | It jumps when you don't want it to. |

- Render distance is usually set somewhere around 10 to 20. Runners who play buried treasure seeds start lower (around 8) and raise it once they know the seed type.
- Ranked's own options menu has an FOV effects slider. Turning it down (HiTrippy uses 25%) stops soul speed and potions from warping the screen without changing your speed.
- Set these in StandardSettings once you use it, or every new world reverts them.
