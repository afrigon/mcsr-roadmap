A pearl crawl throws a pearl into a one-block-tall gap so you land already crawling, and already
sprinting. It is a little faster to set up than a boat crawl, at the cost of a pearl and 2.5 hearts.

## How it works

- Mine a one-tall gap into the wall.
- Sprint and throw the pearl at the bottom of the gap. You teleport into it crawling, and you keep the
  sprint. Sprinting while crawling is an allowed glitch under the speedrun rules.
- While sprint crawling you can sprint-jump forward. Pressing sneak ends the sprint.
- On low health, use a boat crawl instead and keep the hearts.

## How to practice

1. In a nether practice world, mine a short one-tall gap and pearl into it while sprinting.
2. Check that you are sprinting by jumping once inside. Repeat until it works first try.
