Many crafts can happen during travel instead of at a stop. Doing them on the way leaves your
arrival free for the next task.

## How it works

- **Bastion to fortress**: nether bricks, glowstone and wool are 2×2 crafts, so the inventory grid
  is enough, even on the move.
- **Fortress to stronghold**: beds, respawn anchors, TNT and the bow need a crafting table. Craft
  them standing at a table, overlapped with something else, such as waiting for blaze waves.
- Eyes and blaze powder are 2×2 crafts; see Crafting While Pearling.

## How to practice

1. On every bastion-to-fortress trip in practice, finish the 2×2 crafts before you arrive.
2. At the blaze spawner, craft beds, anchors and the bow between waves.
