TNT can't break blocks underwater, because the water absorbs the blast. Give it a pocket of air and it
clears a patch of sea floor: lots of gravel for flint, or sand.

## How it works

- Swim down, place a pressure plate on the floor and the TNT right on top of it, then swim through.
  The plate gives the explosion a pocket of air to break blocks in.
- You don't need to swim far away: place a few blocks around your legs and you only take about half a
  heart.
- Collect the drops and get back up for air.

## How to practice

1. Try it on a desert temple seed near water, with the leftover TNT.
2. Compare the gravel you get with mining it by hand.
