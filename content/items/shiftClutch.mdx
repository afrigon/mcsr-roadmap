The shift clutch is not legal. Timing sneak just before landing can cancel fall damage, but it is
banned in Ranked and in speedrun.com runs, so never use it in a match.

## How it works

- Sneaking changes your speed just before impact. The game's collision check then misses the
  landing, and you take no fall damage. An "edge stall clutch" works the same way without sneaking.
- The Ranked wiki lists "Shift clutch" under disallowed glitches. The speedrun.com rules ban every
  glitch not on their allowed list, and the shift clutch is not on it.
- Legal ways to survive a fall: a water bucket clutch, or the sticky boat clutch (landing in a
  placed boat), which is an allowed glitch. The offhand clutch is also banned.
