On a tall tower you can run out of beds and anchors with the dragon one hit from dying. The tower's
own crystal is one more explosive.

## The save

- Shoot or hit the tower's crystal as the dragon passes, exactly as you would explode a bed.
- A crystal that is close enough also deals 10 damage when shot.
- It only works once and only on towers whose crystal is in reach; plan it as the very last
  explosive, after the bottom anchor if you use one.
