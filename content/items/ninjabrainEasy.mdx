Ninjabrain Bot is a calculator that turns two eye throws into the stronghold's exact location. It
is legal in Ranked and replaces following eyes in every serious run.

## How it works

- Ninjabrain Bot reads the F3+C text (your position and view angle) that Minecraft copies to the
  clipboard.
- Throw an eye, put your crosshair on it, press F3+C. The bot draws a line from you toward the
  stronghold.
- Move sideways from that line, as far as the bot suggests, throw a second eye and press F3+C
  again. Where the two lines cross is the stronghold.
- The bot shows the stronghold chunk, a certainty percentage, the distance, and the matching nether
  coordinates for travelling there.
- In Ranked, the second throw never breaks, so a confirming throw costs no eye.

## How to practice

1. Install the bot and check it updates when you press F3+C in a test world.
2. In practice seeds, measure two eyes on every blind and compare the certainty you get with how far
   apart you threw.
3. Aim for 95% certainty or more before you commit to travelling.
