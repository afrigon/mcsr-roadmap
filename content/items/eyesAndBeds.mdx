Eyes of ender find and open the stronghold, and beds are the main explosive for killing the dragon.
Both are crafted from what you collect in the nether, so you make them on the way to the End.

## How it works

- **Blaze powder**: one blaze rod makes two powder. Craft it in the 2×2 inventory grid.
- **Eye of ender**: one blaze powder plus one ender pearl, also in the 2×2 grid. So one rod covers
  two eyes.
- **Wool**: four string in a 2×2 square makes one white wool, or shear sheep.
- **Bed**: three wool of the same colour over three planks, in a crafting table.
- Keep a few pearls uncrafted for travel and the End; only turn into eyes what you will throw or
  place.

## How to practice

1. In a casual world, craft eyes and beds from the inventory and a table with the recipe book until
   you don't have to look for them.
2. Practise crafting eyes in the 2×2 grid without opening a table; you will do this while moving.
