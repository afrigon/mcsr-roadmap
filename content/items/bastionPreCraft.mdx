The last trades always take a while. Use that wait to craft what the fortress and the End need, so you leave the bastion ready.

## How it works

- Runners usually craft one or two beds and an iron axe while piglins trade. The axe kills blazes faster (axe hit then sword hit) and breaks chests quickly.
- Craft a third bed when a second blind or a lava-covered spawner is possible. It costs an inventory slot.
- With no TNT fuse to overlap, there is no reason to wait: some runners craft their gold helmet and gold pickaxe right when they enter.
- Only pre-craft while you are waiting anyway. If your trades are already done, crafting the beds later at the spawner is about 1.7 seconds faster.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, open your crafting grid as soon as the gold is thrown.
2. Craft beds from your string, and an iron axe from rampart iron.
3. Check the trades between crafts so the hole is never left waiting on you.
