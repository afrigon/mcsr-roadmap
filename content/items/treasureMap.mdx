On random seeds, the map chest in a shipwreck points to the nearest buried treasure. Following it was
the classic way to find treasure; in Ranked it has no use.

## How it works

- Open the shipwreck's map chest, hold the map and walk to the red X. The chest sits under it.
- Ranked's seed filter keeps buried treasure away from shipwreck seeds, so the map never leads to a
  useful chest in a match.
- On buried treasure seeds there is no map: Ranked players find the chest with a pie-chart scan of
  block entities and dig at chunk coordinates 9, 9. That is the skill worth learning.
- On shipwreck seeds, skip the map chest and take the iron and food chests.
