Wood is the first resource of every run: it becomes your crafting table, sticks, tools, boat and the
blocks you build with. Aim for about 10–14 logs before you leave the overworld.

## How it works

- Punch two logs first. That is enough for a crafting table and sticks, so you can craft an axe and
  cut the rest faster.
- Around 10 logs is enough for most seeds; 14 is comfortable. Take more if logs are your only
  building blocks.
- On ocean seeds you need fewer logs when you have shears, because leaves cover your building blocks.
  The wood then only goes into doors, a boat and beds.
- Count your logs before you walk away from the trees. Coming back later costs far more than a few
  extra seconds of chopping.
