You spawn on a small obsidian platform away from the main island. Getting across quickly, without
falling into the void, is the first step of every End fight.

## How it works

- Bridge across with blocks, or throw an ender pearl onto the island.
- Pearls toward a tower or the island are thrown low and fast rather than high and floaty.
- Jump just before a pearl lands. The extra air time gives you a moment to place your first block.

## How to practice

1. In an End practice map, alternate bridging and pearling from the spawn platform.
2. Throw pearls at different distances until you know roughly where a low throw lands.
