Placing a block where an item lies pushes the item up on top of the new block. Routes use this to lift gold drops and ingots to where you or the piglins are, instead of walking down to fetch them.

## How it works

- An item inside a block you place gets pushed out upward. Keep placing and it keeps rising.
- Mining gold below you: keep blocks in your offhand so you can mine and place without switching slots. The dropped gold rides up with each block you place.
- Feeding piglins above you: throw ingots straight up and place a block right after. The ingots reach the trading hole while you keep mining (Housing top-down).
- It can trigger by accident: placing a block in the exact spot where you just broke a gold block can send that drop up out of reach. When that matters, place in a different spot.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, run Housing top-down and elevate the backup gold blocks to the trading hole.
2. Practice throwing ingots up and placing the block immediately, until none fall back down.
