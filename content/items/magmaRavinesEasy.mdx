On ocean seeds the portal is built in a magma ravine. Kelp can't grow where a ravine cut through the
sea floor, so it floats to the surface instead, marking where to dive.

## How it works

- Raise render distance (around 15) and entity distance (500%), and turn on hitboxes (F3+B). Floating
  kelp is an item, so its hitbox shows from far away.
- A large patch of floating kelp is a good sign of a ravine, though other things also cause it.
  Don't mistake squid ink for kelp.
- Search near the structure first. Ranked's filter starts looking for ravines at the shipwreck or
  buried treasure, so the ravine is often close to it.
- Not every Ranked ravine shows kelp. Without it, boat around in third person and look for wide, deep
  ravines.
- Raise your settings back up while you are still crafting on the island, so you spot kelp early and
  pick a direction.

## How to practice

1. On every ocean practice seed, guess the ravine from the surface before diving, and check.
2. Learn your settings toggles so switching them costs no time.
