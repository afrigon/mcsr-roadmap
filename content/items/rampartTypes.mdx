Every stables route starts with knowing what each of the three top ramparts (the towers above the
stables) is. The rampart type decides which route you play, because only triples hold guaranteed
gold.

## How it works

| Type | Height | Contents |
| ---- | ------ | -------- |
| Single | Shortest | One chest at the back. Older guides call it the diagonal rampart. |
| Lantern (also called double) | Medium | Two chests: one up top, one below next to the lantern. |
| Triple | Tallest | A single and a double chest, plus four gold blocks in a room below. |

- From the top of the bastion, a triple shows a basalt formation on the stairs leading up to it.
- Housing also has a triple (always its right rampart). Tell them apart by the chests: Housing
  leaves a three-block space between the single and the double chest, Stables only two.
- Other bastions: Housing's left rampart is a triple, single or ruined; Bridge's are single or
  triple.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag bastion map, set Stables and randomize the ramparts.
2. Name all three ramparts from your spawn view before moving, then fly up to check.
