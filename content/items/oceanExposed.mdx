A stronghold under an ocean can be cut open by the seabed, so its corridors or even its portal room
show in the water. If the blind comes out onto ocean, look before throwing a second eye.

## How it works

- Signs of an ocean stronghold: an ocean biome in F3, and once inside, water dripping from the
  ceiling or behind hidden-room indentations.
- From one eye's direction, swim or boat along the line and look down for stone bricks.
- Third-person view (F5) to see under water is an allowed glitch. Don't clip the camera into
  blocks: that counts as x-ray.
- If you spot the stronghold, skip the second eye and go straight in.

## How to practice

1. When a practice blind comes out onto ocean, check the seabed along the eye's line before
   measuring again.
