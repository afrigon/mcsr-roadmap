Piglins get angry at you in three distinct ways, and each behaves differently. Knowing which one you caused explains late piglins, piglins that ignore you, and why routes sometimes punch and sometimes mine a chest.

## How it works

| Level | Cause | What ends it |
| ----- | ----- | ------------ |
| 1 (light) | A piglin sees you without gold armor. Range about 15 blocks, less when sneaking. | Putting on gold armor, losing line of sight, or a gold item. |
| 2 (medium) | Opening a chest in its view, or breaking a chest or gold-type block within 16 blocks of your feet. | Only a gold item it can see. Gold armor does nothing. |
| 3 (heavy) | Hitting a piglin. Spreads to piglins near the one you hit. | Nothing; lasts about 20–30 seconds. That piglin won't trade meanwhile. |

- **Levels never upgrade.** A level-1 piglin stays level 1 when you break gold. It loses you when it loses sight, gets stuck, and becomes a late piglin.
- To get level 2 instead, break line of sight first so level 1 wears off, then break the gold block.
- Punching overrides every level and guarantees a chase, but costs that piglin's trades for a while. Breaking a chest gives level 2 to everyone near you and keeps them all able to trade.
- Level 2 is centered on you, so break the first gold from far back to avoid pulling piglins that would drop on you.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, stand in view of piglins without armor, then break a gold block. Watch them stop following when you hide.
2. Repeat after blocking their line of sight first. They should follow you all the way.
