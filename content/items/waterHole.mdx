A burning cow runs for water. Give it water right next to it, in a hole, and it runs there instead of
to a distant lake, so it stays in reach until it dies.

## How it works

- Dig a small hole beside the cow and pour your water bucket into it.
- Light the cow. It paths to the water you placed, not to the lake.
- Finish it with an axe hit while it is still in reach.
- This is a cow trick; it needs a water bucket, so it comes after you have one.
- The burning cow may still put itself out partly, so finish it quickly.

## How to practice

1. Try it on cows standing near a pond. Compare with lighting a cow without the hole.
2. Pick the water back up afterwards; you need it for the portal.
