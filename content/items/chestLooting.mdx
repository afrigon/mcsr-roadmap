Every seed starts by looting a structure chest for iron, food and tools. Shift-clicking moves a
whole stack in one click instead of dragging it.

## How it works

- Right-click a chest to open it. Hold Shift and left-click a stack to move it straight into your
  inventory.
- Take what the run needs (iron, food, gold, diamonds, TNT, tools) and leave junk you would only
  throw away later.
- Breaking a chest drops everything in it. On ruined portals, runners take the gold axe from the
  chest and break the chest with it, which is about a second faster than clicking.

## How to practice

1. Loot chests in casual worlds with shift-clicks only, never dragging.
2. Move on to Fast Looting once this is automatic.
