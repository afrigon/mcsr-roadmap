A 2+2 (two rotations, both with doubles) on a 1/8 straight node. It uses its own standing blocks, one
step off the normal 1/8 ones.

## How it works

- Stand on 22 0 front or −23 −1 back, at y92. This is kohout's hybrid setup (bed facing
  north–south).
- Like every 2+2, it relies on the bow +1 on both rotations.

## How to practice

1. Get the 1/8 3+2 consistent first.
2. On the practice map, move to the 2+2 standing blocks and drill both rotations.
