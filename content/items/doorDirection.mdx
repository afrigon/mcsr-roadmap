Stronghold doors tell you which way the starter staircase is. Reading them lets you find your way back
after entering off target or getting lost.

## How it works

- A door **recessed** into the wall leads **toward** the starter.
- A door **flush** with the wall leads **away** from it.
- Iron bars follow the same idea: flush with a wall, they lead back toward the starter. In irregular
  rooms such as turns it can be unclear which side is flush; don't rely on them there.
- The main opening of a five-way always leads back toward the starter.

## How to practice

1. In StrongholdTrainer, start somewhere random and predict from each door whether you're heading
   toward or away from the starter, then check.
2. Combine it with stair direction (see Backwards Navigation) until you can walk back to the starter
   from anywhere.
