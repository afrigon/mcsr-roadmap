The optimal, "prepless" version of hotbar sorting: only the pearls are guaranteed in the hotbar, and
everything else is sorted in the air while each pearl flies. It removes the time spent sorting before
the End.

## How it works

- Throw the pearl, open the inventory, and move the zero items into their slots before it lands.
- Keep the final layout identical to your pre-sorted one, so the zero itself doesn't change.

## How to practice

1. Use Semperzz's sorting practice map, or the zero practice map's inventory randomizer.
2. Sort the whole hotbar during a single pearl's flight.
