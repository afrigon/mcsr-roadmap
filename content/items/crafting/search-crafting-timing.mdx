Open search crafting during time the run spends waiting anyway:

- **Village**: while killing the iron golem.
- **Desert temple or TNT buried treasure**: while the TNT blows up.
- **Flint and steel**: when you open the inventory to craft it.
- **Any other start**: after crafting, or on entering the nether.
