Which spawner setup to use depends on what you carry and the spawner in front of you. The rule of thumb from JackoWacko62: do the best setup you can while still keeping enough explosives for the End fight.

## How it works

- **Explosives for the End come first.** Count the beds or anchors your one cycle or zero cycle
  needs, and only spend the rest at the spawner.
- **4 or more TNT**: always 3 TNT on an open spawner, 4 TNT on a buried one.
- **Open or buried**: some setups only work on one kind; Jacko marks them.
- **Rods already held**: several explosive counts have a faster setup that clears less (use it
  when you already have some rods) and a slower one that clears more.
- **Blaze bed or zero cycle**: in Ranked, a frontcage analysis found that keeping a bed for the zero
  cycle saves more (about 29–38 s) than spending it on a blaze bed (about 20–23 s), from
  intermediate level up.
- With 5 or more rods, ClearColdWater's simulations favour overlaps over finishing the clear.

## How to practice

1. For each loadout in Jacko's pinned comment, say which setup you would pick, then check.
2. In practice runs, decide the setup before you reach the spawner.
3. Review runs where you ran short of explosives in the End and find the choice that caused it.
