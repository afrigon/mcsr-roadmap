Tools decide how fast you get wood, stone and iron, so the order you make them in shapes the whole
overworld. The usual path is a quick wooden tool, then stone, then iron once you have it.

## How it works

- Punch two or three logs, make planks, a crafting table and sticks.
- A wooden pickaxe mines the stone you need for stone tools. Skip it when you already have iron or
  cobblestone from a chest.
- A stone or iron axe speeds up wood. A shovel speeds up gravel and sand. An iron pickaxe is needed
  for diamonds and gold blocks.
- Gold tools are cheap and fast but fragile: a gold axe is good for a quick wood grab and a gold
  pickaxe for netherrack and blackstone.
- Most seeds give enough iron for an iron pickaxe, a bucket and flint and steel. See Crafting by
  Iron Count for what to make with the rest.

## How to practice

1. Play a few casual worlds and get from spawn to an iron pickaxe and a bucket without stopping to
   think about the next craft.
2. Count your logs before crafting so you never run short of planks for sticks.
