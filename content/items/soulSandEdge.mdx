Crouching normally stops you at the edge of a block. Stepping from a full block onto soul sand while
crouched doesn't: soul sand's top is slightly lower, and the edge protection lets you walk off.

## Where it bites

- Bridging or crouch-walking along a soul sand edge over a drop or lava, for example around a
  blaze spawner platform extended with soul sand.
- Sneak bridging backward onto a soul sand block you just placed.

## Avoiding it

Don't trust crouch at the edge of soul sand. Keep a full block under you at the edge, or face the
edge and stop with your own movement instead.
