The donut is the simplest 4-TNT spawner setup: a ring of TNT around a buried spawner, all lit at once. It clears quickly but often leaves hard blocks behind, which later 4-TNT setups fix.

## How it works

- Place four TNT around the spawner, one on each side, and light them all quickly.
- TNT makes no fire, so there is nothing to put out afterwards. Mine the leftover blocks.
- Daniel_H212 traces the 4-TNT line from the donut to the raised donut (more consistent, slower,
  still leaves hard blocks) to Fyroah's 4 TNT, which replaced both.
- Calisthenicuber rates 3 TNT about as good or better, with fewer blocks to place.

## How to practice

1. In Mescht's Blaze Practice map, give yourself four TNT and flint and steel or fire charges.
2. Build the donut on a buried spawner, light it and check the available spawn space.
3. Compare the result with 3 TNT on the same spawner.
