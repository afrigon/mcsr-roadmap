Every village house is built from a fixed set of designs. Knowing which houses hold beds, hay, a
crafting table or a chest lets you route through a village without checking every door.

## How it works

- In plains and acacia villages, the small houses are the ones with beds.
- Desert villages have houses with a crafting table, which saves wood when trees are far away.
- Acacia village houses can hold a bucket. It isn't worth planning for, but it can save killing the
  golem when the house is on your route.
- The house reference below shows each design per biome.

## How to practice

1. Go through the reference one biome at a time and memorise where beds and chests sit.
2. On village practice seeds, name each house's contents before you enter it.
