Entering a boat snaps your view to a known angle. Ninjabrain Bot uses that to recover far more
precision than F3+C prints, so one well-measured eye is usually enough to find the stronghold.

## How it works

- After the boat snap, every mouse movement turns you by a fixed step that depends on your
  sensitivity. Knowing the start angle and the step, the bot can compute your exact angle.
- Combined with tall resolution and pixel adjustment, the single eye is precise enough to go
  straight to the stronghold.
- Use a god sensitivity: one where entering a boat always lands on a valid angle, so you never need
  F3+C when you get in. Ninjabrain Bot's boat icon must be green before you measure.
- Bot settings: enable boat measurements, enter the exact sensitivity from `options.txt`, set the
  resolution height to your tall resize (16384) and keep the allowable boat error at 0.03.
- Workflow: get in and out of the boat, stand still, throw, measure with tall zoom, F3+C, correct
  the pixels.
- If the result is unsure, throw a second eye; in Ranked the second throw never breaks.

## How to practice

1. Set up god sensitivity first (see its page), then the bot settings above.
2. Measure single eyes in practice seeds and check how often the top chunk is right.
3. Watch the boat icon every time: a non-green icon means the measurement is wasted.
