Most failed runs end with a death in the nether, and lava is the usual cause. Treat every lava block
as a reset and carry fire resistance as your safety net.

## How it works

- **Look before you move**: lava sits under ledges, behind netherrack you mine, and at the bottom of
  drops. Mine and bridge with the crosshair where you are going, not where you are.
- **Blocks first**: place a block on lava that flows toward you, and bridge over lava instead of
  jumping across it. Bring a stack of blocks into the nether.
- **Fire resistance**: piglins barter fire resistance potions, both drinkable and splash. Under its
  effect lava and fire deal no damage. Take two or three with you out of the bastion.
- **If you fall in**: with fire resistance you surface and can climb or crawl out. Without it,
  pearl out at once.
- **Death resets**: dying clears potion effects, so drink fire resistance again after a hunger reset.

## How to practice

1. Run nether-only practice seeds and count your lava deaths per session; aim for zero.
2. Each time you jump a gap, ask whether a block would have been safer. Usually it is.
3. Keep fire resistance in the same hotbar slot every run so you can drink it without looking.
