A run fills your inventory with junk: saplings, extra swords, stray blocks. Clearing it as you go
keeps the items you need easy to find and the recipe book short.

## How it works

- Throw junk away right after picking it up, while travelling, instead of in a separate stop.
- Craft what you can (logs into planks, string into wool) then drop the rest.
- The offhand holds one extra stack: park an item there to free a hotbar slot or to take it out of a
  recipe-book list.
- Habit matters more than speed here. The inventory gets much fuller in the nether.

## How to practice

1. In every practice run, empty your inventory of junk before entering the nether.
2. In bastion practice, drop junk between chests instead of after the bastion.
