The portal room holds the stronghold's only silverfish spawner, and silverfish make noise. With
subtitles on, you can hear the room before you see it and head toward it.

## What to listen for

- **Silverfish hisses**: the spawner's silverfish, heard from about 16 blocks. When the source is off
  screen, the subtitle shows an arrow (`<` or `>`) for the side it's on.
- **Lava pops**: the portal room has lava under the portal, though other rooms and caves can too.
- Silverfish footsteps make no sound, and the portal frames are silent.

## Using it

Turn subtitles on in your core settings. When a hiss appears, turn until the arrow disappears, then
go that way.
