A failed zero costs far more than a one cycle. Decide during the first pearl whether this tower,
node and spawn is one you can hit, and take the safe kill when it isn't.

## How it works

Go when:
- the tower and height leave you spare time (positive lenience) for your speed and spawn;
- you have enough explosives: 5 singles, about 4 with a bow, more for side setups without a bow.

Bail to a one cycle when:
- the pearl breaks the crystal;
- you are too late for the lowest safe height;
- your health is low on a back dragon;
- you have only 3 pearls on back (take only the easy setups).

In real runs, take a one cycle on any setup you can't hit yet in practice.

## How to practice

1. Keep a list of the setups you hit consistently in the practice map, and only go for those in
   real runs.
2. Grow the list one tower at a time.
