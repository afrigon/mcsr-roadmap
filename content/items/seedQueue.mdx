SeedQueue is a mod for random-seed (RSG) runs that generates many worlds at once and lets you reset
them from a wall of previews. Ranked hands both players the same seed, so there is nothing to reset.

## How it works

- The mod keeps a queue of worlds generating in the background, each using about 250 MB of memory.
- The wall screen shows previews of the queued worlds. Keys act on the hovered preview:
  - **Play** enters that world.
  - **Lock** keeps it safe from mass resets.
  - **Reset** discards it; **Reset All** discards every unlocked world.
  - **Focus Reset** plays or locks the hovered world and resets all the others.
- A reset cooldown protects previews that just appeared from accidental resets.
- It needs the Atum and SpeedrunAPI mods.
