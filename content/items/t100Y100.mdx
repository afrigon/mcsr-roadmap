The top setup on T-100 at y100, front and back. It is hard: you stand level with the top of the tower,
close to the crystal. On back it needs a bow.

## How it works

| Node | Standing | Height |
| ---- | -------- | ------ |
| Front | 25 −27 | 100 |
| Back | −26 26 | 100 |

- Back needs a bow (Couriway's guide).

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set T-100 and drill front y100 first, then back with the bow.
