The Big U is a large U-shaped ruined portal with stone-brick walls. It needs three obsidian and
usually has plenty of lava and gold.

## How it works

- It is the only small ruined portal with stone-brick walls.
- It has 3 gold: two about 6 blocks out and down, one beside the chest 3 blocks down. The chest is on
  the smaller pillar's side.
- Cast the three missing obsidian from its lava.
- The reference completion takes about 5.4 seconds without crying obsidian and about 11.7 seconds with
  it.

## How to practice

1. Copy both reference completions in a practice map.
