When you blind over an ocean, a thrown eye that drops instead of breaking lands in the water, far
from the pillar you threw from. Throw a pearl after it to land beside the eye and pick it up before
it drifts away or despawns.

## The move

- Throw the eye and watch where it drops.
- Throw a pearl toward the spot as soon as it falls, so you land next to it.
- Grab the eye, then get back to building or travelling.

## When it's worth it

An eye is worth far more than a pearl late in the run: losing one can mean one eye short at the
portal. Without spare pearls, swim or boat to it instead.
