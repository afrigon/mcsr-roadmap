Dying on purpose in the portal room, after setting your spawn there with a bed, restores full health,
food and saturation before the End. It is common and often the safest way to enter.

## How it works

- Set your spawn with a bed in the portal room, then die and respawn there.
- A bed in the overworld sets your spawn instead of exploding.
- Your items drop where you die, so die somewhere you can pick them up straight after respawning.

## How to practice

1. Practise the reset in the portal room until it takes the same few seconds every time.
