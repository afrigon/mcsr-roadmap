Respawn anchors are crafted from crying obsidian and glowstone, so they add explosives when you are
short on beds. On a zero they go last, after the beds: a mistimed anchor absorbs damage.

## How it works

- An anchor explodes from its own block, not from a pillow on the node, so time it slightly earlier
  than a bed.
- Anchors can replace the setup's obsidian. Cover the side facing you with a hard block
  (cobblestone, nether brick), not soul sand or netherrack.
- On tall towers the bottom anchor breaks the crystal, so it is the very last explosive.
- On back T-94 and on all offset and side setups, anchors work only as the obsidian.
- A block under the top anchor stops you being knocked off.

## How to practice

1. On front M-88 y95, replace the obsidian with anchors and finish each zero with them.
2. Compare the incorrect and correct T-94 anchor clips below.
