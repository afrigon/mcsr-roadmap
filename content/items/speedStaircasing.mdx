Speed staircasing (also called one stacking) builds a staircase upward without stopping. It is far
quicker than staircasing while sneaking the whole time, and it is how every zero-cycle tower setup
starts.

## How it works

- It is speed bridging with a jump on every block. Hold back and one strafe key (S and D) the whole
  time.
- Tap jump, place the block under and behind you, and release and re-press crouch just like in speed
  bridging. Calisthenicuber releases crouch as he clicks.
- Calisthenicuber places with a butterfly click (two fingers alternating on one button).
- There is a lot going on at once; it only becomes reliable through muscle memory.
- Learning it also helps with the inclined version of diagonal bridging.

## How to practice

1. Practice the motion in creative until you can climb 10 blocks without sneaking the whole way.
2. Then drill it inside zero-cycle setups on a practice map, where the staircase is the first step.
