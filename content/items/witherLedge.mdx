In the hallways there is no bridge edge to knock wither skeletons off. You can make one: a single extra block turns a pillar into a ledge they step out onto.

## How it works

- Stack blocks at the edge of where you stand and place one more block to the side, forming an L.
  The withers path up onto it, push each other off, or are easy to punch off.
- Calisthenicuber's version: go up four blocks instead of three, then place a block on the side.
- Hax recommends the L shape for the bad side, where you cannot just pillar beside a fence.

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, switch to survival in a fortress hallway with several withers.
2. Build the L and knock all of them off.
3. Then try it in a narrow spot where you have only one side to build on.
