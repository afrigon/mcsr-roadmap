A hitbox scan refines the preemptive read: with hitboxes shown, you turn in place and watch the reading
change. It helps tell a spawner apart from a library when the numbers alone are ambiguous.

## How it works

- Keep hitboxes on (F3+B) while turning in the portal for the preemptive read.
- How the hitbox wiggle separates a spawner from a library is shown in the Mikeyboie example below;
  learn it from the footage rather than a description.

## How to practice

1. Master the plain preemptive read first.
2. Watch the hitbox toggle example, then repeat the same scan in a preemptive practice map.
