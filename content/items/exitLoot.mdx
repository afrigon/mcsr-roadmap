The bastion exists to supply the rest of the run. Before leaving, check that you have the four things nothing else in the run gives you reliably.

## How it works

- **Ender pearls**: fast travel through the nether and into the stronghold. Trades give 4–8 at a time. Aim for at least 12.
- **Obsidian**: the blind portal back to the overworld needs 10. Trades give it one at a time, so most of it comes from chests.
- **String**: 4 string make a wool and 3 wool make a bed, so each bed costs 12 string. Beds kill the dragon.
- **Fire resistance potions**: 2–3. Blazes deal mostly fire damage, so one potion makes the fortress safe.
- Wear a gold helmet rather than gold boots; soul speed boots can come from trades.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, finish a route and open your inventory before walking away.
2. Count pearls, obsidian, string and potions out loud. If one is short, wait on the last trades or loot another chest.
3. Make the check a habit in real runs too: leaving short costs far more than a few seconds of waiting.
