A few F3 key combinations and third-person view show information the normal screen hides. They are used in almost every split, so learn what each one does before you need it.

## How it works

| Keys | What it does | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F3+G | Draws chunk borders | Buried treasure, pie-chart scans, fortress finding |
| F3+B | Draws hitboxes around entities | Finding the village golem, magma ravine scouting, timing beds on the dragon |
| F5 | Cycles first and third person | Scouting at spawn, seeing above you in bastions, looking under water |
| F3+C | Copies your position and angle to the clipboard | Sending eye measurements to Ninjabrain Bot |
| Shift+F3 | Opens the pie chart | Structure and spawner scans |
| F3+F / Shift+F3+F | Raises or lowers render distance | Pie-chart scans, pearl hangs |

- Seeing under water or lava in third person is an allowed glitch, so F5 scouting is legal.
- StandardSettings can reset chunk borders, hitboxes and perspective each world; some runners start every world with hitboxes on.

## How to practice

1. In a practice world, toggle each combo until you can press it without looking.
2. At spawn, use F5 to scout for lava, trees and animals before moving.
