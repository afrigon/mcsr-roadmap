Soul sand is slightly shorter than a full block, so a pillar that alternates soul sand with another
full block climbs a little faster than a pillar of one block type.

## How it works

- You stand slightly lower on soul sand than on a full block. You can see your view drop when you
  step onto it.
- Alternate soul sand with a full block such as gravel or netherrack, swapping hotbar slots each time.
- Tap jump and tap place for every block instead of holding both. Calisthenicuber estimates this saves
  about a tick every other block. Tapping and hotkeying the alternating blocks is the fastest way to
  pillar he shows.

## How to practice

1. Put soul sand and another block on neighbouring hotbar slots.
2. Pillar 20 blocks alternating them, one tap of jump and one tap of place per block.
3. Once it is smooth, use it wherever you pillar in the nether.
