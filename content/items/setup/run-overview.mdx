A run goes overworld → nether → stronghold → End. In a Ranked match both players get the same
seed, filtered so every step is possible.

# Overworld

You spawn next to the seed's structure: a village, desert temple, shipwreck, ruined portal or
buried treasure. Loot it for iron (a bucket), food and wood, then build a nether portal, usually by
pouring water on lava or with obsidian.

# Nether

Ranked puts a bastion close to the nether entry and a fortress near the bastion. Find the bastion
first, by sight.

## Bastion

Mine its gold and trade it to piglins for ender pearls, obsidian, string and fire resistance. Loot
the chests on the way. Then find the fortress, usually by reading the pie chart.

## Fortress

Kill blazes until you have about 6–7 blaze rods. Craft them into blaze powder, and with pearls
into eyes of ender.

# Stronghold

Build a portal back to the overworld (the blind), throw eyes of ender and triangulate the stronghold
with Ninjabrain Bot. Travel there, dig down, find the portal room and fill the end portal.

# End

Kill the ender dragon. Beds explode in the End, so the fast kills blow up beds under the dragon: a
one cycle when it perches, or a zero cycle while it circles the towers before its first perch.
