# Every hotbar slot in reach

A run uses most of the hotbar constantly. With the default keys, slots 5 to 9 are too far from movement to press while moving. 
Bind all nine slots to keys around WASD, such as Q, E, R, F, C, V, Z and X.

# Avoid the scroll wheel

Scrolling steps through every slot in between, so it is slower than pressing a slot's key and easy
to overshoot under pressure. The scroll wheel cannot be rebound or turned off, so make every slot's
key reachable enough that you never need it.

# Careful with mouse buttons

Mouse buttons are a poor fit for most actions. Pressing a mouse button nudges your aim and restrict movement. 
Keep them for simple actions where aim doesn't matter, such as F3, toggling perspective, offhand, or wide, thin and tall macros.

# Considering ESDF layout

Moving one key to the right, onto ESDF, frees Q, W, A and Z for more binds, at the cost of
relearning your movement. It is worth it only if you run out of keys around WASD.
