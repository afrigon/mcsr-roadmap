Sometimes the temple entrance is buried and you have to mine sand and drop a long way in. Use the
sand you just mined to break the fall in two.

## How it works

- Drop in, and about halfway down place the sand against the wall and land on it.
- You take a little fall damage twice instead of all of it at once, which is less in total.

## How to practice

1. Practise placing a block mid-fall in a test world, from different heights.
