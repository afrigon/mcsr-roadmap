Eyes of ender and blaze powder are 2×2 recipes, so you can craft them in the inventory while a pearl
is in the air. The flight time then pays for crafts you would otherwise stop for.

## How it works

- Throw the pearl, open your inventory and craft blaze powder, then eyes, in the 2×2 grid.
- Close the inventory before you land.
- Don't craft when the landing is risky, such as a pearl hang near a ledge: you need your hands free
  to clutch with a block or boat.
- Recipes that need a crafting table (beds, anchors, TNT, bows) can't be made mid-pearl.

## How to practice

1. During blind travel in practice, craft one eye per pearl until it fits into the flight.
2. Watch the 7rowl clip below for a case where crafting mid-pearl was the wrong call.
