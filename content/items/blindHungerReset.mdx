A hunger reset is dying on purpose right after setting spawn, so you respawn with full hunger, health
and saturation instead of stopping to eat. The overworld side of the blind is the first place a bed
works, which makes it the natural spot.

## How it works

- Beds explode in the nether, so the blind's overworld side is the first chance to set spawn with
  one.
- Dig a 2×1 hole beside the bed. Die in it so your items stay in the hole, then respawn and pick
  them up.
- Ways to die: throw pearls into a corner, take fall damage, or suffocate in gravel.
- After respawning you have about 3 seconds of damage immunity; pearls thrown in it cost no health.
- It also restores health before the stronghold and the End.

## How to practice

1. In practice runs, reset at the blind whenever hunger is low and time how long it takes.
2. Watch that every item lands in the hole before respawning.
