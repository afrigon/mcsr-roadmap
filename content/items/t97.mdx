T-97 is the first tall tower most players learn. Back y100 and the front offset at y99 are the two
setups every guide agrees on.

## How it works

| Node | Standing | Height | Note |
| ---- | -------- | ------ | ---- |
| Back | −28 26 | 100 | Small chance the first bed breaks. |
| Front offset | 27 −28 | 99 | Some guides use 26 −27; both work. |

- Front y100 at 27 −27 also works.
- Tall towers leave less spare time than low ones, especially on buried spawns.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, set T-97 and alternate back y100 and front y99.
