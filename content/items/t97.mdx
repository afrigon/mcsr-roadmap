T-97 is the first tall tower most players learn. Which setup you build depends on how high you
stand: high enough above the tower and the bed goes straight over the node; lower, and the setup
moves sideways away from the crystal (an offset) so the bed doesn't break it.

## Setups

| Standing height | Node | Setup type | What it's for | Video |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| y104 | Front and back | Hybrid | The plain setup: bed over the node, five explosives without a bow. | [front](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQ7GxWUXvaY&t=1407s), [back](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQ7GxWUXvaY&t=3825s) |
| y100 | Front and back | Hybrid offset | The usual height. Small chance a bed breaks, depending on how fast the dragon is. | [front](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQ7GxWUXvaY&t=1445s), [back](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQ7GxWUXvaY&t=3864s) |
| y99 | Front | Modern offset, hybrid offset or hybrid multiblock | The safe alternative to y100: take it when you see a slow dragon. The front is 2+2-able. | [front](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQ7GxWUXvaY&t=1523s) |
| y86–y95 | Either | Universal side | Standing below the top of the tower; see Universal Side. | |

- Every front setup can be five-bedded without a bow. The back offset at y100 can't consistently:
  bring a bow, or plan for more explosives.
- The three y99 front types are built differently: the modern one is the multiblock build, the
  hybrid offset is the plain two-obsidian build. Pick one and stick to it; no source says one is
  better for a given kind of zero.
