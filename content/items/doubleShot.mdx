## How it works

- An arrow removes the dragon's horizontal speed while it keeps descending. Extra arrows correct the
  dragon onto the node and shed excess height.
- For y95 counter-clockwise, stand on 27 −28 front or −29 26 back.
- 28 −27 beats 28 −26 for 2+2 (kohout's correction).

## How to practice

1. Start at y95, where the dragon has the most room to be corrected.
2. Move to y99 once y95 is consistent.
