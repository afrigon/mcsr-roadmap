A 1-deep ravine can't be recognised from the top, so a serious 2×1 player needs this variant too. It
is the 2×1 with one extra mechanic where the second half of the portal changes.

## How it works

- The setup and first half are identical to the 2×1. Watch for stone under the first two blocks you
  take: that tells you the ravine is 1-deep.
- From the next magma on, mine the magma itself while holding right click, so you grab the lava
  under it, then place a door there.
- Mine the cobble and scoop the lava in the corner behind it, at an angle that takes the lava under the
  obsidian, not the one under the next block.
- Move the water as low as it goes, mine the block under it, then fill the gap with lava from the side.
- Take the corner lava, not the side one: the side lava is part of your portal.
- Break the door block, place the last lava, pick up the water and light.

## How to practice

1. Slow the guide down and copy it step by step. The angled scoop is the hardest part.
2. Drill 1-deep 2×1 setups in a portal practice map until you never take the wrong lava.
