The bastion is the first goal in the nether: its piglins trade the pearls, obsidian and string the
rest of the run needs. Before learning counter tricks, learn to recognise one on sight.

## How it works

- **What it looks like**: a large structure of dark blackstone and polished blackstone bricks,
  often with gold blocks and gilded blackstone visible. Bastions come in four types: Housing,
  Bridge, Treasure and Stables.
- **Where it can't be**: bastions never generate in basalt deltas, the grey, jagged biome. A bastion
  can still reach into one from a neighbouring biome.
- **Ranked guarantee**: the intended bastion is the closest one to 0,0 by at least 10 chunks, sits
  within 14 chunks of 0,0, and has open terrain toward it from the nether entry. Look around the
  open side first.
- **Look around**: on entry, turn a full circle. Third-person view (F5) shows over nearby terrain.

## How to practice

1. Enter the nether on practice seeds and turn a full circle before moving. Name each biome you see.
2. Note how far away a bastion was when you first spotted it, to learn its shape at a distance.
3. When you can't see one, move on to the E-ray rather than wandering.
