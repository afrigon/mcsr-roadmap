One mistimed bed doesn't end a zero. A late bed can be balanced by an early one on the next rotation,
or you can let a rotation pass to get the timing back.

## How it works

- Each hit starts a 10-tick invulnerability window, so beds must be spaced out. A late bed pushes
  the next window late.
- Bed the next rotation slightly early to correct a late bed, or slightly late to correct an early
  one.
- After an extremely late bed, wait a full rotation before the next one.

## How to practice

1. On front M-88 y95, deliberately explode bed 1 late, then correct on bed 2.
2. Repeat with an extremely late bed and practise skipping a rotation.
