Seven iron is the number to remember: it makes the three items every run needs. Each Ranked seed
type except Ruined Portal guarantees at least this much, or four iron plus three diamonds.

## How it works

| Item           | Iron |
| -------------- | ---- |
| Iron pickaxe   | 3    |
| Bucket         | 3    |
| Flint and steel | 1    |

- The pickaxe mines obsidian and gold blocks, the bucket carries lava and water for the portal, and
  the flint and steel lights the portal.
- With four iron and three diamonds, make a diamond pickaxe instead, then the bucket and flint and
  steel.
- Turn iron nuggets into ingots before you count: nine nuggets make one ingot.
- Fill the remaining tools (axe, shovel, sword) from stone or gold.

## How to practice

1. Loot structures in practice worlds and say the count out loud before crafting.
2. Craft the pickaxe, bucket and flint and steel in the same order every time.
