The highest top setups in regular use: front y104 on Tall Boy and back y104 on T-100. Very hard and
leaving little spare time, they are only worth it when the side setup would be slower.

## How it works

- **Front Tall Boy y104**: guides disagree on the block. 29 −31, 26 −28 (Couriway's guide), and
  pion's 29 −30 and 25 −26 all work.
- **Back T-100 y104**: stand on −28 25. Few runners are consistent here; most take y100 or a side
  setup instead.
- Back T-100 y104 has about 2.6 s of spare time on an open spawn for a top runner, and none on a
  buried one.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, drill one block for front Tall Boy y104 and stick with it.
