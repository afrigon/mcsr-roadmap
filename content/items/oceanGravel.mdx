Flint comes from gravel, and on ocean seeds the gravel is on the sea floor, often right next to the
ravine you build in. Mining it there saves a trip to shore.

## How it works

- Cold oceans have gravel all over the floor. Warm oceans only have it near the shore, so stock up
  before you leave the island.
- Place a door on the floor to get an air pocket, then mine gravel until flint drops.
- When you are done, mine the block the door stands on rather than the door itself, then swim over and
  collect.
- You can craft the flint and steel while swimming down to the ravine.
- A bed placed underwater also gives an air pocket, if you have one.
- In Ranked, flint drops are standardized between both players.

## How to practice

1. On ocean practice seeds, find gravel near each ravine before building the portal.
2. Get the door placement right on the first try, so you never run out of air.
