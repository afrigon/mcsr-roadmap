The starter staircase is the spiral staircase where the stronghold begins. Every other room grows
outward from it, so finding it orients the whole stronghold and makes backwards navigation possible.

## How it works

- The starter's center sits at block 4,4 of its chunk. The F3 "Chunk" line shows your block position
  inside the chunk, so you can check whether a spiral staircase is the starter.
- Ninjabrain Bot gives the stronghold's coordinates as that 4,4 block. Digging down within about block
  3,3 to 5,5 of the chunk lands you on the staircase (see Dig In at 4,4). The "Eye Spy" advancement
  confirms you're inside.
- The top of the starter spiral ends in a flat wall.
- If the starter leads in only one direction, count rooms from the first split, not from the starter.

## How to practice

1. In StrongholdTrainer, find the starter at the start of every attempt and check its F3 chunk
   position.
2. Enter at random spots and walk back to the starter until you can do it without guessing.
