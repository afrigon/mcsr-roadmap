Beyond the bare minimum, a full bastion inventory removes the reasons a good run dies later: running out of pearls on the way to the stronghold, too few beds for the dragon, or no blocks at the spawner.

## How it works

| Item | Amount | Used for |
| ---- | ------ | -------- |
| Ender pearls | 12–16+ | Nether travel and getting into the stronghold. |
| Obsidian | 20 | Two 10-obsidian portals: the blind portal and a second one if you need to travel again. |
| String | 48–60 | 4–5 beds at 12 string each. |
| Fire resistance | 2–3 | The fortress and any lava travel. |
| Soul sand | a stack | Blocks for the blaze spawner platform. |
| Gravel | some | Lifting trades out of the hole and a hunger reset. |

- In Ranked, 8 gold blocks (72 trades) always contain exactly 3 pearl trades, so 12–24 pearls. Fewer gold blocks can mean fewer pearls.
- Obsidian trades come one at a time; take the rest from rampart chests.
- Keep a fire resistance potion in a fixed hotbar slot when you leave, so you can drink it without looking.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, run full routes and check the table before leaving.
2. Note which item you are short on most often and change your route to fix it (more gold blocks, another chest).
