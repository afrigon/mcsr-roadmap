Every bastion is one of four types, and each type keeps its gold and chests in fixed places. Knowing the type the moment it comes into view tells you which route to run and where to enter.

## How it works

| Type | How to recognize it | Gold |
| ---- | ------------------- | ---- |
| Housing | Two ramparts; the right one is always a triple chest. Cuts a large cube out of the terrain and never generates buried. Rampart chest gap is 3 blocks. | A buried line behind the double-chest room, with backups. |
| Bridge | Two ramparts, separated and lower than the bridge itself. | The gold "chalice" in the middle holds all the gold you need. |
| Treasure | A lava moat around a center with a magma cube spawner and two towers. The only linear route. | In the tower walls and the bridge. |
| Stables | Very square footprint, three ramparts, two large side gaps. Rampart chest gap is 2 blocks. Terrain inside the structure only happens here (or on Bridge). | In the good gap and the triple ramparts. |

- Housing and Stables are the pair most often confused. Count the ramparts and look at the rightmost one: a single there rules out Housing.
- Rampart refers to the side towers holding the chests (see Rampart Chests).

## How to practice

1. Load the bastion practice map with the type set to random.
2. Spawn in and name the type before moving. Then check it.
3. Repeat from different spawn points until you can call it from the first glimpse, including from below.
