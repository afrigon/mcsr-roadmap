DTGB (Double Triple Go Boom) is Lucento's TNT version of double triple. It only works on the left
side and uses a boat.

## How it works

- Light TNT with a button: flint and steel aggros piglins.
- The showcase has no commentary; copy the block placements from it.

## How to practice

1. Learn left-side double triple first.
2. In the LlamaPag map, set left and middle triples, carry TNT and a boat, and copy the showcase.
