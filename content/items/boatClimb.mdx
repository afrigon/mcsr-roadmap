The same boat trick that gets you out of a pit can replace pillaring or stairs elsewhere in a bastion. Placing a boat, entering and leaving it can put you on a higher spot nearby.

## How it works

- When you leave a boat, the game puts you on a free spot next to it, which can be on top of a higher block. In one example a runner gained about four blocks of height getting out of a cage.
- Boats also help going down: sprint-punch the boat forward before entering it to boat down into buried Treasure without suffocating.
- Boats from the overworld are the only kind you can have; nether wood can't make boats.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, find ledges two to four blocks high and test where leaving a boat puts you.
2. Use it in a route only where it is clearly faster than placing blocks.
