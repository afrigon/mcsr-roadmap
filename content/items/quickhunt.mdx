Quickhunt is manhunt combined with Reek's quicksand route (first made for top-down) and the
crossroads hole. chloe built it, and Daniel_H212's v1.1 guide (August 2026) rates it easier, safer
and faster than Goose. It skips the hard toggle, stays nearly damageless, and needs 2 blackstone.

## How it works

- Place one block before looting the chests, so piglins can hardly shoot or hit you.
- Once up, place a block against stray piglins, let the piglins up, and build a pillar. You land on
  this pillar later to avoid fall damage, and it blocks crossbow piglins' line of sight while you
  pillar up.
- While pillaring, place two blocks that stop lava housing completely. Piglins can still come down,
  but neither they nor their trades can climb out.
- Place three blocks for the piglin path, one more than standard manhunt.
- Jam yourself into the corner: two placed blocks bring the gold to you. Go slowly; placing right
  after mining can elevate the drop away.
- Let the piglins down, drop onto your pillar, throw a gold or two aside, and dig the crossroads
  hole. Move only between the corners away from the hole so you don't pick up thrown gold.
- If a piglin blocks the crossroads hole, toss a gold and switch to the normal hole.

## How to practice

1. Learn the Ranked manhunt first.
2. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, follow the v1.1 guide at reduced speed.
3. Learn it with the normal hole, then switch to the crossroads hole.
