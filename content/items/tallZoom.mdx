Stretching the game window very tall makes the eye of ender span many pixels, so you can read its
position far more precisely than at normal resolution. This is the basis of one-eye measuring.

## How it works

- A window-resize macro, legal in Ranked, stretches the window up to 16384 pixels tall while you
  measure. One resize outside your monitor's bounds is allowed.
- A magnified overlay ("eye zoom") shows the eye and the crosshair at that resolution.
- Line the crosshair up with the eye, press F3+C, then press Ninjabrain Bot's pixel-adjustment
  hotkey once for each pixel the crosshair is still off. Each press corrects one pixel.
- Tell Ninjabrain Bot the resolution height you resize to, or the corrections are wrong.
- Setup tools: Toolscreen (Windows), Waywall (Linux), Jingle.

## How to practice

1. Set up the resize and overlay following the draconix video, then check the bot's resolution
   setting matches.
2. Measure eyes in a test world until counting pixels and pressing the hotkey is automatic.
3. Recalibrate your standard deviation afterwards; it drops a lot with this setup.
