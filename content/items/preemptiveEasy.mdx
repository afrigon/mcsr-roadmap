Preemptive navigation reads the profiler pie chart from the starter staircase to find the direction of
the portal room's silverfish spawner, then routes straight at it. It replaces guessing with a
direction before you take a step.

## How it works

- **Settings**: render distance 8, entity distance 50%, FOV 30, chunk borders off, entity culling off.
- **Pie chart**: open the profiler (Shift+F3) and select `root.gameRenderer.level.entities` with the
  number keys.
- **Reading**: turn slowly and add the left-column percentages of `blockentities` (orange) and
  `unspecified` (green). The direction with the biggest sum points at the spawner. A clean spawner
  reading is about 45–62%.
- Don't look at a block while reading: the block outline changes the numbers.
- Then route toward that direction with your navigation knowledge.
- It works from the staircase or from inside the second portal.

## How to practice

1. Set up the settings once in your practice instance and save them.
2. In a preemptive practice map, read the spike, then fly in spectator to check the direction.
3. Note which numbers come up most for you; they become your reference.
