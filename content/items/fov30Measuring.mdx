Lowering your field of view (FOV) to 30 while measuring makes the eye look bigger, so your crosshair
lands on it more precisely and Ninjabrain Bot's answer is more certain.

## How it works

- Before pressing F3+C, drop FOV to 30 (a key bound in your setup tool, or the video settings),
  line the crosshair up on the eye, then return to your normal FOV.
- Ninjabrain Bot assumes each measurement has a standard deviation (SD): how far off a typical
  measurement is, in degrees. With FOV 30 it is around 0.02–0.04.
- The SD must match how precise you really are. Set it too low and the bot reports 100% for wrong
  chunks; too high and it asks for more throws than needed.
- Ninjabrain Bot has a built-in calibrator: you measure a series of eyes and it computes your SD.

## How to practice

1. Bind an FOV 30 key so switching costs no time.
2. Run the calibrator with at least a few dozen throws and enter the result.
3. Recalibrate whenever you change measuring setup (resolution, zoom, sensitivity).
