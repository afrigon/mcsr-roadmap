When two spawners are near each other, both can produce waves at the same time, but only while you stay within 16 blocks of each. Doing your crafting and the blind portal from such a spot keeps both spawners working during that time.

## How it works

- Find a spot within 16 blocks of both spawners. Put the crafting table and the blind portal
  there.
- Every action you overlap (crafting, the portal build) then runs while both spawners make waves.
- Blazes knocked into a lit nether portal travel to the overworld and are lost. Runners break or
  avoid lighting a portal right next to a spawner for this reason.
- A second spawner is also a backup: if lava covers the first after a blaze bed, you can blaze bed
  the second straight away (keep a spare bed for it).

## How to practice

1. In a creative world, find two spawners less than 32 blocks apart.
2. Mark the spots within 16 blocks of both and pick one for the crafting table and portal.
3. Play a full spawner cycle from there and count the waves.
