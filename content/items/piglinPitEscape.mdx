Falling into the trading hole, or any pit full of angry piglins, can end a run in seconds. Getting back out fast with what is already in your inventory keeps it alive.

## How it works

- **Boat**: place a boat in the pit, get in, and get out again. Leaving a boat puts you on a free spot nearby, which can be higher than where you stood. Calisthenicuber climbs out of his Housing hole this way and calls boating "a really good way out".
- **Gravel pillar**: jump and place gravel or soul sand under yourself until you are out. You often carry both from trades.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, jump into a finished trading hole and climb out with a boat.
2. Repeat with a gravel pillar, and compare which is faster from your hotbar layout.
