Before bastion routes, runners got pearls and obsidian by trading gold with piglins wherever they
found them, often around the portal. It is slow next to a bastion route, which aggroes many piglins
into a trading hole while you mine and loot.

## How it works

- Wear a piece of gold armor so piglins stay neutral, then throw gold ingots at adult piglins. Each
  ingot is one barter.
- Piglins near a portal are few, so trades come in slowly. A bastion route pulls 11 to 23 piglins
  into one hole.
- Ranked standardizes barters: the obsidian chance is raised and every 72 barters (8 gold blocks)
  give at least 6 obsidian trades and exactly 3 pearl trades. Both players get the same outcomes in
  the same order.
