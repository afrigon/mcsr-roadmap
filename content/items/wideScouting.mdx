The wide macro stretches the game window into a very wide strip. With planar fog on, terrain at the
screen edges renders much farther away, so you can spot a bastion or fortress past the normal fog
line.

## How it works

- Planar fog is a Sodium option. At wide views, fog at the left and right edges starts farther away
  than in the center.
- The wide macro, from Waywall (Linux) or Toolscreen (Windows), resizes the window to a wide
  strip on a hotkey and back again. Ranked rules allow both the resize macro and planar fog.
- Point the screen edge at the area you want to see, not the center.
- Use it on entry to check for a visible bastion, and from the bastion to look for the fortress.

## How to practice

1. Bind the wide macro to a key you can press while moving.
2. On every practice nether, scout with the wide macro on entry, then compare with your E-ray
   direction.
3. While trading in the bastion, sweep the horizon for nether brick before reaching for the pie
   chart.
