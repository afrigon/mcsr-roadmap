The north–south one cycle is the standard setup: the dragon moves faster and more regularly, so bed
timing is easier and each bed does more damage once you are on the correct side.

## How it works

- Place one obsidian on the north or south side of the bedrock pillar, chosen by the direction the
  dragon perches from.
- Wait behind the pillars around z70 with hitboxes on (F3+B). Standing back lowers strafe chances.
- When the dragon starts landing, it snaps to face the fountain: that is your cue to run or pearl in.
- Stand on the side the head swings toward. The correct side lets beds do more damage.

## How to practice

1. In an End practice map, practice spotting the perch direction from the snap.
2. Build on the right side within a second of the snap, then finish with five beds.
