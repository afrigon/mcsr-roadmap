With one bed and an open spawner, raise the bed above the spawner so the blast clears the spawn box without breaking the spawner. It is the standard first blaze bed.

## How it works

- Place a hard block (nether brick) on top of the spawner, two soft blocks (soul sand, dirt) above
  it, then the bed on top.
- Step away and detonate the bed.
- **Put out all fire right away**: fire blocks blaze spawns.
- Mine two blocks out the back and one down each side, then build the soul sand platform.
- With 4 rods already after the first wave, ClearColdWater's simulations say to still blaze bed but
  only build out the back before moving on to overlaps.

## How to practice

1. In Mescht's Blaze Practice map, give yourself one bed, nether brick and soul sand.
2. Build, detonate, clean up fire, mine out and build the platform.
3. Repeat until the whole sequence fits before the first wave arrives.
