In a basic trading hole you have to stop and mine blocks to let the next wave of piglins in. An auto-funnel hole is shaped so late piglins path into it by themselves while you keep mining gold.

## How it works

- The hole sits where the piglins' path to you already ends, so they fall in without you opening anything.
- Piglins start trading the gold you mine while you are still mining, instead of waiting for a second wave.
- Auto-funnel holes are route-specific. Known ones: the BeefSalad hole on Stables (about 15 piglins trading before 1:30), its successor the Crispy hole, and Soog on the right side of Bridge.
- Learn each one from its route; the block placements differ per bastion.

## How to practice

1. Pick one route you already run with a normal hole, such as the Stables good gap.
2. Learn its auto-funnel version on the practice map and copy the exact block placements.
3. Count the piglins in the hole and the time trading starts, and compare with your old hole.
