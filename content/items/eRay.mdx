E-ray finds the bastion through walls from the F3 entity counter, so you can leave the portal
running the right way without seeing anything. It works on any nether where the bastion is within
render distance.

## How it works

- Open F3. The top-left line `E: a/b` shows two numbers: `b` counts every entity loaded within
  render distance, `a` only the ones inside your view.
- A bastion holds about 30 mobs of its own in one spot, so when your view crosses it, the left
  number jumps. That jump is the spike.
- Switch to the thin macro: the window becomes a narrow strip, so the left number counts only a thin
  slice in front of you and the spike points at an exact direction.
- Turn slowly through a full circle, note the angle of the largest spike, switch back and run that
  way.
- A fortress's blazes and withers can also spike, but more gently; a bastion spikes sharply.

## How to practice

1. On practice seeds, e-ray before moving, then check your direction with F5 or the wide macro.
2. Count your fake reads: each time you ran the wrong way, check whether you followed a gentle
   spike instead of the sharp one.
3. Turn slowly enough that a one-slice spike can't slip past between frames.
