Sprint-bridging is speed bridging while keeping your sprint, which k4yfour estimates is about 30%
faster. It is legal because sprinting while sneaking is an allowed glitch that happens by accident
all the time.

## How it works

- Speed bridging: walk backwards holding S and D at about 45° yaw, crouch at the block edge, place,
  release crouch right away, and crouch again before the next edge.
- Sprint-bridging keeps the sprint state through the crouches, so you move at sprint speed instead
  of walking speed.
- Re-crouching too early only costs speed; too late and you fall.

## How to practice

1. Learn plain speed bridging first, in a flat creative world.
2. Follow k4yfour's video to add the sprint, and bridge long straight lines.
3. Bridge over the void or lava only once you no longer fall on flat ground.
