When there is no natural ceiling, you can build your own head hitters by placing blocks above your
path, for example on trees or in the nether. It costs blocks and time, so it only pays off on a route
you have already committed to.

## How it works

- The effect is the same as natural head hitters: a ceiling cuts each sprint jump short, so you take
  off more often.
- Calisthenicuber makes them on trees and especially in the nether, and only when he knows where he
  is going and can get there faster that way. Don't build them while you are still looking around.

## How to practice

1. Learn natural head hitters first, so you know what the faster rhythm feels like.
2. On a nether practice seed, pick a known straight path to a bastion or fortress and try placing
   ceiling blocks along it as you run.
