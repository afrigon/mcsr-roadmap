A 1+3 zero kills the dragon in a single rotation: one explosion, then three more chained on the same
pass. It is a party trick; a 2+2 is the top practical target in real runs.

## How it works

- In N+M notation, N is the rotations used and M the extra explosives chained into them. 1+3 is one
  rotation with four explosives, each extra one skipping a cooldown.
- Each skip works like the bow +1: a bow shot starts the invulnerability timer, so explosions land
  just before and just after it ends.
- Both example clips use front M-88 at y95 and kill in about 23 s.
- Mescht's Zero Practice map prints the result as `Explosives: 1+3`.

## How to practice

1. Master the bow +1 and 2+2 kills first.
2. On the practice map, build front M-88 y95 and go for four explosives on one pass.
3. Check the kill summary to see how many chained hits counted.
