On a desert temple seed, the hard part is often not the temple but finding lava, flint and wood
around it. Ranked guarantees lava pools and a river nearby, so plan the route to them from spawn.

## How it works

- Scout in third person as you run to the temple. Note the lava pool, water, trees and animals, and
  any ruined portal that might hold a light.
- Decide your exit before you go in: "out the front, left to the trees".
- The river gives water and gravel for flint. Gravel sometimes turns up from the temple blast; then
  you can skip the river.
- If flint is far, a woodlight saves the trip.
- Wood is mostly nearby on Ranked desert temple seeds, but not always.

## How to practice

1. On desert temple practice seeds, say the full route aloud before reaching the temple.
2. Check afterwards whether a closer pool or river was in sight.
