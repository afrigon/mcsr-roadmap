Pick block (middle click) puts the block you are looking at into your hand, pulling it from your
inventory if needed. It refills or reselects a slot without opening the inventory.

## How it works

- Look at a block and middle-click. If you carry that block, it moves to your hotbar or is
  selected if it's already there.
- **Blaze beds**: "block, bed, pick block, block, bed" reselects the hard block between two beds.
- **Restocking blocks**: drop an emptied stack and pick block a full one from the inventory.
- **Portal fix-up**: keep a fire resistance potion in the obsidian slot while building the portal,
  drink it during the portal animation, then pick block the obsidian to restore the hotbar.

## How to practice

1. Bind pick block to a key you can reach while moving.
2. Rehearse the double blaze-bed sequence in a fortress practice map.
