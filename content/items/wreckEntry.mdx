Shipwrecks generate upright, on their side or upside down. The chests stay at the same end of the
ship, but the way in changes, and knowing it saves air and seconds.

## How it works

- In every orientation, the iron is at the stern and the food is at the bow.
- Upright: enter the stern from either side, or through a hole in the back. Swimming down to the side
  and looting through the gap works well.
- Sideways: there are no masts. Swim down through the top hole to reach the iron chest; the back hole
  works too. Loot the food chest through the trapdoors.
- Upside down: same chests, same ends. Ignore the chest you can see from outside: it only holds the
  map and paper.
- Read the orientation from the surface, so you dive straight to the right opening.

## How to practice

1. On shipwreck practice seeds, plan your entry for each orientation before diving.
