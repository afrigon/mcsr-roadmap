Lenience is the spare time a setup leaves you between landing on the tower and the moment you have to
be in position. Buried spawns and slow pearls eat into it, so the same setup can be easy on one seed
and impossible on another.

## How it works

Seconds of spare time for a top runner with a gold pickaxe (ClearColdWater's sheet), by spawn:

| Setup | Open | O56 | O58 |
| ----- | ---- | --- | --- |
| Front M-88 y95 | 11.05 | 7.20 | 4.75 |
| Front M-88 y99 | 5.85 | 2.00 | −0.45 |
| Front T-94 y99 | 8.20 | 4.35 | 1.90 |
| Front T-100 y104 | 4.95 | 1.10 | −1.35 |
| Front Tall Boy y94 side | 12.20 | 8.35 | 5.90 |
| Back M-91 y95 offset | 9.70 | 5.85 | 3.40 |
| Back T-97 y99 | 5.85 | 2.00 | −0.45 |
| Back Tall Boy y94 side | 11.30 | 7.45 | 5.00 |

- Each O level of a buried spawn costs about 1.1 s. That pushes you to lower heights, and to offset
  or side setups.
- Negative values are not necessarily impossible, but leave no room for mistakes.
- The values are for top-runner execution; subtract your own extra time.

## How to practice

1. Time your own build on two or three setups and compare with the sheet.
2. Use the difference to decide your go or no-go list per spawn.
