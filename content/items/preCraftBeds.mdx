Crafting beds on the fountain wastes seconds while the dragon is already coming down. Craft them
earlier, at a moment when you are waiting anyway.

## How it works

- Beds come from village beds or, more often, wool made from bastion string.
- Craft them before the End, during dead time such as waiting for blazes to spawn.
- If they aren't crafted yet, the wait for a perch is the last safe moment to do it.

## How to practice

1. In practice runs, make crafting beds part of your blaze routine so it happens without thinking.
