Ocean seeds, ravines and buried treasure all put you underwater. Placing a block that water can't fill
gives you a pocket of air to breathe from, so you can stay down long enough to loot and mine.

## How it works

- A door or a bed placed underwater pushes the water out of its space and leaves air you can breathe
  in. Doors placed inside the player are an allowed glitch under the speedrun rules.
- Shipwreck and buried treasure routes use a pressure plate (iron or gold) or a button for the same
  air pocket.
- Runners carry doors on ocean seeds and use one when mining gravel for flint in a magma ravine.

## How to practice

1. On a shipwreck or buried treasure practice seed, swim to the loot, place a door and breathe from
   it.
2. Practice placing the door at the spot you are about to mine, so you can breathe while you work.
