The MCSR Practice Map collects most of the community's drill maps into one world for Minecraft 1.16.1, with a hub to jump between sections. It is the place to repeat one skill many times without playing whole runs.

## How it works

- **Sections**:
  - Portal practice, customizable.
  - Zero and one cycle practice, including zero setup practice.
  - Overworld practice: shipwrecks (normal, sideways, upside down), desert temple, village, ruined portal and buried treasure, looting and crafting drills, inventory sorting, chest looting and zero prep.
  - Movement: pearls (wall, overhang, lava), bridging (normal, soul sand, soul speed), boats (jump, MLG, sprint-crouching).
  - Search crafting, bastion practice and customizable fortress spawner practice.
  - Queue games to play while waiting for a Ranked match.
- **Hub**: `/trigger repairhub` repairs the hub, and `/trigger` with a section name jumps straight to that section.
- **Install**: download the latest release zip, unzip it (make sure the map folder isn't nested inside another folder) and move it into your instance's `saves` folder. In Prism, you can also drag the zip into the instance's Worlds tab.
- Credits: Dibedy (overworld practice, hub, queue games), Mescht (portal, zero, search crafting and fortress practice) and LlamaPag (bastion practice).

## How to practice

1. Pick one weak split per session and repeat it until it's clean, rather than touring every section.
2. Use the movement section to drill bridging and pearls before relying on them in matches.
