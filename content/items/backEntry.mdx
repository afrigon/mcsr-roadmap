When you reach treasure from behind, you don't have to pillar all the way up the outside. Spiral
paths inside the bastion lead up to where the route starts.

## How it works

- Treasure's back always generates open, so you can usually reach it.
- Look for the squares of brick in the back wall. Dig in there and you reach an internal path that
  spirals up through the bastion.
- Follow it up and across to the spot where Safe Boomer starts. This saves the blocks and time of
  climbing the outside.
- If the back has lava on one side and terrain on the other, dig in wherever you can.
- Digging straight through the back of the big cube to its gold is a different, slower idea: you
  mine a lot and still have to climb back up to the piglins.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, turn terrain off and walk around the back of treasure until you can spot
   the brick squares from a distance.
2. Dig in from both sides and time the climb to the route start.
