A burning animal runs to the nearest water, puts itself out, and you lose the cooked drops. This kill
never lets it run while on fire.

## How it works

- Swing a fully charged axe hit at the animal, without critting.
- Wait for it to stop running. You can manage your inventory while it runs.
- Give it one tick of flint and steel. The fire finishes it before it can move toward water.

## How to practice

1. Practice on animals standing near water, where the plain fire kill fails.
2. Learn the attack cooldown bar: the hit has to be fully charged for the one fire tick to kill.
