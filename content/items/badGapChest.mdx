A bad gap has no gold, only a single chest that can hold obsidian. When trades come up short, a
fast trip to that chest can save you a second round of trading.

## How it works

- Use it when you are a few obsidian short and the piglins are already traded out.
- Right bad gap: from the middle staircase, dig through the wall and drop to the single chest.
- Left bad gap: follow the path the Hourglass route uses, then dig through the wall to the chest.
- The Hourglass route loots this chest on the way to the good gap, so it comes for free there.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, set one bad gap, finish a gap route, then time the trip to the chest from
   your hole on each side.
