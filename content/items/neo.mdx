A neo is a parkour jump around the end of a one-block-wide wall, from one side to the other. It has
no real use in Ranked routes; learn it for fun.

## How it works

- Jump toward the wall's end and steer around it in the air with air strafing (holding a sideways
  key while turning).
- Double and triple neos go around longer walls.
