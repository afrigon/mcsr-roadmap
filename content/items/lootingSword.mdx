A sword enchanted with Looting raises both the chance and the number of blaze rods per kill. With one, a fortress can be over after a handful of blazes: Mikeyboie got 9 rods from 3 kills in his demonstration.

## How it works

- Looting only counts when the looting sword lands the killing blow. Soften the blaze with other
  hits first:
  - axe crit, weak axe swing, then a sword crit (Mikeyboie);
  - or a full axe crit, then two crits with the golden looting sword (Calisthenicuber).
- Do not blaze bed the spawner: explosion kills get no Looting bonus, and the sword already makes
  the spawner fast enough.
- Looting swords come from chests: golden swords in ruined portals, enchanted diamond swords
  sometimes at the bottom of a treasure bastion, and bastion chests in general.
- Ranked never guarantees a Looting sword.

## How to practice

1. In a blaze practice map, give yourself a Looting golden sword and an axe.
2. Practice the soften-then-finish pattern until the sword always lands the last hit.
3. Check your enchantments every time you loot a sword in practice runs.
