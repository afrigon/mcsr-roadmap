Use the bow +1 on offset and side setups too. Only take it on the good rotation: on these setups the
bed position depends on the rotation, and the double only works when the bed would have done good
damage anyway.

## How it works

- The mechanic is the same as on normal setups: the arrow starts the 10-tick invulnerability timer,
  then bed 1 goes just before it expires and bed 2 just after.
- An arrow removes the dragon's horizontal speed while it keeps descending, so shooting a low dragon
  breaks the bed.
- On side setups the bow also cuts the explosive count: without it, side setups can need more than
  five.

## How to practice

1. On the practice map, take back M-91 y95 offset and front T-100 y90 side, and bow only on the
   rotation that gives good damage.
