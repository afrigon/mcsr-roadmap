The trading hole at the end of manhunt can be dug several ways. They differ in how many blocks you
mine, how long until the first trade, and whether lava flowing down the housing can ruin them. The
MCSR wiki compares them in a table.

## How it works

- The twice-elevated hole needs 15 blocks mined, against 19 for the standard hole, and starts
  trading sooner in the wiki's table.
- Lava stops piglins from pathing to you, but you still need a block above your head so they can't
  push each other in. The lava can also burn junk items.
- Throwing gold to the piglins is awkward. Throw into the corner you tower from.
- The wiki recommends it for top-down, where lava is more practical and you usually arrive with
  extra gold.
- Not every hole is lava-safe. Check the wiki's lavaproof column before relying on one.
- The crossroads hole is a faster trading hole used by quickhunt.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, finish manhunt with a different hole each run.
2. Time from the last block mined to the first trade.
3. Keep the fastest hole you can do every time.
