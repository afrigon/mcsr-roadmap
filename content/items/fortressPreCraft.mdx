Between blaze waves you stand around anyway. Crafting the End fight's explosives and bow there means you leave the fortress with everything ready, and the blind portal is the only thing left to do.

## How it works

- Between waves, craft in the 2×2 inventory grid: wool, sticks for the bow, and glowstone blocks
  for respawn anchors. Clear junk from your inventory at the same time.
- Then open a crafting table and search-craft: with the recipe book open, T focuses the search box.
  Calisthenicuber types "b" to find the bow and "re", which matches both red bed and respawn anchor.
- Build the blind portal last, across from the crafting table.
- Craft beds here only when trades are already done. If you are still waiting on piglins, crafting
  them in the bastion is free. ClearColdWater measured about 1.7 s saved by crafting at the spawner
  instead of after trades; some guides still craft during trades.

## How to practice

1. In a blaze practice map, keep the materials for 2 beds, 2 anchors and a bow in your inventory.
2. Kill blazes and finish one craft in each gap between waves.
3. Leave with all crafts done and no extra time spent after the last rod.
