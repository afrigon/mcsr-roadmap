The Half Portal is the fastest ruined portal to complete: the frame is nearly whole, just sunk in a
lava pool, and missing a single obsidian.

## How it works

- Recognise it by half the frame being under lava and two slabs on top.
- It has two gold blocks, both exposed, one chest and plenty of lava.
- Only one obsidian is missing. Cast it with your water and the pool's lava.
- The completion in the reference video takes about 3.6 seconds without crying obsidian and about 16
  seconds with it.

## How to practice

1. Copy the completion from the video until you finish in one motion.
2. Practise the crying-obsidian version separately; it is a different route.
