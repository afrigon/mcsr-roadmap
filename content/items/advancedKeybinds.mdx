Some of the most pressed keys in a run, F3 and F5, sit far from your hand by default, and lowering render distance means pressing Shift, which also crouches. Moving these to mouse buttons or spare keys saves time every time you scan.

## How it works

- **F3 on a mouse side button**: Minecraft hardcodes F3, so you rebind it in Toolscreen (Windows) or Waywall (Linux), not in the game's controls. A side button lets you hold F3 while you walk and strafe.
- **Toggle perspective on a side button**: useful for looking above you in bastions and scouting at spawn.
- **Render distance without crouching**: Shift+F3+F lowers render distance, but left Shift crouches. Rebind right Shift to Caps Lock, Alt or another easy key, so you can lower render distance (and open the pie chart with Shift+F3) while still walking.
- **Toggle sprint**: set Sprint to Toggle in Controls. Toggle sprint is one of the two macros the rules allow.
- **Keep it legal**: when you move a key with a rebind tool, also bind the original key to the old one, so each input still has exactly one key.

## How to practice

1. Add one rebind at a time and use it for a session before adding the next.
2. Practice lowering render distance and reading the pie chart while strafing.
