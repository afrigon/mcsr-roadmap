Ruined portal seeds guarantee only a little iron. Often you can't make both a bucket and an iron
pickaxe, so you choose one and replace the other with gold.

## How it works

- Ranked guarantees at least 18 iron nuggets beyond what the bucket or flint and steel needs. An iron
  pickaxe isn't guaranteed.
- Turn all nuggets into ingots before you decide what to craft.
- If the portal's own obsidian completes it, you don't need the bucket: make the iron pickaxe.
- If you need the bucket, go into the nether without an iron pickaxe. Use gold or stone tools, and a
  gold pickaxe for netherrack and blackstone.
- Gold tools mine fast but break quickly. Use them for the big jobs.

## How to practice

1. On ruined portal practice seeds, decide "pickaxe or bucket" right after looting, then check it was
   right.
