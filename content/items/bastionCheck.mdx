Before spinning for a spike, check whether a bastion is loaded at all. This one glance tells you
whether the E-ray will find anything or whether you need to move first.

## How it works

- F3 shows three numbers to compare: the right E number (all loaded entities), M (normal hostile
  mobs such as piglins, hoglins, ghasts and skeletons) and C (creatures, which in the nether are
  striders).
- Normally M plus C equals the right E number.
- The piglins generated with a bastion are counted in E but not in M. So if E is about 30
  higher than M plus C, a bastion is loaded.
- About 60 higher usually means two bastions.
- If there is no difference, no bastion is within render distance: move, raising render distance if
  it's low, and check again.

## How to practice

1. On every practice nether, add M and C before anything else and say out loud whether a bastion is
   loaded.
2. Confirm with the E-ray spin each time until the check feels reliable.
