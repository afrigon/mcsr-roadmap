Instead of building as soon as the rating is good, keep following the bot's "head X°" hint while you
travel until it reads excellent. A better blind spot means a shorter trip in the overworld.

## How it works

- After F3+C with no eye entered, Ninjabrain Bot says which angle to head for better coordinates.
- Travel along that angle, pressing F3+C again as you go, and build once the rating is excellent.
- Build at nether Y48 or higher. In Ranked this guarantees the overworld side comes out on the
  surface, never underground.
- Don't over-travel: nether terrain and lava can cost more time than the better rating saves.

## How to practice

1. In every practice run, follow "head X°" to excellent and note how far it took.
2. Check your Y before placing the first obsidian.
