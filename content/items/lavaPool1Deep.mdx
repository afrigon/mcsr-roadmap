Some lava pools are only one block deep. Under the spot where your bottom obsidian should be there is
stone instead of lava, so the normal cast leaves a gap you have to fill by hand.

## How it works

- Build the portal exactly as for a 2-deep pool, up to the point where the sides and top are cast.
- Pick up the water and place it one layer lower, on the level of the missing bottom blocks.
- Mine out the stone under the frame and pour lava into each gap. The water turns it into obsidian.
- The speed trick from draining also works here: leave one wall unfinished, move the water down, fill
  the bottom, then close the wall.
- If you are too slow and the water drains away, put the water back where it was and it fills in
  again.

## How to practice

1. In a portal practice map, pick 1-deep pools and spot the stone under the first obsidian before
   you commit.
2. Practice moving the water down one layer without losing the flow.
