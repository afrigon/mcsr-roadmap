Without flint and steel, for example on woodlight seeds or ruined portal seeds where the light is a
fire charge, a lava bucket kills animals and cooks the drops. Done carelessly it milks cows and
sets you on fire.

## How it works

- Clear blocks around you first, so the lava can't flow onto you.
- Place lava inside the animal's hitbox (F3+B shows hitboxes).
- Pick the lava back up after a short time. It depends on the animal and is learned by feel.
- The animal keeps burning and dies on dry ground. Picking the lava up matters: drops that land in lava
  are destroyed, and you need the lava for the portal.
- On cows, aim at the lava outside the cow's hitbox when you pick it up. Aiming at the cow milks it
  instead: you lose the food and the lava can flow onto you.
- Pigs and sheep can't be milked, so only the timing matters.
- If the animal survives, punch it.

## How to practice

1. Kill several animals in a row with the same bucket to learn the timings.
2. Practice cows with hitboxes on until you never milk one.
