Going through a nether portal makes you wait about 4 seconds. With god sensitivity, getting in and
out of the boat for boat eye can happen inside that wait instead of after it, so you can throw as
soon as you arrive.

## How it works

- God sensitivity keeps the boat angle valid through the angle wrap a portal causes, so a boat
  entry made before arriving still counts.
- That turns the boat step into an overlap with the portal wait rather than a separate action on
  the overworld side.
- The exact block placement varies by player; watch top runners' blinds and copy what they do.

## How to practice

1. In a practice world with god sensitivity set up, build a portal and try the overlap until the
   boat icon is green on arrival every time.
2. If it isn't green, fall back to a normal boat entry before throwing.
