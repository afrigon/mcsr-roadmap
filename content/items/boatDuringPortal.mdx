Boat eye measuring needs your angle aligned by getting into a boat and back out before you throw.
Most players do that once they arrive in the overworld. You can do it during the portal animation
instead, while you would be waiting anyway, and throw the moment you arrive.

## The overlap

- Place the boat inside or beside the nether portal before you step in.
- While the portal animation plays, get into the boat and back out.
- Arrive with the alignment already done and throw your eye right away.
- God sensitivity keeps the boat angle valid through the angle wrap the portal causes, which is what
  lets the alignment survive the trip.

## Getting it consistent

In a practice world with god sensitivity set up, build a portal and run the overlap until the boat
icon is green on arrival every time. If it isn't, fall back to a normal boat entry before throwing.
