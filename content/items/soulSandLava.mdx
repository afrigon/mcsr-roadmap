Soul sand is slightly lower than a full block, so standing on it puts your feet below the top of the
blocks around you. Next to lava at the same level, part of your hitbox sits inside the lava block
and you catch fire, even though you never stepped into it.

## Where it bites

- Soul sand valleys and the soul sand around fortress spawners, where lava pools sit at the same
  height as the floor.
- Walking along a lava edge on soul sand: you burn where the same walk on netherrack would be safe.

## Avoiding it

Stay one block away from lava while on soul sand, or put a block between you and the lava. With
fire resistance on, it doesn't matter.
