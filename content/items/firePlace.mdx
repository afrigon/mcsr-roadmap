When a zero goes wrong you can end up with no block to place the next explosive against: the bed's
spot is gone and there's only air. If the previous explosive left fire behind, aim at the fire.

## The move

- A bed or anchor explosion often leaves fire on the blocks around it.
- Fire has a hitbox you can target. Aim at it and place the next bed or anchor there, as if it were a
  block.
- It's the easy version of Air Place: you get a target to aim at, but it only works when the last
  explosion left a fire.
