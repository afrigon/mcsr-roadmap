The C-Dot is a ruined portal with a C of lava on the floor and a single obsidian "dot". A pillar of
iron bars makes it easy to recognise, and its own pools hold everything you need to complete it.

## How it works

- Recognise it by the C of lava, the lone obsidian block and a pillar of iron bars, which no other
  type has.
- It has 2 gold: one about 7 blocks from the base, one in the corner lava below it. The chest is
  beside the small structure.
- Complete it with water and lava from its own pools. The reference completion takes about 7 seconds
  without crying obsidian; with crying obsidian the recast takes longer.

## How to practice

1. Copy the reference completion step by step in a practice map.
