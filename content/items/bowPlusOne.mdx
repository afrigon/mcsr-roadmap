Shoot the dragon with an arrow before the first bed, so two beds can land on the same rotation. This
is the "+1" in damage notation: one skipped damage cooldown.

## How it works

- The arrow starts the dragon's 10-tick invulnerability timer, and later hits don't reset it.
  Explode bed 1 just before the timer expires and bed 2 just after: two near-full hits in one
  rotation.
- An arrow also removes the dragon's horizontal speed while it keeps descending. Shooting a dragon
  that is already low makes it drop onto the bed and break it.
- Only bow when the bed would have done good damage anyway.

## How to practice

1. Start on F m88 y95, which you already know from bed timing.
2. Shoot, then do the double on that rotation.
3. Watch both mistake clips below until you can tell a late shot from an early one by eye.
