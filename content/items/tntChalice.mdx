Blowing up the chalice with TNT is much faster than mining it, and the fuse time is not wasted: you
craft while it burns.

## How it works

- Place the TNT on the chalice and light it with a button. Flint and steel makes the explosion count
  as your damage, which aggros piglins.
- Overlap the fuse with crafting: a gold helmet, a gold pickaxe, or clearing your inventory. If you
  place your crafting table there, the blast removes it for you.
- Cancel the explosion's knockback by setting yourself on fire just before it goes off and walking
  toward the TNT. Put the fire out quickly. The same trick cancels hoglin knockback.
- crossroads198x's notes: wait about half a second before placing the TNT, or the first gold block
  can fly away. You usually get 11 blocks, sometimes 12, rarely 13. About a third of the time the
  drops land out of reach and you must block up. Have gold ready, because a few piglins usually
  aggro right away.
- When enclosed or in a cave, put the TNT in the middle of the chalice instead; it loses gold
  blocks slightly more often.

## How to practice

1. In LlamaPag's bastion practice map, spawn at the chalice with TNT.
2. Repeat the fire cancel until you take no knockback.
3. Count gold blocks recovered per attempt.
