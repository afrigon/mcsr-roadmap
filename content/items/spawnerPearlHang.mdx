Treasure has a magma cube spawner, and its spawner slice hides the fortress on the pie chart, so
pie-ray (finding the fortress from the debug profiler's spawner reading) stops working. A pearl hang
lets you go down, break the spawner and come back up in one move.

## How it works

- A pearl hang: throw an ender pearl, then drop render distance to 2. Once the pearl is in unloaded
  chunks it freezes. Raise render distance later and the pearl lands.
- Throw the pearl back up toward the trading tower, holding S, and lower render distance. Crossing a
  chunk border unloads the pearl.
- Drink fire resistance and boat down to the bottom of treasure. Break the block by the spawner and
  mine it from the side.
- Raise render distance to return to your trades. With the spawner gone, pie-ray works again.
- While you are down there, you can loot the bottom chest or mine the bottom gold. If piglins drop
  on you, raise render distance to escape.
- Before doing this, scan for the fortress from the roof with a wide window and check your
  coordinates. The hang is for seeds where you still don't know the direction.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, finish a treasure route, then do only the hang: throw, lower, boat down,
   break the spawner, raise.
2. Practice until you lower render distance before the pearl lands every time.
