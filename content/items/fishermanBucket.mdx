Some villages give you no bucket: no iron to spare and no bucket in the chests. A fisherman sells a
bucket of cod, and placing the fish leaves you with an empty bucket for water and lava.

## The trade

- Find the fisherman: a villager with the fisherman's hat, whose workstation is a barrel.
- His novice trades include a bucket of cod for emeralds.
- Place the cod somewhere (it's a bucket of water with a fish), then pick the water back up or use
  the empty bucket as usual.
