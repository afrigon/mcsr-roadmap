The LeBron portal is the advanced 2×1: you place the lava as obsidian before putting the block back,
so you never need the door on that side. It is the fastest magma portal, sub-5-second builds are
possible, and it is the only one that works when your building blocks are leaves.

## How it works

- The setup is the same as the 2×1.
- Mine the magma and scoop the lava under it. Then place the lava as obsidian first, and only then place
  the block back.
- Because of how water flows, you can now mine the next block without a door there, and no water gets
  in.
- Doors can't be placed on leaves, which is why this order is the only option with leaves as blocks.
- Stay on the pickaxe hotkey and right-click the lava before switching to blocks. Switching early is
  the common mistake.
- The rest of the portal is the 2×1. On a 1-deep ravine, add the 1-deep fix.

## How to practice

1. Learn the plain 2×1 first, then switch the order of that one step.
2. Practice with leaves as your only blocks, so you can't fall back on the door.
