Triple to gap (TTG) means looting a triple rampart, mining its four gold blocks, then leading the
piglins down into the good gap. This page covers the forgiving versions: middle TTG and the Suigi
route. The faster side variants are on the Side page.

## How it works

- Every TTG starts the same: tower up, loot the triple's chests, mine the four gold blocks under
  it. Keep gilded blackstone or boots as distraction items that don't cost trades.
- **Middle TTG**: from the four gold, throw a distraction gold, mine down two, and throw before the
  middle-rampart piglins reach you. Block the gaps where piglins get stuck. Use the placed blocks
  as a checkpoint that holds the piglins above you while you check the gap wall.
- Because Ranked guarantees a good gap, you don't need to know which side is good before you
  start: if this side is bad, take the train to the other.
- **Suigi** (k4yfour's route, improved by maexxez): one forced route for every stables, with a
  version for each triple and gap combination. Nearly every block placed or broken is needed for
  piglins to path to the middle triple. Safe, beginner friendly, and slower than the side variants.
- Throw distraction gold at every choke point: piglins take about a second to notice it.

## How to practice

1. In the LlamaPag map, set a middle triple and run middle TTG to the classic gap blocks.
2. Learn Suigi for each combination in maexxez's chapters.
