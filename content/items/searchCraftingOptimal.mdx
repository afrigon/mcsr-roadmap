Some game languages give every speedrun item a unique one- or two-letter search prefix. Top runners
switch to one, usually Danish or Norwegian, to search-craft with fewer keypresses than English.

## How it works

- In the HiTrippy guide, English needs 15 different characters for all junkless queries, while
  Norwegian needs 14 and never more than two letters per item.
- Set the language in the game options and in StandardSettings, so every world loads in it.
- Letters your keyboard lacks (ø and å for Norwegian) can be added with a split rebind in
  Toolscreen: it types the character in the search bar without changing the key's normal input.
- Item names, subtitles and enchantments also change language, so expect a learning period.
- Rebinding a key to Home lets Shift plus that key select the whole query, so the next one
  overwrites it instead of needing backspaces.

## How to practice

1. Pick the language, then find the queries for your common crafts on the optimizer.
2. Learn one split at a time: fortress crafts, then bastion crafts, then the 2×2 grid.
