One scan from the portal gives a direction, but a small error grows over a long run. Rescanning on
the way corrects it, and a close bastion fills more of the view, so the second reading is easier.

## How it works

- Scan again partway to the bastion, without stopping to spin: sweep the area ahead.
- The closer the bastion, the wider its spike. A spike that appears as soon as the bastion enters the
  edge of the screen tells you which side it lies on, so you can turn toward that edge instead of
  sweeping the full view.
- Spawning low in the nether (around y35–40), aim slightly above the horizon to read the spike; high
  up (y70 or more), aim slightly down.
- A near-instant jump in the right E number while chunks load often means a Bridge bastion facing
  toward or away from you.

## How to practice

1. On practice seeds, rescan halfway to the bastion every time, even when you think you're right.
2. Note how often the rescan changes your heading, and by how much.
3. Practice catching the spike at the screen edge while running, without a full stop.
