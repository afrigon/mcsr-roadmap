## How it works

- Craft beds and anchors before the End, overlapped with blazes or other dead time.
- Don't rebuild the hotbar just for the zero; swap only what's needed, so muscle memory holds.
- Glowstone or crying obsidian goes in the obsidian slot. Keep the bed in your offhand for doubles.
- Save the same layout as a loadout on the practice map.

## How to practice

1. Pick one layout and use it in every run and every practice session.
