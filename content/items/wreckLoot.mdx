A shipwreck is a small village on the sea floor: an iron chest at the back, a food chest at the
front. Ranked shipwreck seeds always give enough iron and some food, so the wreck is the first stop.

## How it works

- The iron chest is at the stern (the back). It holds iron, gold and sometimes diamonds.
- The food chest is at the bow (the front), under trapdoors. Flip the trapdoors open and loot through
  them.
- The third chest has a treasure map. Skip it in Ranked: your iron always comes from the stern.
- Swim on the surface for as long as you can to keep your air, then dive.
- Take rotten flesh, carrots (for golden carrots in the bastion) and leather armor, unless it has Thorns
  or Curse of Binding. Potatoes are rarely worth taking.
- Swim back to the island for wood, and craft by your iron count.
- In Ranked the wreck is within 4 chunks of spawn.

## How to practice

1. Use an overworld practice map's loot generator to drill fast looting and crafting by iron count.
2. On shipwreck practice seeds, name the wreck's orientation from the surface before diving.
