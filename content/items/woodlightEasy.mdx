A woodlight lights the portal with fire instead of flint and steel. Lava next to wood sets the wood
on fire, and the fire spreads into the frame. Use it when there is no gravel nearby, or when you would
rather spend the flint-and-steel iron on something else.

## How it works

- Build the lava pool portal as usual, but make the front out of wood where the obsidian would
  normally be.
- Fill the back and the blocks around the frame with wood, shaped like a bowl around the inside.
- Place lava next to the wood on the sides.
- Switch the difficulty to Hard while you wait: fire spreads faster there.
- The wait is random and can take a minute or two. Use it to clear your inventory.
- As soon as the portal lights, switch back off Hard, so you don't enter the nether on it.
- Scoop a lava bucket before going in, without setting yourself on fire.

## How to practice

1. Build the bowl setup in a test world a few times and time how long it takes to light.
2. Make the Hard toggle and switching back part of the routine.
