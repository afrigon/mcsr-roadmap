Animals give the cooked food you need for the nether. Set them on fire with flint and steel and
finish them with an axe, and the drops come out already cooked.

## How it works

- Light the animal with flint and steel, then hit it with the axe until it dies. Burning animals drop
  cooked meat.
- If an animal steps out of the fire before it catches, hit it back into the flames.
- Pigs first, then cows, then sheep.
- A burning animal runs for water. Near a lake, use the one-hit method or the water hole instead.
- Keep the flint and steel in your offhand to swap between lighting and hitting quickly.
- For a beginner, 7–10 pieces of good food (pork or steak) is a safe amount.

## How to practice

1. Kill every animal near your route on practice seeds until the light-and-hit rhythm is automatic.
2. Count your food before entering, so you know whether you need more.
