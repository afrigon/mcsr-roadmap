On a 1/8 node the dragon comes in straight, between the fountain and a middle pillar, instead of on a
diagonal. Learn the two low heights, y88 and y92, front and back.

## How it works

- Front or back is fixed per seed; diagonal or straight (1/8) is not. Read it
  from the dragon's approach during the first pearl.
- Standing blocks: front 23 0, back −23 0. Some guides write −24 0 for back, which is probably the
  same block approached from a different side.
- Straight heights are y84, 88, 92/93, 97 and 102. An easy way to remember them is one less (87,
  91, 96): bridge at that height, then staircase up one.
- Back 1/8 is precise above y92.

## How to practice

1. Practice 1/8 nodes on the practice map, alternating front and back.
2. Practice reading the node from the approach until diagonal versus straight is instant.
