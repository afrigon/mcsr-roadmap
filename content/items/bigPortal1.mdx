Big Portal 1 is a large ruined portal with an obsidian L along its top. It can't be completed, so
recognise it quickly and move on to plan B.

## How it works

- Recognise it by its size and the L of obsidian at the top.
- It has 2 gold and 1 chest.
- It can't be completed. Mine its obsidian for your own frame, or build a lava portal nearby.

## How to practice

1. Learn to tell it apart from Big Portal 2 at a glance.
