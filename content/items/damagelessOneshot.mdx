A perch kill with no explosives at all: melee the dragon, then fire a weak arrow while moving fast.
Arrow damage scales with velocity, so one shot (sometimes a few) kills. It saves a run after a
failed zero or one cycle, and on low-explosive seeds it frees string and glowstone for other uses.
Oneshot is an allowed glitch.

## How it works

**You need** a bow, one arrow, an axe or sword, blocks to reach the top of the fountain, a crosshair
overlay image with a toggle hotkey, and OneShotLagFix turned on in Ranked settings → Performance.

For a dragon facing along X:

1. While waiting at the one-cycle spot, put the bow in your offhand and enable the overlay.
2. Run to the side the head turns to and land 5 full axe hits or 8 sword hits.
3. Pillar 2 blocks on the bedrock opposite where the dragon flew in and jump onto the fountain.
4. Move to the dragon's centre (the L shape around two lighter pixels).
5. Look straight forward and down, switch to third person, and stop all mouse movement. Any
   movement means 0 damage, so lift the mouse or use a DPI toggle.
6. Land one more full hit.
7. Line up from the tail direction: tail down-right means ½ pixel down and ¼ pixel right, mirrored
   for other directions.
8. Draw the bow while holding shift and nudge with the movement keys. Releasing deletes the arrow;
   with one arrow, switch hotbar slot instead of firing.
9. Final shot: jump, press left and right click together, and release the weak arrow at the top of
   the jump. Repeat until it dies.

A 45° dragon takes a ½ pixel second adjustment; for a dragon facing along Z, aim ½ pixel toward the
head. Avoid fall damage for 3 s after the kill; if launched, land with a boat or bucket, not a pearl.

## How to practice

1. Practice the final shot alone first (the tutorial's practice section shows how).
2. Then run the full sequence in the zero practice map's one-cycle mode. thecamo6 estimates about
   15 minutes to learn it.
