# MCSR Ranked roadmap

Each item is a learnable skill, placed in the time-goal column it unlocks: learning everything up to a
column should make that time reachable. Easy and optimal versions of a technique are separate items.
Research and sources live in [research/](research/).

Markers: `[R]` Ranked-specific (seed filter, RNG standardization or Elo-gated seed pool); `⚠` placement or
definition not confirmed by a primary source.

## Time goals

The columns are the in-game "Break the Barrier" achievement levels.

| Goal | Rough Ranked equivalent (median winning time) |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------- |
| First completion | — |
| Sub 30 | Coal (29:35) |
| Sub 20 | Iron → Gold (24:27 → 17:23) |
| Sub 15 | Emerald (14:00) |
| Sub 13 | High Emerald |
| Sub 12 | Diamond (11:21) |
| Sub 11 | Mid Diamond |
| Sub 10 | Netherite (9:27) |
| Sub 9 | Mid Netherite |
| Sub 8 | Top Netherite |
| Sub 7 | Top ~10 players |
| Sub 6 | Record pace (Ranked record 5:45 in mid-2026) |

Seed types unlock by Elo [R]: Ruined Portal at 600 (Iron), snowy and taiga villages at 900 (Gold),
Buried Treasure at 1200 (Emerald).

## Rows

- **Fundamentals**: Setup & controls · Crafting & inventory · Movement & recovery
- **Overworld**: General · Village · Desert temple · Shipwreck · Ruined portal · Buried treasure
- **Nether**: General · Bastion general · Housing · Bridge · Treasure · Stables · Fortress
- **End**: Finding the stronghold · Stronghold · End fight · Zero cycle
